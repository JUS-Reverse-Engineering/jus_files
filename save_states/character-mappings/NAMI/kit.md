### Nami — FAQ 46921

**Source:** `46921-nami.txt`; author: puTTytHiEF09.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Neutral B:** Nami swings the Clima-Tact a little bit forward; used for basic poking, chains smoothly into other attacks.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6; Laughter: 9.

- **side B — Thunder Tempo:** Nami shoots a boxing glove attached to a spring forward; decent pushback, chains into neutral B, but has a long recovery unless chained into neutral Y quickly.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6; Laughter: 9; Properties: projectile.

- **up B — Rain Tempo:** Nami places a rod of the Clima-Tact on her left hand, head, and right hand respectively; no longer multi-hits (lost from Jump! SuperStars), but hits both sides of Nami.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6; Laughter: 9.

- **down B — Cloudy Tempo:** Nami forms the Clima-Tact like a rifle and fires a bouquet of flowers forward. Forcibly switches the opponent's character, like all down B attacks. Somewhat crappy range and hard to combo into, but comes out quick.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6; Laughter: 9; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B:** Nami does a downward strike with the Clima-Tact, which knocks an opponent down through platforms. Quick start-up and recovery.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6; Laughter: 9; Properties: knockdown.

- **Y — Neutral Y:** Nami spins the Clima-Tact around in front of her. Basic version only does 3 hits, but pressing X along with the attack extends the number of hits up to 15.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 1 damage per hit, 3-15 hits, max damage of 15 in total; Laughter: 1-2 damage per hit, 3-15 hits, max damage of 22 in total; Hits: 3-15; Properties: multi-hit, mash-extend.

- **side Y — Cyclone Tempo:** Nami throws a small rod of the Clima-Tact at her opponent like a frisbee; can hit up to 4 times (twice going out, twice returning) and can hit multiple opponents in a line.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 6 per hit, 4-hits max, 24 damage total; Laughter: 9 per hit, 4-hits max, 36 damage total; Hits: 4; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **up Y — Fine Tempo:** Nami forms a triangle with her Clima-Tact and causes three doves to fly out (upper-left, straight up, upper-right). Slow start-up and recovery, but good projectile against higher platforms since it fans out.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 5 per bird, 15 if all three hit at the same time; Laughter: 7 per bird, 22 if all three hit at the same time; Hits: 3; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Nami gets on a waver and dashes forward with it. Breaks guards like all down Y attacks; can hit from about half a screen away and moves fast.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 12; Laughter: 18; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Thunder Ball:** Nami sends out a ball of electricity slowly forward for about half the screen, pushing the opponent back with every hit; two can be sent out at once. Nami also rises upward and moves back a bit when used.
  - Damage: (deals up to 4 hits) Power/Knowledge: 2 damage per hit, 8 damage in total; Laughter: 3 damage per hit, 12 damage per hit; Hits: 4; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Nami taunts her opponent and points at them; anyone close enough who is not blocking loses some special bar and takes 1 point of damage.
  - Damage: 1.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide covers two Nami variants sharing this single file: base 'Clima-Tact Nami' (chr_b 15, this file's focus) and 'Perfect Clima-Tact Nami' (PCT Nami, chr_b 19). The PCT variant has its own basic-attack set (different side B 'Swing Arm', up B 'Thunder Charge', up Y 'Thunderbolt Tempo', down Y tornado dash, and reduced hits on side Y) and its own combo list (B->Up B->Up Y; B->(Up B)->Y; B->Forward B->Forward Y->Gokudera 3-koma/Kuroro 3-koma->X; B->Y->Gokudera 3-koma/Kuroro 3-koma->X; Shun 3-koma->Up X). None of that PCT-specific moveset data is captured in this file's moves/combos arrays since chr_b 15 is the base variant; it belongs in a separate PCT Nami (chr_b 19) extraction.
- The guide's note at the top of section 5 says: for 4-koma laughter Nami, swap the power damage values with the laughter damage values and vice-versa. This file records the damage numbers exactly as labeled in the guide's Power/Knowledge and Laughter columns without applying that swap, since it's unclear which basic-attack instances (if any beyond the 4-koma) this applies to.
- Both the 4-koma (Knowledge) and 4-koma (Laughter) sections in the guide list 'Special Attack Nature: Knowledge' for both Trouble and Mirage Tempo, even under the Laughter-labeled koma heading; this looks like a copy-paste error in the source guide but is reproduced as written since no correction is stated.
- The 5-koma and 6-koma sections do not explicitly state a nature label the way the 4-koma sections do (no 'Special Attack Nature:' line); this file assumes 'knowledge' for those komas based on the evolution chart context, but the guide does not say so directly.
- Ultimate Action text says opponents lose 'some' of their special bar with no numeric value given; recorded as null/unspecified in the description.
- Guide states forward Y (Cyclone Tempo) 'can hit multiple opponents in a line' but doesn't quantify how many; not included as a numeric property.
- Section 4 (Evolution Chart) and section 7 (Ally Boosts / suggested koma) and section 10 (Sample Decks) contain deck-building, gem-cost, and ally-boost content that is explicitly out of scope per the battle-mechanics-only extraction rule and is not represented in this file.
