### Allen Walker — FAQ 47453

**Source:** `47453-allen-walker.txt`; author: Syfex Blade (guide credits Logan Murry as copyright holder); version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Basic B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (12 to Laughter).

- **side B:** Forward B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (12 to Laughter).

- **air B:** Aerial B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (12 to Laughter).

- **Y:** Basic Y attack.
  - Damage: 12 (18 to Laughter).

- **side Y:** Forward Y attack. Can be charged for extra damage.
  - Damage: 12 (18 to Laughter); charged: 36 (54 to Laughter); Properties: charge.

- **air Y:** Aerial Y attack.
  - Damage: 12 (18 to Laughter).

- **select — Timcanpi:** Timcanpi (the golden thing flying with Allen) goes around in a circle; if he connects with an opponent you steal a small amount of their SP.
  - Properties: status:SP steal.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:**
  - X — Cross Beam: Similar to Allen's 2-koma Support, only he shoots it ahead of you instead of above. He shoots out 5 lasers that deal a good bit more than his support. (Damage: 1 hit: 8 damage (12 to Laughter); 2 hits: 16 damage (24 to Laughter); 3 hits: 24 damage (36 to Laughter); 4 hits: 32 damage (48 to Laughter); 5 hits: 40 damage (60 to Laughter); Hits: 5; Properties: projectile, multi-hit)
  - Up+X — Cross Grave: Exactly the same as Allen's 3-koma support. (Damage: 30 (45 to Laughter); Properties: guard break)

- **5-koma — knowledge:**
  - X — Cross Beam: Completely different from his previous two Cross Beams. This is a practical straightforward barrage of beams that fire directly in front of you. (Damage: 42 (63 to Laughter); Properties: projectile)
  - Up+X — Cross Grave: It's just got a slightly bigger damage area than the 4-koma did. Easier to combo into, but overall it's not really different. (Damage: 38 (57 to Laughter); Properties: guard break)

- **6-koma — knowledge:**
  - X — Destruction Claw: Shoots a much larger energy beam that takes the shape of a claw. Fires directly in front of you. (Damage: 41 (57 to Laughter); Properties: projectile, charge)
  - Up+X — Clown Belt: Allen creates a mass of spinning web-like things that damage anyone near him. (Damage: 50 (75 to Laughter))

- **6-koma — power:**
  - X — Destruction Claw: Same as the base 6-koma Allen's Destruction Claw; this Power-variant koma takes more damage from Laughter instead of Power, and deals more damage to Knowledge instead of Laughter. (Properties: projectile, charge)
  - Up+X — Clown Belt: Same as the base 6-koma Allen's Clown Belt; this Power-variant koma takes more damage from Laughter instead of Power, and deals more damage to Knowledge instead of Laughter.

#### Combos

- B - Forward+B - Forward+Y                  28 (42) -- A simple fast combo, gets them away enough for you to be safe.
- B - Forward+B - Y - Forward+Y              40 (60) -- A little slower, but deals more damage with the same effect as the above.
- Down+Y - Aerial B - Aerial Y               34 (51) -- A great combo, starts off with his Guard break and goes into a bit of extra damage. Especially useful in Sudden Deaths.
- B - Forward+B - Y - 4 Koma Allen's X       68 (102) -- Nice damage, easy to pull off. Only problem is it's a bit slow, and you have to be at close range to be able to pull it off.
- B - Forward B - Y - 4/5-Koma Allen's Up+X  58 (87) / 66 (99) -- Damage is pretty good, but you have to be even closer to do it. It ends with a nice guard break though.
- 2-koma Kanda - 6-koma Allen's X             59 (71) -- Another nice one, works best with 6-koma Allen, but 5-koma can work as well. What this does is take advantage of Kanda's guard breaking and small popup effect to shoot them with a beam.
- 2-koma Piccolo - 6-Koma Allen's Up+X        66 (91) -- Good, fast, unavoidable damage. Can't beat it.
- 2-koma Gyro - 6-Koma Allen's Up+X           66 (99) -- Exactly the same, just slightly slower, has slightly better range, and does slightly more damage to Knowledge characters.
- B - Y - 3-Koma Lavi                         47 (70) -- Nice unexpected Allen combo, causes burn, and fits a D.Gray-Man theme.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide never explicitly lists 'down B', 'up B', 'down Y' as separate named moves in the basic moveset table, but the combo section uses 'Down+Y' (described as 'his Guard break') implying a down+Y guard-break move exists that isn't detailed in the basic moveset section. No damage value is given for Down+Y alone, only within the combo total.
- No down+B forced-switch attack is mentioned anywhere in the guide.
- No per-koma HP/J-Soul-to-HP values are given as 'hp'; the guide instead reports 'J-Soul' values (4-koma: 136, 5-koma: 152, 6-koma: 168, 6-koma Power Variant: 168), which are not the same stat as HP per the schema, so hp is left null and J-Soul values are recorded here instead.
- The Power-variant 6-koma's X and Up+X special damage numbers are not restated numerically; the guide only says the effect is 'exactly the same' with swapped nature interactions, so damage is null for that koma's specials.
- The guide's koma role/shape section calls the 4-koma and 5-koma Support koma 'Support' with sizes 2 and 3 respectively (2-koma and 3-koma), and separately there is a 4/5/6-koma Battle koma line; no 1-koma battle koma is described in this guide.
- Author byline in the header says 'Created by Syfex Blade - 2007' but the Legal section says 'This guide is Copyright 2007 Logan Murry.' -- these two names may refer to the same person under different names/handles, or this may be a discrepancy in the source text itself.
- No 6-koma or higher komas beyond the two 6-koma variants are described; the evolution chart implies two separate 6-koma battle paths (one via Cross unlock), matching what's captured.
