### Tsuna — FAQ 48474

**Source:** `48474-tsuna.txt`; author: Felix Frenander // Neophos // Offensive_Altar.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — jab:** His jab. Fast, easy to combo from, can cancel into almost anything, decent range and safe enough. Poke on people two or three times to mix them up a bit.

- **side B:** Fast with good priority. Crosses up on block when close. Can combo into his Y with timing. A good poke. Has an almost ridiculous amount of invincibility frames, nearly from start to end, letting it counter almost any kind of move and bypass certain 'safe' walls. After the invincibility ends, the hitbox stays close to the ground for a short while.
  - Properties: counter.

- **down B:** His force change. Pretty slow, decent range, can hit characters on platforms above you. Not terribly useful.
  - Properties: forced switch.

- **up B:** Can catch people by surprise if they're not used to it. On block, use to cross up (or cross up twice in one jump to mix it up). Follow with aerial B for pressure. On hit, can be repeated if the opponent doesn't move away, or followed by a Y or forward Y after a moment.

- **air B:** Can be used to recover; clashes with a lot of edge guarding attacks. Can catch people after an up B, and can catch people running away on platforms. Useful, but becomes predictable if overused.

- **Y:** Good range but slow startup. On hit, can combo into his X special regardless of koma, as long as the opponent doesn't anticipate it. The opponent can fast fall through it to make the special miss.

- **side Y — the stumble:** Tsuna's best edge guarding option when used properly. Use it for pressure and combos, without getting predictable with the number of stumbles. Can be ended at any time, resulting in between 2 and 5 stumbles. Amazing priority, but very easy to escape on block if the opponent knows about it.
  - Hits: between 2 and 5 (mash-extendable); Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Tsuna's guard break. Has range, but the hitbox is small. Decently fast, but the small hitbox and the chance of simply jumping over the opponent makes it rather useless.
  - Properties: guard break.

- **up Y:** A decent projectile (basketballs). Mix up between using one and both to keep the opponent guessing on when to stop blocking. Has rather bad priority and is easily destroyed by rapid hits.
  - Properties: projectile.

- **air Y:** Has less lag than it looks like, but still a laggy attack. Useful when unexpected, but shouldn't be overused. Impossible to cancel after using on the way down (beware of gaps), though the landing lag is special-cancelable.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from specials (innate ability).
  - Passive: Immune to the blindness effect (innate ability).
  - Passive: Has auto-guard (innate ability).
  - X: A three hit combo. The last hit hits them downwards, making it deadly on the edge. It's fast and decent pressure, although it's a pretty weak special. (Damage: 26; Hits: 3; Properties: multi-hit)
  - up X: A splash attack, useful in FFAs/teams but otherwise a pretty bad special. Slow enough that most people can block, dash, or jump away on reaction. Pretty strong against power characters.

- **5-koma:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from specials (innate ability).
  - Passive: Immune to the blindness effect (innate ability).
  - Passive: Has auto-guard (innate ability).
  - X: A nine hit combo. Against people who don't push block, a great pressure tool, especially with multihitting supports near the wall. The last hit is again a pseudo-spike, but earlier hits often push people off the edge, making the last, deadly hit miss unless spaced well. (Hits: 9; Properties: multi-hit)
  - up X: Not very useful; the major range of this attack makes it hard to properly space since much of what it hits isn't visible from the starting position. Strong but rarely lands; can sometimes get you out of corner pressure.

- **6-koma:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from specials (innate ability).
  - Passive: Immune to the blindness effect (innate ability).
  - Passive: Has auto-guard (innate ability).
  - X: Generally Tsuna's best special. The final hit spikes them down, making it almost a guaranteed KO over an edge or on a stage with a lot of gaps. If near a wall, one of the hits will miss and the special deals a lot less damage. Deals a tremendous amount of damage to power characters. (Properties: multi-hit, spike)
  - up X: Tsuna's most powerful special. Deals near 100 damage on power characters, so a decent combo into it is almost always a guaranteed KO on most power characters. Has very slow startup, and neither impressive range nor a huge hitbox. (Damage: near 100 (on power characters); Properties: charge)

#### Combos

- B, Y, Kagura 3 koma support (spaced/timed correctly) is an infinite combo; also infinite against a wall using a stumble then Kagura 3 on the fourth hit or so.
- Y into X special, regardless of koma, if the opponent doesn't anticipate it.
- 6 koma uX can sometimes land after a finished stumble against the wall if the opponent doesn't jump away instantly.
- Aerial Y landing lag is special-cancelable, so a special can follow an aerial Y.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide states Tsuna has 3 battle komas and one alternate, with Tsuna being laughter and the alternate being power, but only describes specials for 4/5/6 koma without stating which of those (or the alternate) correspond to the power nature; only the 4 koma is explicitly labeled laughter in this extraction based on the general statement 'Tsuna is a laughter character.' The 5 koma and 6 koma nature (laughter vs the power alternate) is not stated per-koma, so nature is null for those entries.
- No SELECT Ultimate Action is described anywhere in the guide.
- No explicit 'B string' section describing a chained basic-attack sequence beyond individual B/side B/up B/Y/side Y/up Y descriptions; treated as separate moves per the guide's own breakdown.
- No koma HP values are given in the guide text itself (chr_b_note HP of 348 comes from the mapping table, not this guide).
- Down B (force change) has no damage/hit count given in the guide.
- The guide never explicitly labels its author 'version'; only a last-updated date of 19/06/2007 is given, and the closing credits list the author as 'Felix Frenander // Neophos // Offensive_Altar'.
- Innate ability 'takes less damage from specials' is stated once in the battle koma intro, and it's unclear if this applies to all three battle koma sizes or only some; applied here to all listed battle koma (4/5/6) per the general framing 'Along with Tsuna's innate ability... he lives longer then most characters with his HP.'
