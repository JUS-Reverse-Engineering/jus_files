### Jaguar — FAQ 46575

**Source:** `46575-jaguar.txt`; author: shadowboyz; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Regain a small amount of your SP bar.

- **B — Recorder Jab:** Hits opponent with his recorder, has a 45 degree range.
  - Damage: 12 power, 8 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1.

- **side B — Stumble:** Falls and hits opponent. Decent knockback.
  - Damage: 6 power, 4 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1.

- **up B — Extending recorder:** Recorder extends upward, knocking opponents a few inches off the ground if it hits.
  - Damage: 12 power, 8 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1; Properties: pop.

- **down B — Recorder Puff:** Blows on recorder. Causes opponent to switch characters if it connects.
  - Damage: 12 power, 8 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Jaguar swimming:** Swims on air using his recorders. Good for dodging beam attacks.
  - Damage: 12 power, 8 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 8; Properties: multi-hit.

- **Y — Ramen Dump!:** Drops a bowl of hot ramen on the enemy. Good for wall breaking.
  - Damage: 24 power, 16 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1.

- **side Y — Jaguar submerged swimming:** Does a land version of Jaguar swimming. Can turn around and hits opponent with oxygen tank at the end for knockdown.
  - Damage: 39 power, 26 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 7; Properties: knockdown, multi-hit.

- **up Y — Jaguar kick:** Uses recorder to propel off the ground and kick the opponent.
  - Damage: 18 power, 12 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1.

- **down Y — Jaguar text balloon:** Puts on white suit and speaks a huge text bubble.
  - Damage: 24 power, 16 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1.

- **air Y — Jaguar Ramen Swirl:** In the air Jaguar pulls ramen out of a bowl and swirls it below him, propelling him upward and catching the enemy. Can be comboed to up Y attack.
  - Damage: 34 power, 23 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 9; Properties: multi-hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma:** HP 144.
  - X — Jaguar Fart: Lags for a while, then hits enemies directly in front and back of him. (Damage: 54 power, 36 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 1)
  - up X — Jaguar healing dance: Heals around 1/4 of Jaguar's HP after a long delay.

- **5-koma:** HP 160.
  - X — Jaguar Dash Attack: Jaguar dashes forward for a short while, and if he hits an opponent he grabs them, headbutts them, and then two white creatures appear on both sides of the opponent and shout, launching the opponent sky high. (Damage: 60 power, 40 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 2; Properties: launcher)
  - up X — Jaguar Dance: Does a LONG dance, at the end of which either 3 bombs or three J-Coins appear. Guide calls it worthless.

- **6-koma:** HP 176.
  - X — Summon Drummer: Summons a kid with drums who moves forward slowly, hitting enemies with his drumsticks. (Damage: 69 power, 46 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 12; Properties: multi-hit)
  - up X — Summon drooler: Summons a kid who drools onto the platforms below Jaguar. Causes speed down on enemy. (Damage: 120 power, 80 knowledge/laughter; Hits: 4; Properties: status:speed down)

#### Combos

- Up Y + Jump Y - Knocks opponent into the air, then you can hit them with two (or 3 if you have a triple jump help koma equipped) ramen swirls for around 100 damage.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Passive abilities listed under 'Jaguar's basic attacks' (JR3) apply to Jaguar overall, not to a specific koma: 'Immunity to Confusion' and 'Immunity to Paralysis'.
- Boost koma: Piyohiko, Hammer and Hamii each give Jaguar an 8 J-soul boost; these are 1/2/3-block boost koma, not standard help/support/battle koma, so they are not represented in the koma array.
- Guide's strategy section (JR5) recommends support koma from other characters (Trunks 3 koma, Seiya 3 koma, Satsuki 2 koma/Ichigo 100%, Sena 3 koma) as synergy picks for Jaguar; not Jaguar's own koma, so omitted from the koma array.
- Guide states Jaguar 'should never use Jaguar's specials' as a strategy note, seemingly at odds with describing the X specials as usable moves — kept as-is since this is guide commentary, not a contradiction in the move data itself.
- Guide is incomplete/unfinished: JR5 ends with 'More supports, combos, and a better strategy coming when the weather isn't 40 degrees farenheight where I live.' -- no B string is described anywhere in the guide.
