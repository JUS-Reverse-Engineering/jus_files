### Taikoubou — FAQ 46293

**Source:** `46293-taikoubou.txt`; author: Dojun Woo (ahrimin); version 1.11.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Weak Attack:** Taikoubou's standard "chi" attack; he swings his Paopei in a quick manner.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 7 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 10 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 10 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 7 to laughter komaman..

- **up B — Weak, Upwards Attack:** Taikoubou wildly swings his Paopei around in a 5-hit combo.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 6 total to power komaman, 6 total to knowledge komaman, 9 total to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 9 total to power komaman, 6 total to knowledge komaman, 6 total to laughter komaman.; Hits: 5; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down B — Short-ranged Attack:** Taikoubou's apprentice Bukichi runs onto the battlefield. If he hits someone at close distance, he inflicts damage and causes the target to switch battle characters.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 8 to power komaman, 8 to knowledge komaman, 12 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 12 to power komaman, 8 to knowledge komaman, 8 to laughter komaman.; Properties: forced switch.

- **side B — Long-ranged Attack:** Taikoubou swings his Paopei and releases an energy wave.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 7 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 10 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 10 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 7 to laughter komaman.; Properties: projectile.

- **air B — Short-ranged Aerial Attack (Jump-B, A+B):** Taikoubou rides on Suupuusan to charge head-on.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 7 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 10 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 10 to power komaman, 7 to knowledge komaman, 7 to laughter komaman..

- **Y — Strong Attack:** Taikoubou calls on Kou Hiko to swing his two-handed sword, which talks.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 19 to power komaman, 19 to knowledge komaman, 28 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 28 to power komaman, 19 to knowledge komaman, 19 to laughter komaman..

- **up Y — Strong, Upwards Attack:** Taikoubou's genius-ranked ally Youzen flies upwards and swings his trident-shaped Paopei.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 16 to power komaman, 16 to knowledge komaman, 24 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 24 to power komaman, 16 to knowledge komaman, 16 to laughter komaman..

- **down Y — Concentrated, Short-ranged Attack:** Fugen, one of Taikoubou's strongest friends, uses his Paopei to make a small energy explosion. This can knock back items and enemies, and can break the opponent's guard.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 14 to power komaman, 14 to knowledge komaman, 21 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 21 to power komaman, 14 to knowledge komaman, 14 to laughter komaman.; Properties: guard break.

- **side Y — Strong, long-ranged Attack:** The son of Kou Hiko, Kou Tenka, rushes forward and slashes with his "light-saber" Paopei.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 14 to power komaman, 14 to knowledge komaman, 21 to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 21 to power komaman, 14 to knowledge komaman, 14 to laughter komaman..

- **air Y — Strong, Ranged and Homing Attack (Jump-Y, A+Y):** The "Human Paopei" Nataku appears and shoots his metal "wrist-bands" onto the closest enemy; the attack hits twice, once for each projectile.
  - Damage: Knowledge based: 16 total to power komaman, 16 total to knowledge komaman, 24 total to laughter komaman. Laughter based: 24 total to power komaman, 16 total to knowledge komaman, 16 total to laughter komaman.; Hits: 2; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **other — Down-Jump or Down-Down:** On a thin platform, this drops Taikoubou through to the area below. In the air, Taikoubou falls faster than usual, but cannot jump again while falling faster.

- **other — Forward-Forward:** Allows Taikoubou to dash forward; near an enemy, repeated dashes let him "dash through" them.

- **other — Holding Down:** A simple blocking stance.

- **select — Select Button (Taunt):** Universal taunt. Taikoubou sits on the ground and holds his Paopei out to fish.

- **other — L & R Buttons:** Assigning support/battle komas to the shoulder buttons lets you execute them directly, and lets you perform a "dream combo" using L & R.

- **other — Touch Screen:** Touching a battle koma triggers a quick attack (or a dream combo); touching a support koma activates the selected support character.

- **B string — Combo Starters:** The guide lists B, Up+B, and Forward+B as combo starters that lead into further hits; see combos for the specific chains and damage totals.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide gives every move's damage as both 'knowledge based' and 'laughter based' variants against power/knowledge/laughter komaman targets, but never states which specific koma panel-size or which specific koma is 'knowledge' vs 'laughter' natured; koma.nature is left null throughout because of this ambiguity.
- No HP values are given anywhere in the guide for any Taikoubou koma size, so koma.hp is null for all entries.
- The 6 Panel Koma section lists only one set of X / Up-X specials under the heading '6 Panel Koma (Horizontal & Vertical)', implying 6BH and 6BV share the same special moves; the guide does not distinguish separate specials for 6BH vs 6BV.
- In the 'From B' combo section, the laughter-based damage line reads '24 total damage to power komaman, 40 total damage to knowledge komaman, 59 total damage to laughter komaman' -- the 24/40/59 pattern is inconsistent with every other laughter-based line in the guide (which normally leads with the highest number first); copied verbatim as a likely guide typo.
- Section VI describes Help Komas and Support Komas for Dakki, Outenkun, and Suupuusan (Taikoubou's ally-boost characters), not Taikoubou himself; these were left out of the koma array since they belong to other characters' trees, per the character-scoped schema, but are noted here: Dakki help = 'Increase SP Gauge when using multiple Special Attacks in a short time'; Outenkun help = 'Immunity from Poison'; Suupuusan help = 'Triple Jump'; Dakki support (2S) = summon Dakki for two quick slashes, 24 damage, 2 hits, knockback; Dakki support (3S) = Dakki performs a battle seal and drains 1 SP.
- Section IX (Ally Boost Data) describes HP/SP boost mechanics from Dakki, Suupuusan, and Raamenman (Kinnikuman) when placed near Taikoubou -- general ally-boost system info, not a Taikoubou move/koma stat, so not captured in the schema.
- Section VII gem-price/koma-tree diagrams (unlock costs in colored gems) are meta-progression data, not combat stats, and were not mapped into the schema.
- The guide is annotated with dagger (Genroh) and double-dagger (ChocoboMog123) symbols marking contributor-sourced claims (e.g. Down-Y guard break, the 6-panel X ring-out/deck-size scaling, and the combo list); these are folded into the relevant quotes but the guide's own attribution symbols are not separately modeled.
