### Piccolo — FAQ 46844

**Source:** `46844-piccolo.txt`; author: Yamcha; version 1.1.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Arm Swing:** The basis for all combos, it is a simple short range starting move. Fast and effective to get the job done.
  - Damage: 8 [Power][Knowledge], 12 [Laughter].

- **side B — Kousengan - Eye Beam:** A nice fast move. You can use it as the next part of a combo after the idle B, or to poke an unexpecting adversary. There isn't too much lag time afterwards so it's a safe poking move to rely on.
  - Damage: 8 [P][K], 12 [L].

- **up B — Upward Kick:** Piccolo's air launcher move. Use this when you want to kick someone upwards for an aerial combo. It's a very fast move so feel free to use it on its own or integrate it into a combo.
  - Damage: 9 [P][K], 13 [L]; Properties: launcher.

- **down B — Quick Knee:** The character change move. Don't rely on this too much unless you really need to finish off a character that is almost dead.
  - Damage: 8 [P][K], 12 [L]; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Aerial Arm Swing:** Combo filler to be used after the Up B. It has a short reach so you have to be close for it to connect.
  - Damage: 8 [P][K], 12 [L].

- **Y — Bakurikimaha - Exploding Powerful Demon Wave:** This is Piccolo's hardest hitting attack. However it has significant lag time after it is used and knocks your opponent down, so it is best reserved for ending a combo. If you miss when using this move, Piccolo is wide open to a counterattack.
  - Damage: 15 [P][K], 22 [L]; Properties: knockdown.

- **side Y — Nobiru ude - Expanding Arm:** Piccolo extends his arm forward to bring the fight to him. This is an excellent surprise attack, poke, and combo extender all in one package.
  - Damage: 11 [P][K], 16 [L].

- **up Y — Kuchikarakikouha - Energy Wave from the Mouth:** An anti-air attack that fires at a 45-50 degree angle. This is an excellent move to ground those pesky airborne fighters.
  - Damage: 15 [P][K], 22 [L].

- **down Y — Tsuno Beam - Antenna Beam:** The guard breaker move. Use it to help any shy turtlers get into the fight.
  - Damage: 15 [P][K], 22 [L]; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Delayed Air Fireball:** Piccolo leaves a purple fireball in the air. It will activate after either 2 seconds pass or an enemy approaches it.
  - Damage: 8 [P][K], 12 [L]; Properties: projectile, status:delayed detonation.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 136.
  - Passive: Gradually Regain Health
  - Passive: Gain more SP when fighting Laughter type opponents.
  - X — Makankosappo: A beam that drills through multiple enemies. Has a brief charge time before firing. (Damage: 3-27 [P][L], 4-45 [K]; Properties: projectile, charge)
  - up X — Sai Sei - Rebirth: Piccolo tears his arm off and to recover a substantial amount of health. (Damage: N/A; Properties: status:heal)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 152.
  - Passive: Gradually Regain Health
  - Passive: Gain more SP when fighting Laughter type opponents.
  - X — Makankosappo: The powered up version of his trademark move. Hold X to charge it up until the aura around his hand gets more powerful. (Damage: 3-48 [P][L], 4-66 [K]; Properties: projectile, charge)
  - up X — Kakusanyudokodan - Homing Fireballs: 6 Fireballs are summoned and simultaneously close in on an adversary. They must be on your screen for all the fireballs to connect. (Damage: 36 [P][K], 54 [L]; Properties: projectile, multi-hit)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide does not give a SELECT Ultimate Action move description outside the koma's Ultimate Action line ('Hold Select to slowly regain SP energy' for both 4 and 5 koma) — recorded as a passive-adjacent koma detail rather than a top-level move, since the guide presents it under koma info, not the Controls and Moves section.
- Koma section shows koma sizes as ASCII grids of brackets ([ ] layouts) for the 2, 3, 4, and 5 koma rather than a plain number; sizes recorded above (2, 3, 4, 5) are inferred from the koma piece count in the guide's title lines (e.g. 'Piccolo 2 Koma', 'Piccolo 4 Koma'), not measured from the bracket grids.
- The 2 koma and 3 koma entries are listed under '~Help and Support Koma~' with 'Effect:' lines but the guide never explicitly labels them 'support' vs 'help' individually — both are grouped under one heading covering help and support; treated the Piccolo Help Koma line as the help koma and the 2/3 koma (which have damage values and Effect descriptions like specials) as support koma.
- No explicit 'up X' input label is given for Rebirth/Homing Fireballs in the source text — the guide lists them as 'Up X (4 Koma)' and 'Up X (5 Koma)' headers, which is used directly as the input.
- No down+B forced-switch damage distinction or explicit 'forced switch' wording is stated by the guide beyond 'The character change move' — property inferred from schema's own note that down+B is always the forced-switch move.
