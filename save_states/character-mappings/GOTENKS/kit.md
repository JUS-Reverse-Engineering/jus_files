### Gotenks — FAQ 46608

**Source:** `46608-gotenks.txt`; author: fuzzylittlbunny.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A standard jab. Not particularly fast or strong, but a useful combo starter.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power.

- **side B:** Gotenks flies headfirst into the opponent. He doesn't travel too far, but it has pretty good priority.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power.

- **Y:** A flurry of quick punches; you can tap Y to do more hits.
  - Damage: 9 to Knowledge/Laughter, 13 to Power; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **side Y — Galactic Donuts:** Gotenks makes a golden ring over his head and throws it forward. If it hits the opponent, it temporarily stuns them for about one second. It can't repeatedly hit them for an infinite combo.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power; Properties: projectile, status:stun.

- **down B — Forced Change:** He winds up and delivers a left punch that hits three times and changes their current battle character.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power; Hits: 3; Properties: forced switch.

- **down Y — Guard Break:** He takes a deep breath and yells something in scratchy Hiragana letters.
  - Damage: 20 to Knowledge/Laughter, 30 to Power; Properties: guard break.

- **up B:** Gotenks jumps diagonally up/forward and delivers a kick at the peak of his jump.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power.

- **up Y:** Gotenks does a back flip and launches himself diagonally up/forward, using the same animation as his side+B attack.
  - Damage: 20 to Knowledge/Laughter, 30 to Power.

- **air B:** Gotenks uses his fist to knock the opponent back to the ground. It's a great spike in terms of speed, but it has pretty bad range. It will also knock standing opponents through platforms that you can drop through.
  - Damage: 10 to Knowledge/Laughter, 15 to Power.

- **air Y:** Basically an aerial version of his side+B attack, except you can control its path by holding a direction on the D-Pad. This can also be used as a third jump for recovery if your double jump is used. It can also be used to surprise an opponent waiting to edge-guard while you're hanging on a ledge, by letting go and using this move upward.
  - Damage: 18 to Knowledge/Laughter, 27 to Power.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** He powers up in the traditional DBZ style, slowly gaining SP while you hold Select.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma:**
  - X — Galactic Donuts: Gotenks goes Super Saiyan and fires five rings forward. If they all hit, the opponent is temporarily immobile in a little golden ball. The ball remains off the ground, which stops the opponent from combo breaking with most supports. (Damage: 30 to Knowledge/Laughter, 45 to Power; Properties: status:immobile)
  - up X — Super Hitsusawazafurukosu: Gotenks does an eleven-hit combo, yelling stuff along the way. It starts up pretty quickly and is stronger than his Galactic Donuts. There is no way to cancel the move once started, so missing leaves him wide open. (Damage: 35 to Knowledge/Laughter, 52 to Power; Hits: eleven-hit combo; Properties: multi-hit)

- **5-koma — laughter:**
  - X — Saiko No Kogeki (AKA Victory Cannon): Gotenks turns Super Saiyan 3, opens his mouth and lets out a blast of energy. A standard beam special; the guide's author uses this in most matches for its fast start and good knock back. (Damage: 36 to Knowledge/Laughter, 54 to Power; Properties: projectile)
  - up X — Super Ghost Kamikaze Attack: Gotenks turns Super Saiyan 3 and spits out a little ghost clone of himself that is sent toward an opponent and explodes on contact. Holding X charges out up to three more ghosts (up to four total), based on how many times his cheeks visibly expand before release. Long start-up makes it risky at close range. (Damage: 12-48 to Knowledge/Laughter, 18-72 to Power; Properties: charge, status:explode-on-contact)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - X — Saiko No Kogeki (AKA Victory Cannon): Gotenks turns Super Saiyan 3, opens his mouth and lets out a blast of energy. A standard beam special; the guide's author uses this in most matches for its fast start and good knock back. (Damage: 36 to Knowledge/Laughter, 54 to Power; Properties: projectile)
  - up X — Super Ghost Kamikaze Attack: Gotenks turns Super Saiyan 3 and spits out a little ghost clone of himself that is sent toward an opponent and explodes on contact. Holding X charges out up to three more ghosts (up to four total), based on how many times his cheeks visibly expand before release. Long start-up makes it risky at close range. (Damage: 12-48 to Knowledge/Laughter, 18-72 to Power; Properties: charge, status:explode-on-contact)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- chr_b ambiguity: the guide describes Gotenks as one continuous character whose 4-Koma form is base and 5-Koma form is Super Saiyan 3 (via evolution), not as two separately selectable characters. chr_b-Complete-Mapping.md splits base Gotenks (row 6) from Gotenks (SSJ) (row 7). Assigned chr_b: 6 for the overall file; the 5-Koma Laughter/Power entries in the koma array may map to chr_b: 7 instead, but the guide gives no basis to split them into a separate JSON entry.
- The guide states 'Only his four Koma can combo into a special without a counterhit, so there won't be any long, flashy combos' and 'He can't link the basic B, forward+B, Y, forward+Y combo' -- this is general strategy commentary about the character overall, not a specific move or combo, so it isn't captured as a 'combos' entry.
- 'Help Koma: Increase SP Gauge when using multiple Special Attacks in a short time' is stated in the Evolving Koma section as a general property of a Help Koma slot for this character, but the guide never states which Koma size (if any, beyond the 2/3/4/5 listed) is the Help Koma, or gives it a distinct entry in the evolution cost list. Not added as a separate koma entry due to missing size.
- Damage figures throughout are given as pairs, e.g. '10 to Knowledge/Laughter' and '15 to Power' -- these appear to be damage dealt TO opponents of those natures, not Gotenks' own nature, per the guide's own framing note: 'the damage is using the Laughter Gotenks. If you're using the Power Gotenks, just switch the higher damage to Knowledge characters and the lower damage to Power and Laughter characters.' This is preserved verbatim in each move/special 'damage' field as written, but the target-audience convention is unresolved/confusing as written by the guide's author himself.
- 'Gotenks gets a boost from Goku, Vegeta, Vegetto, and Bobobo' is stated in the moveset intro but does not fit any schema field (it describes support-boost synergy from other characters' support koma, not Gotenks' own support/help effects).
- No explicit HP values are given anywhere in the guide for any Koma size.
- No B string (multi-hit B combo chain) is separately described beyond the single B move; the guide explicitly says Gotenks cannot chain B -> side B -> Y -> side Y as a combo.
