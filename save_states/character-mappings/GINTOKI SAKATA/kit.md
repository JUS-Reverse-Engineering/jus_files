### Gintoki — FAQ 46732

**Source:** `46732-gintoki.txt`; author: Demon-Inuyasha; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Foot Thrust:** A plain foot thrust, can be used for starting combos.
  - Damage: 10 or 7 (power); 7 (laughter); 7 or 10 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **side B — Forward Swing:** Gintoki steps forward and swings his bokuto. Can be used for combos.
  - Damage: 12 or 8 (power); 8 (laughter); 8 or 12 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **up B — Bomb in MY face!!!!:** Gintoki pulls out a bomb and it explodes in his face. Good surprise move.
  - Damage: 13 or 9 (power); 9 (laughter); 9 or 13 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **down B — Word Bubble ( Force Change ):** Gintoki turns around and says something making a word bubble appear which counts as a hit.
  - Damage: 10 or 7 (power); 7 (laughter); 7 or 10 (knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Side Swing:** Gintoki swings his bokuto in the air.
  - Damage: 10 or 7 (power); 7 (laughter); 7 or 10 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **Y — Construction Worker ( Can hit on both sides ):** Gintoki quickly turns around with some lumber on his shoulder. Good range.
  - Damage: 28 or 19 (power); 19 (laughter); 19 or 28 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **side Y — Bokuto Throw:** Gintoki throws his bokuto and if it connects it will go straight up in the air then coming back down. Really good pressure and keep away move.
  - Damage: 19+7 or 13+4 (power); 13+4 (laughter); 13+4 or 19+7 (knowledge); Hits: 3+5; Properties: multi-hit.

- **up Y — Bomb Toss:** Gintoki throws a bomb over his shoulder. Good edge guarding move.
  - Damage: 21 or 13 (power); 13 (laughter); 13 or 21 (knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **down B — Headbutt ( Breaks Guard ):** Gintoki will swing back and thrust forward with his head.
  - Damage: 24 or 16 (power); 16 (laughter); 16 or 24 (knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Mini Bombs:** Gintoki throws mini bombs and they explode soon after.
  - Damage: 7+19 or 5+13 (power); 5+13 (laughter); 5+13 or 7+19 (knowledge); Hits: 1+1; Properties: multi-hit.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Gintoki stops and pulls out his Jump Magazine and starts to read, sometimes falling to the floor looking depressed. Also regains his SP.
  - Properties: status:SP regain.

#### Combos

- COMBO #1: Neutral B, then Forward+B, then Neutral+B. Short but good damage, especially on the last hit.
- COMBO #2: Neutral B, then Up+B, then Neutral+B. Good and damaging like the first.
- COMBO #3 (Only with 5 Koma): Neutral B, then Forward+B (can be replaced with Up+B) as a basic combo starter, then Neutral+Y to hit with wood, then instantly Special A. Easy, fast, does a lot of damage, and has a possibility of a ring out.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The FAQ's title banner reads 'KENSHIRO CHARACTER FAQ' even though the entire document is about Gintoki; likely a copy-paste error by the author.
- The guide lists a move labeled 'Down+B: Headbutt ( Breaks Guard )' appearing after Up+Y in the B-string move list, which conflicts with the earlier 'Down+B: Word Bubble ( Force Change )' entry — both are labeled Down+B. The Headbutt entry's position in the list (between Up+Y and Jump+Y) suggests it may actually be intended as Down+Y, but the guide's text explicitly says 'Down+B' for both, so both are recorded here as written under input 'down B'.
- The 6-koma appears twice with identical move names/hit counts/descriptions but with power and laughter/knowledge damage numbers swapped between the two entries (one nature 'Laughter', one nature 'Power') — recorded as two separate koma entries per the guide.
- Section 5 ('Getting Gintoki') is vague/uncertain: 'You start off with Gintama's help support I think.' — the guide author is unsure.
- Combos section is explicitly marked incomplete: 'WILL UPDATE WITH MORE COMBOS ASAP ( Just need to train with him some more )'.
- The guide gives evolution/cost data (koma tree, yellow/white/red/blue point costs) which is economy data and out of scope per battle-mechanics-only extraction rules; omitted from this JSON.
- Support koma recommendations from other characters (Shishio, Trunks, Seiya, Hitsugaya, Sena, Satsuki, Gotenks) are general strategy notes about pairing, not Gintoki's own moves/koma, so not included as Gintoki koma entries.
