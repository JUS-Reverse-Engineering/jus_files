### Rukia — FAQ 46229

**Source:** `46229-rukia.txt`; author: Shiroiame (tennoame@gmail.com).

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Weak attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 7(10).

- **up B:** Upwards weak attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 7(10).

- **down B:** Force Character Change Attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 7(10); Properties: forced switch.

- **side B:** Forward weak attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 8(12).

- **air B:** Aerial weak attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 8(12).

- **Y:** Strong attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 10(15).

- **up Y:** Upwards strong attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma. Chargeable; second value pair is the fully-charged damage.
  - Damage: 3-21(4-31) ; 6-36(9-54); Properties: charge.

- **down Y:** Guard Break attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma.
  - Damage: 14(21); Properties: guard break.

- **side Y:** Forward strong attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma. Chargeable; second value pair is the fully-charged damage.
  - Damage: 3-21(4-31) ; 6-36(9-54); Properties: charge.

- **air Y:** Aerial strong attack, universal to all Rukia battle koma. Chargeable; second value pair is the fully-charged damage.
  - Damage: 3-21(4-31) ; 6-36(9-54); Properties: charge.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Rukia breathes out. It counts as a hit, though it deals no damage, and recovers 1/5 of a full SP bar.
  - Damage: 0; Hits: 1.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Ability: sodenoshirayuki (Sleeve of White Snow) - Enables Air Dash and gives an immunity to Freeze effect. Also recovers SP when attacking with Support Koma.
  - X — Hadounosanjuusan-Soukatsui (Destructive Teachings 31-Falling Pale Fire): After roughly a 1/3 second startup, Rukia fires a large blast forward, dealing damage and knocking the enemy away and down. Reaches roughly a full half screen; the enemy needs half their body on screen to be hit. (Damage: 25(37); Properties: projectile, knockdown)
  - up X — Sodenoshirayuki (Sleeve of White Ice): Rukia's Shinigami outfit appears, she pulls her sword back, then slices sideways, causing a quick spread of ice on the floor that freezes anything on the ground roughly 2/3 of a full screen outwards from Rukia. There is roughly a 3/4 second startup, and it can spread to any other platform within range/on the same level of height. (Damage: 5-20(7-30); Properties: status:freeze, charge)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - Passive: Ability: sodenoshirayuki (Sleeve of White Snow) - Enables Air Dash and gives an immunity to Freeze effect. Also recovers SP when attacking with Support Koma.
  - X — Hadounosanjuusan-Soukatsui (Way of Destruction 31-Falling Pale Fire): Same as 4 Koma Rukia's, but she jumps into the air (allowing an Air Dash, jump away, another attack, or a drop) and can charge it. Charging doesn't increase damage; instead she leaps forward until she contacts an enemy, the ground/platform, or you let go, in which case she fires automatically. Useful for diving into a group of enemies from another platform or a moderate distance. (Damage: 32(48); Properties: projectile, charge)
  - up X — Sodenoshirayuki (Sleeve of White Ice): Same as 4 Koma Rukia's except it does more damage and it can reach across the screen. (Damage: 8-24(12-36); Properties: status:freeze, charge)

- **6-koma — knowledge:** HP 160.
  - Passive: Ability: sodenoshirayuki (Sleeve of White Snow) - Enables Air Dash and gives an immunity to Freeze effect. Also recovers SP when attacking with Support Koma.
  - X — Bakudouichi-Sai (Way of Binding One-Obstruction): Rukia quickly dashes forward and paralyzes whoever's in front of her. No damage. Good Ringout move since Rukia moves and knocks them back a distance you can repeat; combos with 3 Koma Hitsugaya, and can be chained into a semi-infinite with (Forward+)B, Y, X plus SP recovery boosting Help Koma. (Damage: 0; Properties: status:paralysis, mash-extend)
  - up X — Sodenoshirayuki-Somenomai [Tsukishiro] (Sleeve of White Ice-First Dance [White Moon]): Same range as the 5 Koma version, but now the iced floor itself doesn't freeze; instead a blast upwards from the ice that follows does the freezing, and can send the enemy flying upwards. (Damage: 3-15(5-22), 4-16(6-24); Hits: 2; Properties: status:freeze, charge)

#### Combos

- (Forward+)B, Y, X, run a little, repeat - semi-infinite with 6 Koma Rukia given enough SP recovery rate Help Koma
- Activate 3 Koma Hitsugaya then instantly follow up with 6 Koma Rukia's X (paralysis dash) before Hitsugaya's attack starts, to send the opponent a good distance while still being able to Dash in before the freeze runs out
- Freeze with 3 Koma Hitsugaya, then dive in with Rukia's 5 Koma Up+X, or Dash in and follow up with 6 Koma Rukia's X
- Dash into combos, using 5 Koma Special attack (X) or Left/Right+Y (if not enough SP) as a combo ender

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide gives no explicit source 'version' number in the header/credits; only author name and email are stated.
- Section KR5 intro states 'the number in brackets are against characters weak to the nature of the attack' but KR4 states the bracketed number is 'the 50% damage boost from hitting an opponent of a weaker nature' - both describe the same bracket mechanic, listed here for completeness since phrasing differs slightly between sections.
- The Koma Evolution Chart price table (Red/Green/White gem costs for koma sizes 2-6) does not fit the schema and is omitted from structured fields.
- Compatible Koma (Health Boost) list - Abarai Renji, Kuchiki Byakuya, Kon - does not fit the schema's move/koma fields and is omitted.
- Deck-building recommendations (KR6) for Help/Support/Battle Koma partners are guide commentary/opinion, not Rukia's own moves or koma stats, and are omitted from structured fields.
- General character evaluation text in KR7 (below average health, average damage, relies on speed/Air Dash) is prose commentary not tied to a specific move stat, so it is only partially reflected via the combos list.
- 4 Koma X evaluation explicitly says it's outclassed by 5 Koma's Up+X for being 'seemingly faster, more damaging' - this cross-koma comparison/contradiction in usefulness is noted here rather than forced into a single move entry.
