### Dio — FAQ 49230

**Source:** `49230-dio.txt`; author: Draconated.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Dio uses a sideways punch, good for starting combos.
  - Damage: 10 damage to power/laughter and 15 damage to knowledge.

- **side B:** Dio shoots a vampire laser beam. Makes a good end to combos, but it's hard to connect with most; use it when an opening is seen.
  - Damage: 10 damage to power/laughter and 15 damage to knowledge; Properties: projectile.

- **up B:** Dio does a backflip kick that launches him in the air. The perfect aerial combo starter and a good dodge move; use it often.
  - Damage: 10 damage to power/laughter and 15 damage to knowledge; Properties: launcher.

- **down B:** The World does a quick sideways punch. A good combo starter.
  - Damage: 10 damage to power/laughter and 15 damage to knowledge.

- **air B:** The World punches downwards. Good move if you're just randomly falling, okay for an aerial move.
  - Damage: 10 damage to power/laughter and 15 damage to knowledge.

- **Y:** The World does a quick 1-2 punch in one attack. Strong and perfect for combos.
  - Damage: 20 damage to power/laughter and 30 damage to knowledge; Hits: 2.

- **side Y:** The World rushes forward for a sweeping punch. With great strength and distance, this is his best combo move.
  - Damage: 18 damage to power/laughter and 27 damage to knowledge.

- **up Y:** Dio jabs his hand into his opponent to steal blood. A great stand-alone move that recovers your health.
  - Damage: 14 damage to power/laughter and 21 damage to knowledge; Properties: status:heal.

- **down Y:** Dio rushes forward and turns around for The World to punch his opponent. Good strength, speed, and distance; use it often.
  - Damage: 20 damage to power/laughter and 30 damage to knowledge.

- **air Y:** Dio throws a knife at his opponent. A good aerial move; use its distance well.
  - Damage: 15 damage to power/laughter and 22 damage to knowledge; Properties: projectile.

- **select — Dio's ultimate action:** Dio pulls out a book, and if he is attacked while reading, he'll teleport behind the attacker. Not all that useful or easy to use; guide advises avoiding it.
  - Properties: counter.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma:**
  - X — Muda Muda: Dio unleashes five kicks to an opponent that is hit by the first kick. An okay combo ender but weaker than Muda Muda Muda; Dio's weakest special. (Damage: 15 damage to power/laughter and 22 damage to knowledge; Hits: 5; Properties: multi-hit)
  - X+up — Circle Knives: Dio takes out knives and teleports them around his opponent to hit them. Works well with long-distance combos, fairly good with a normal combo. The only worthwhile special of 4 Koma Dio. (Damage: 30 damage to power/laughter and 45 damage to knowledge; Properties: projectile)

- **5-koma:**
  - X — Muda Muda Muda: The World comes out and rapidly hits his opponents with his fists; tap X rapidly for more hits. Dio's best combo ender, used as a crowd clearer, ringout move, and normal combo finisher. (Damage: 19-43 damage to power/laughter and 28-64 damage to knowledge; Properties: multi-hit, mash-extend)
  - X+up — Road Roller: Dio finds a steam roller and crushes his foes with it, then bashes the roller until it explodes. A great surprise attack, pretty strong, but easy to dodge if seen coming; falls slowly and can kill the user if not careful. Also usable over a fall-through area to KO other opponents and yourself; only use it if Dio is not the last battle koma. (Damage: 40 damage to power/laughter and 60 damage to knowledge)

- **6-koma:**
  - X — Muda Muda Muda: The same as 5 Koma Dio's Muda Muda Muda except it's a tad bit weaker. Still a great attack. (Damage: 19-41 damage to power/laughter and 28-61 damage to knowledge; Properties: multi-hit, mash-extend)
  - X+up — The World: Dio and The World use their ultimate attack, ZA WARUDO! Time freezes for about 2 seconds; the opponent cannot be moved by attacks but still takes damage, and any attack the opponent was mid-execution gets cancelled if hit. Enough room for one combo during frozen time, or spam Muda Muda Muda with SP. Can run away to heal (but cannot use supports or change character while time is frozen). Can also freeze time to run out the match timer for sudden death (the time gauge does not move while frozen). Opponents can hear The World activate and block to avoid damage, so it works better in 4v4 matches where opponents are too occupied to remember to block. (Properties: status:time stop)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide never describes a down+B forced-switch attack for Dio; C4's 'B+down' entry is a standard basic attack (The World sideways punch), not a forced-switch move, so no 'down+B forced switch' move exists in this guide.
- The guide never describes a dedicated 'B string' move; only the individual B, side B, up B, down B, and air B basics are given.
- Support koma damage lines list 'power/knowledge' and 'laughter' groupings (e.g. '22 damage to power/knowledge and 33 damage to laughter' for the 2-koma support), which is the opposite grouping from every other move in the guide (which groups power/laughter vs knowledge). Copied exactly as written; likely a guide typo but not corrected here.
- Support koma sizes/shapes are described as ASCII art in the source (e.g. '[ ][ ]' and an L-shape) rather than plain text; not captured beyond size and name since the schema has no field for koma shape diagrams.
- The guide describes an 'Over twice' maneuver (a dash forward with a red aura) and an 'A' jump/double-jump input and 'A+down in air' jump-cancel input under 'Other Manuvers' in C4; these are universal engine mechanics, not Dio-specific moves, so they were left out of the moves list rather than forced into an input enum the schema doesn't cover.
- Help koma section (C6) lists five separate 'Helpful help koma for Dio' (Giorno, Jonathan, Rohan, Gyro Zeppili, Taizo, Frieza) with their own effects, but these are OTHER characters' help koma, not Dio's own help koma entry; only Dio's own help koma effect was captured in the koma array, and the recommended-others list is omitted as it does not describe Dio himself.
- No HP values are given anywhere in the guide for any koma size (battle, support, or help); all left null per instruction not to invent values.
- Guide gives no version number for this FAQ.
