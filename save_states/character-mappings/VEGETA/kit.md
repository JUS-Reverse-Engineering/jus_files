### Vegeta — FAQ 51174

**Source:** `51174-vegeta.txt`; author: AsdfYunsungfdsA; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A simple jab. The guide calls it a basic parrying attack (one that cancels an opponent's attack) and a basic combo starter.
  - Damage: 10; Properties: counter.

- **side B:** Vegeta dashes and punches. Used to charge into the fray from a distance, but has pretty bad lag afterward if it misses or is guarded.
  - Damage: 10.

- **down B:** Vegeta hammers downward with both hands, switching the opponent's character. Fast, but the guide says it's rarely used except by accident or in missions requiring a character-change.
  - Damage: 6; Properties: forced switch.

- **up B:** An upward kick that sends the opponent forward a little. Doesn't combo well with Vegeta's other moves, but allows an extended combo 'string' if you dash right after (B, up B, dash, side B, then continue a normal combo).
  - Damage: 9.

- **air B:** A 'knee' that sends the opponent upward. Not a bad aerial attack, but not too good either; his air-Y is usually a better alternative unless recovering vertically to the stage.
  - Damage: 9; Properties: launcher.

- **Y:** Two kicks that knock the opponent down. A good, strong, long-lasting Y attack and a good support comboer because of its decent 'period' of attack, but if it misses the opponent can easily knock you off or combo you.
  - Damage: 18 total; Hits: 2; Properties: knockdown.

- **side Y:** Several Ki blasts; press Y many times for more blasts (up to 6). A good support comboer and can chain from Y against a wall, though the guide says it's too easy to see coming and block.
  - Damage: 3 per blast, 6 blasts total; Hits: 6; Properties: projectile, mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Vegeta points his hand out and explodes a bit of Ki, breaking the opponent's guard. An average guard break with decent range; the guide says Vegeta isn't really a guard-breaking kind of character.
  - Damage: 19; Properties: guard break.

- **up Y:** An uppercut with a second hit if Y is pressed twice. One of Vegeta's most important moves; can connect with his Air-Y if both hits land, and can chain from Y when pinning an opponent against a wall.
  - Damage: 18 total (8 first hit, 10 second hit); Hits: 2.

- **air Y:** Vegeta lunges forward and smashes the opponent downwards through the floor. A spike, usually only good in combos since the forward lunge telegraphs it when used alone.
  - Damage: 18.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Vegeta begins charging energy, which slowly restores SP. Fairly quick but the guide says you likely won't have time to use it unless you've ringed out the opponent.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - Passive: All battle koma have the SP-gain-on-low-health ability naturally included.
  - X — Special A: Vegeta fires a large beam. Standard DBZ-style beam, pretty quick and very 'tall' compared to most beams. (Damage: 34; Properties: projectile)
  - up X — Special B: Vegeta charges and pushes out his arms, knocking back anyone within a certain radius. This attack guard breaks, and is usually the strongest special of the 4-koma (unless the opponent is pinned against a wall, where the beam does more). (Damage: 32; Properties: guard break)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - Passive: All battle koma have the SP-gain-on-low-health ability naturally included.
  - Passive: Referred to by the guide as koma 'B5a'.
  - X — Special A: Vegeta fires several Ki blasts; tap X many times for more blasts. The guide dislikes this special, saying it never gets more than 3 hits in before the opponent gets pushed too far away. (Damage: 39; Properties: projectile, mash-extend)
  - up X — Special B: Vegeta jumps into the air and fires a beam diagonally downwards. Potentially amazing if the opponent is on a platform above (up to 56 damage, 48 if on equal level), but risky if it misses due to lag afterward. (Damage: 56 (48 if opponent is on equal level); Properties: projectile)

- **5-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: All battle koma have the SP-gain-on-low-health ability naturally included.
  - Passive: Referred to by the guide as koma 'B5b'; unlocked via an alternate path in Kaiou-sama's chart.
  - Passive: The guide describes Vegeta as 'primarily power based except for his B5b koma which is knowledge'.

- **6-koma — power:**
  - Passive: All battle koma have the SP-gain-on-low-health ability naturally included.
  - X — Special A: Vegeta fires a small ball of Ki that explodes. Good special; strong but with small range and large start and end lag. (Damage: 52; Properties: projectile)
  - up X — Final Flash: Vegeta 'explodes'. A strong special, but the guide advises against using it because it's too slow and costly. (Damage: 80 but reduces health to 1; Properties: charge)

#### Combos

- Combo 1: B >B /\Y (with the second hit) Aerial Y. Fast and hits for decent damage, with potential ringout ability.
- Combo 2: B /\B >Y (all hits of >Y if possible). Unexpected because /\B usually hits people out of combo range; with all hits of >Y the combo can inflict decent damage.
- Combo 3: B >B Y. Knocks the opponent down; near the edge, a second Y disrupts the opponent's jump back to the stage for a likely RO.
- Combo 4: B /\B dash forward then Combo 1 or 3 (excluding the first B).
- Combo 5: B /\B X (Koma 6 only). A short, simple special combo, decent damage with few hits.
- Combo 6: B /\B /\X or X (/\X is 5-Komas only, X is for any Koma). Knocks the opponent slightly up and forward then sends them down with a beam; vulnerable after the Up X.
- Support combo 1 (Allen 2): R and a split second later Combo 4. The first hits of combo 4 knock the opponent right in front of Allen as he shoots his 5 laser beams, all 5 hits connect for 30 damage.
- Support combo 2 (Trunks 3): Same as Support combo 1 except perform /\B before Trunks fires his Burning attack at the end.
- Support combo 3 (Gotenks 2/3 or Hyoga 3): /\B, R, *wait*, Combo 1. Very good if the opponent is against a wall.
- Support combo (Joseph 2 or Shishio 2): B >B Y R /\X or X. The /\X is for 4 and 5 komas and the X is 4 or 6 koma.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- chr_b ambiguity: the mapping table lists chr_b 2 = 'Vegeta' (base) and chr_b 3 = 'Vegeta (SSJ)'. The guide never distinguishes an SSJ form and describes koma 4, 5a (power), 5b (knowledge), and 6 all as one continuous 'Vegeta' evolution chart, so chr_b 2 was used for the whole file. It's possible the guide's higher koma (e.g. 6-koma, which learns 'Final Flash') actually corresponds in-game to the SSJ chr_b row, but the guide gives no naming cue to confirm this.
- No HP values are given anywhere in the guide for any koma size (4/5a/5b/6); the guide only states help koma and battle koma share an SP-gain-on-low-health passive.
- The 5b-koma (knowledge alignment, unlocked via Kaiou-sama's alternate path) is mentioned only in the deck-building section ('primarily power based except for his B5b koma which is knowledge') and the evolution chart; the guide gives no special-move data for this koma at all, so its specials list is empty.
- Support koma S2 and S3 are described only as reusing Special A of koma 4 and Special B of koma 5 respectively, with slightly different damage numbers (26 and 36 vs. 34 and 56 for the battle specials); guide gives no further detail (no nature, no additional effect text) for these two support koma.
- Deck-building section (V3) recommends other characters (Luffy, Kinnikuman, Piccolo, Trunks, Sasuke, Kakashi, Allen, Gotenks, Hyoga, Joseph, Shishio, Sakura, Yoh, Naruto, Seiya) as team synergy partners; these describe other characters' effects, not Vegeta's own moves, so they were omitted except where a support combo explicitly used Vegeta's own inputs (captured under 'combos').
- The guide's Index (V1-V6) never actually surfaces a separate section for 'Koma effects' beyond the single sentence about the help koma and the battle koma SP-gain passive; captured as-is.
