### Neuro — FAQ 46537

**Source:** `46537-neuro.txt`; author: puTTytHiEF09.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Neutral B:** Neuro snaps his fingers, sending the opponent upward. A good combo starter or ring-out setup with his up-Y attack.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 10; Laughter: 15; Properties: launcher.

- **side B — Forward B:** Neuro slips into his true form and fires a rifle at the opponent; not a great move, but chains easily into his forward Y.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 10; Laughter: 15.

- **up B — Up B:** Neuro turns his arm into a chainsaw, which can hit opponents on ledges above due to Neuro's height and the chainsaw's length. Leaves Neuro facing the opposite direction, making it a good combo-ender.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 1 damage per hit, 10 in total; Laughter: 1-2 damage per hit, 15 in total; Hits: 10; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down B — Down B:** Neuro causes a speech bubble to appear while asking a question; opponents hit by it are forced to switch characters, like all characters' down B attacks.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 10; Laughter: 15; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Aerial B:** Neuro turns part of his hair into a three-pronged spear end. Not that great a move compared to his aerial Y, best used against opponents who spend most of their time evading.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 10; Laughter: 15.

- **Y — Neutral Y:** Neuro swings Yako around like a club. One of his best moves: good damage, combos easily into his neutral X super and certain support koma, and knocks the opponent back a decent distance.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 20; Laughter: 30.

- **side Y — Forward Y:** Neuro brings out Yako to have her hair try to cover the opponent; if the initial hit connects, the hair squeezes the opponent, and pressing Y rapidly extends the number of squeeze hits before the opponent is thrown to the other side of Neuro. Probably his best normal attack, since the opponent stays vulnerable to other attacks and status effects during the squeeze.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 15-25; Laughter: 22-37; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **up Y — Up Y:** Neuro leaps from the ground and attempts to grab an opponent. Best used for ring outs, since it's hard for opponents to recover before getting back up; the lower the area, the better. Can also set opponents up into traveling support koma like Kuwabara's 2-koma.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 20; Laughter: 30.

- **down Y — Down Y:** Neuro brings out Yako again, causing her to shoot lasers out of her eyes. Breaks an opponent's guard, like all characters' down Y moves; otherwise not great.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 20; Laughter: 30; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Aerial Y:** Neuro slips into his regular form and coughs up one of his 777 Underworld Tools: Evil Friday. Once it hits the ground, it breaks into two eyeballs that crawl the floor in each direction until they hit an opponent/object or a few seconds pass; the eyeballs will not go off a ledge. If an opponent is hit by the ball before it lands, they are stunned for a second or two. A very good move overall, especially in arenas with little to no ledges.
  - Damage: Power/Knowledge: 13 per crawling eye hit; Laughter: 19-20 per crawling eyeball hit; Properties: status:stun.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 152.
  - Passive: SP slowly drains away
  - Passive: Immunity to Confusion
  - Passive: Immunity to Stun
  - Passive: Gain SP by attacking characters or defending against characters that have the opposite nature
  - X — You, you are the killer!: Yako suddenly appears with Neuro in his regular form right beside her. Yako and Neuro then point in the direction they are facing, causing a purple aura to come out of her fingertip in a straight, narrow fashion. It only goes a short distance (less than half the player's screen), and the aura never leaves her fingertip. (Damage: Power/Knowledge: 3 hits, 10 per hit, 30 total; Laughter: 3 hits, 15 per hit, 45 total; Hits: 3)
  - up X — Ejiki: A monster (in reality Shirota, the chef who injects himself with the doping soup in the middle of chapter 6) appears behind Neuro and charges at him. Neuro quickly dodges the charge and then Shirota jumps forward and slams down on the ground. (Damage: Power/Laughter: 40; Knowledge: 60)

- **4-koma — power:** HP 152.
  - Passive: SP slowly drains away
  - Passive: Immunity to Confusion
  - Passive: Immunity to Stun
  - Passive: Gain SP by attacking characters or defending against characters that have the opposite nature
  - X — You, you are the killer!: Text identical to the 4-koma (Knowledge) entry: Yako suddenly appears with Neuro in his regular form right beside her, pointing to create a purple aura. Guide gives no koma-specific difference for this version. (Damage: Power/Knowledge: 3 hits, 10 per hit, 30 total; Laughter: 3 hits, 15 per hit, 45 total; Hits: 3)
  - up X — Ejiki: Text identical to the 4-koma (Knowledge) entry: a monster (Shirota) appears behind Neuro and charges, Neuro dodges, Shirota slams down on the ground. (Damage: Power/Laughter: 40; Knowledge: 60)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 168.
  - Passive: SP slowly drains away
  - Passive: Immunity to Confusion
  - Passive: Immunity to Stun
  - Passive: Gain SP by attacking characters or defending against characters that have the opposite nature
  - X — You, you are the killer!: More or less the same as the 4-koma neutral X special, but the aura travels a good amount further and is much wider, making it easier to combo into. (Damage: Power/Knowledge: 5 hits, 5 damage per hit for four hits and 15 on the final hit, 35 total; Laughter: 5 hits, two hits for 7, two hits for 8, and 22 on the final hit, 52 total; Hits: 5; Properties: multi-hit)
  - up X — Hitorikii: Yako appears in an evening gown and starts singing. Anyone hit in the radius around Neuro while Yako is singing is afflicted with the Confusion status effect. (Damage: 0; Properties: status:confusion)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- No SELECT Ultimate Action or B-string move is described anywhere in the guide.
- The 4-koma (Power) battle koma section repeats the exact same Neutral X ('You, you are the killer!') and Up X ('Ejiki') text, damage, and health (152) as the 4-koma (Knowledge) section verbatim; the guide gives no koma-specific differences between the two 4-koma variants despite listing them as separate entries.
- Only one 5-koma variant (Knowledge) is described, even though the evolution chart in section 4 lists '5: 5-koma(battle)' without specifying nature, and the 'Which Battle Koma To Use?' comparison discusses '4 and 5 komas' generally; no 5-koma (Power) entry appears.
- Section 5 opens with a note that for Neuro's 4-koma power nature, the knowledge and laughter damage keys should be swapped ('Neuro will cause the most damage to knowledge characters, so switch the knowledge and the laughter damage key for that specific koma'); this note is not reflected anywhere in the per-move damage numbers themselves, which are only labeled Power/Knowledge vs Laughter throughout section 5.
- The support koma section (6) does not state a nature for the 2-koma or 3-koma, unlike the battle koma section which specifies nature per koma.
- Guide text has typos/garbled spots (e.g. 'Nuetral X', 'thier', 'availible', 'excat', 'sythe', 'radious') left as-is in quotes where directly cited.
