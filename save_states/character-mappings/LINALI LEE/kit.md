### Lenalee Lee — FAQ 53025

**Source:** `53025-lenalee-wong.txt`; author: Wong Yeang Chiong; version 2.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Reverse Heel Kick:** A basic ground attack; Lenalee does a Reverse Heel Kick on the enemy.
  - Damage: 7 (normal), 10 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **Y — Flying Kick (Heavy):** A flying kick that advances toward the opponent, near to mid range; can be used to move toward the opponent while damaging them, but it penetrates the enemy, causing Lenalee to fly past them.
  - Damage: 15 (normal), 22 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **other — Dash:** Tap left or right twice to dash from one point to another quickly on the ground; if an opponent is in the way, Lenalee is slowed until passing through them, sometimes causing a double-dash.
  - Damage: 0; Hits: 0.

- **select — Super Jump:** A high jump that takes a while to start; Lenalee can jump up to 2 platforms high. Consumes one of her jumps.
  - Damage: 0; Hits: 0.

- **down B — Enbu Kirikaze (Minor):** A pretty slow ground move; a successful strike leaves the opponent vulnerable to the next attack. Guide suggests summoning a Support Koma via hotkey right after landing this.
  - Damage: 7 (normal), 10 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **side B — Flying Kick (Minor):** Unlike the heavy version, this causes Lenalee to stop right in front of the target on a landed hit, allowing a follow-up attack, Support Koma use, or retreat. Can be used for a normal combo after a B attack.
  - Damage: 6 (normal), 9 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **up B — Crescent Kick:** Can be comboed from the basic B attack; pushes the opponent slightly further to allow a follow-up move.
  - Damage: 8 (normal), 12 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **down Y — Advancing Crescent Kick:** Useful for kicking away item boxes/items toward the opponent; requires correct distance to avoid consuming the item instead. This move can Guard Break.
  - Damage: 14 (normal), 21 (laughter); Hits: 1; Properties: guard break.

- **side Y — Whirlwind Kick:** Ground-to-aerial move; repeatedly pressing Y continues the attack for a mashable combo. Useful for ringing out opponents or escaping; a 3-hit combo does 15 damage (normal) by repeated Y presses, and a successful combo can send the opponent up a platform, evolving into an aerial attack.
  - Damage: 2 to 13 (normal), 3 to 21 (laughter); a 3-hit chain does 15 (normal); Hits: 1 to 5; Properties: mash-extend, launcher.

- **up Y — Reversed Crescent Kick:** Useful for extending a combo; can be followed with a B attack. Pushes the opponent down, useful for edge ring outs.
  - Damage: 15 to 23 (normal), 22 to 34 (laughter); Hits: 1 to 2.

- **other — Quick Landing:** Aerial move (Down + A) that lands Lenalee faster; useful for escaping mid-air pressure, but she may land right in front of the enemy so blocking after landing is advised.
  - Damage: 0; Hits: 0.

- **other — Air Dash:** Tap left or right twice in mid-air to dash, passing through the opponent (unlike the ground dash). Useful for escaping charged attacks.
  - Damage: 0; Hits: 0.

- **air B — Aerial Crescent Kick:** Best used near the edge of the arena against an opponent who can be ring-outed; quick move.
  - Damage: 8 (normal), 12 (laughter); Hits: 1.

- **air Y — Aerial Advancing Crescent Kick:** Can ascend or descend (with up or down directional input) or advance toward the opponent while kicking; sends the opponent crashing down. Useful near the edge of the map for maximum advantage, for clearing a path, for escaping a ring out with a final jump, or for stopping opponents from jumping around when descending from a higher platform.
  - Damage: 14 (normal), 21 (laughter); Hits: 1.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - X — Enbu Kirikaze: Lenalee kicks out a whirlwind conjured by her Dark Boots, sending caught opponents flying. Useful for pushing opponents away or saving herself from a ring out near the edge; also good for punishing opponents trying to jump back onto the arena. (Damage: 2 to 36 (normal), 3 to 54 (laughter); Hits: 1 to 6; Properties: knockback)
  - up X — Enbu Kuroshizuku: Lenalee performs a forward somersault before striking downward toward the ground, damaging and pushing opponents in her way; she descends all the way to the ground. Useful for pushing opponents away or scoring a ring out at the edge, but risky since a bad estimation can ring the user out instead. (Damage: 2 to 28 (normal), 3 to 42 (laughter); Hits: 1 to 2; Properties: ground to aerial to ground)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - X — Onkyou no Tougi: Otakase: Dashing forward, Lenalee starts a combo with a swift kick to the face before going aerial on the opponent. Especially useful if the opponent is at the edge of the arena; can also be used to escape a tight situation. The final attack kicks the opponent down and, used outside the arena, is an instant ring out. (Damage: 2 to 42 (normal), 3 to 63 (laughter); Hits: up to 4; Properties: ground to aerial)
  - up X — Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase: Like Enbu Kuroshizuku but much more powerful. Lenalee jumps straight up vertically before striking downward vertically, crashing into enemies below her. The maximum damage comes not from a direct attack but from blades conjured on the sides of her Dark Boots as she strikes down, so the user should position slightly further than usual and on a platform above the opponent. (Damage: 2 to 46 (normal), 3 to 69 (laughter); Hits: 1; Properties: ground to aerial to ground)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide's damage-analysis legend defines 'Normal' as the damage dealt to Power and Knowledge type characters, and 'Laughter' as the (1.5x) damage dealt to Laughter type opponents, since Lenalee is Knowledge type; damage fields in this file use the guide's own 'Normal'/'Laughter' labels rather than schema's knowledge/laughter/power labels used in other files, to avoid inventing numbers not stated.
- The guide does not label any move with 'name: null' inputs as belonging to a specific koma size beyond the general 'Basic Moves' section (Part 1: The Moves) that applies to all of Lenalee's battle komas; it is unclear whether Basic/Advance/Aerial moves differ between her 4-koma and 5-koma forms, but the guide presents them once, before discussing the 4 and 5 Koma specials, implying they are shared.
- The guide's Dash, Super Jump, Quick Landing, and Air Dash entries are traversal/utility moves with 0 damage and no attack input in the schema's move-input vocabulary; input was set to 'other' for these.
- Guide never gives Lenalee's Help/Support koma's own HP; that field is left null for those koma entries per schema (HP is only for battle koma).

### Lenalee — FAQ 49273

**Source:** `49273-lenalee-vicevirtuoso.txt`; author: Vicevirtuoso; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Spinning Heel Kick:** Lenalee does a high, sweeping heel kick. A standard combo starter that combos into Up-B, Forward-B, Forward-Y, and 4-Koma Up+X.
  - Damage: 7/10.

- **side B — Dashing Side Kick:** Lenalee dashes forward with a jumping side kick. A great poking move with nice range, but doesn't combo into anything outside of a quick support.
  - Damage: 6/9.

- **up B — Crescent Axe Kick:** Lenalee does a spinning overhead kick. Used for close-range poking or combos, typically the second attack in combos. Unlike many other Up-B attacks, this does not launch the foe. Combos into Up-Y, Forward-Y, and 4-Koma Up-X.
  - Damage: 8/12.

- **down B — Gust Side Kick:** Lenalee charges up for a split second, then does a side kick powered with a small burst of wind. Forces the opponent to switch characters on hit, if possible. Excellent with a support koma that inflicts the Battle Seal status. Also combos into Forward-Y.
  - Damage: 7/10; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y — Dropkick:** Lenalee backflips in place, then thrusts forward with both feet. Cannot be comboed into outside of supports, puts Lenalee in an airborne state, and is slow. Near the edge, combos into Air-B for a guaranteed ring-out.
  - Damage: 15/22.

- **side Y — Rising Cyclone Kicks:** Tatsumaki Senpuu-Kyaku! Lenalee's signature move. Puts Lenalee in an airborne state; each kick makes her rise higher in the air. First hit launches opponent. Keep pressing Y for up to 5 hits. Her main combo tool, ending with Air-Y or occasionally Air-B for a ringout.
  - Damage: 2/3 (first four hits), 5/7 (last hit), 13/19 total; Hits: up to 5; Properties: launcher, multi-hit, mash-extend.

- **up Y — Somersault Kick:** Lenalee backflips into the air with a kick, moving up roughly 2/3 of a jump height and launching the opponent at roughly the same height. Excellent move that can be easily comboed into up close, then followed up with Air-B for a good combo.
  - Damage: 15/22; Properties: launcher.

- **down Y — Frontflip Axe Kick:** Lenalee crouches and charges her power, then flips forward with a slow kick, breaking the opponent's guard on hit if they are guarding. Not the best guard break; will whiff if the opponent is too close.
  - Damage: 14/21; Properties: guard break, charge.

- **air B — Aerial Axe Kick:** Lenalee flips in the air with a kick. Good aerial attack that knocks the enemy downwards when hit in the air; good ringout move and a preferred air combo ender because of its speed compared to Air-Y.
  - Damage: 8/12; Properties: knockdown.

- **air Y — Dash Aerial Axe Kick:** Lenalee dashes forward with a kick, knocking the enemy down on the ground and spiking the enemy in the air. Angle of dash can be controlled with Up or Down. A combo ender for most combos; outside of supports it will not actually combo, but is quite difficult to escape.
  - Damage: 14/21; Properties: knockdown.

- **other — Triple Spinning Kick (Touch Screen / Dream Combo Attack):** Lenalee does three of her Forward-Y kicks, but stays on the ground. Cannot be comboed into, and cannot be comboed out of except for other characters' Dream Combo attacks. Activated by touching Lenalee's koma when she is on the field.
  - Damage: 6/9.

- **select — Super Jump:** Lenalee crouches down, then jumps at a height slightly lower than the maximum triple jump height. After this super jump, she can still perform two air actions (airjump, airdash, or attack). Great for putting distance between her and opponents.
  - Damage: (N/A).

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Boosts: All from D.Gray-man: Kanda (samurai bishie), Allen (white haired main character), Komui (guy with glasses).
  - Passive: Ability: Dark Boots (allows for triple jump and air dash)
  - X — Enbu: Kirikaze <Waltz: Misty Wind>: Lenalee kicks so hard that she creates a horizontal wind tunnel in front of her that hits enemies caught in it up to five times. Slow and does not combo without support, but good for edge-guarding; hits opponents hanging from a ledge. (Damage: 36/54; Properties: knockdown)
  - up X — Enbu: Kuroshizuku <Waltz: Black Droplets>: Lenalee flips forward (the flip can hit for a tiny bit of damage), then falls forward with a vertical split kick surrounded by a purple aura. Her only special that can be comboed without support, but the damage sucks (reduced damage on an air hit). When falling, Lenalee does not stop until she hits the ground floor, and will fall through and die if the ground floor is a platform and not a solid floor. Lenalee is airborne for the duration of the move. (Damage: 2/3 (flip), 28/42 (ground hit), 20/30 (air hit) (all assuming the attack is from the same elevation as opponent); Properties: launcher)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - Passive: Boosts: All from D.Gray-man: Kanda (samurai bishie), Allen (white haired main character), Komui (guy with glasses).
  - Passive: Ability: Dark Boots (allows for triple jump and air dash)
  - X — Onkyou no Tougi: Otokase <Sound Stepping Skill: Sound Shackles>: Lenalee backflips, pushes off of a wall made of sound waves, then dashes forward with a kick. If the kick connects, she then performs another three dashing kicks. Good damage, but cannot be comboed into and is easy to see coming. Lenalee is airborne for the duration of the move. (Damage: 40/60 (3 hits), 42/69 (4 hits); Hits: 3 or 4; Properties: launcher)
  - up X — Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase <Falling Stepping Skill: Iron Shackles>: Lenalee floats up into the air, then falls to the ground floor, through any platforms. A slower, suckier version of her 4-Koma Up-X: for maybe 3 or 6 more damage, it is much slower, doesn't move forward, and has a tinier hitbox. Lenalee is airborne for the duration of the move. (Damage: 2/3 (rising), 30/45 (falling) (all assuming the attack is from the same elevation as opponent); Properties: launcher)

#### Combos

- 1. Neutral-B > Forward-B. Damage: 13/19. Range: Moderate-Far.
- 2. Neutral-B > Up-B > Up-Y > Air-B. Damage: 38/57. Range: Close. A superb combo, all 4 hits combo correctly.
- 3. Neutral-B > Forward-Y > Air-Y. Damage: depends on distance. Range: Moderate-Far.
- 4. Neutral-B > Up-B > Forward-Y > Air-Y. Damage: 28/42 (combo), 14/21 (Air-Y, which doesn't exactly combo). Range: Close.
- 5. Neutral-B > Forward-Y (2 hits) > Air-B. Damage: 22/33. Range: Moderate-Close.
- 6. Down-B > Forward-Y > Air-Y. Damage: 20/30 (combo), 14/21 (Air-Y). Range: Moderate. Other: Switches opponent's character. Forced-switch combo.
- 7. Neutral-Y > Air-B. Damage: 23/34. Range: Far.
- 8. Neutral-B > Up-X (4 Koma only). Damage: 9/13 (combo), 20/30 (falling hit). Range: Close. Only way to combo into a special without support.
- Trunks (Dragon Ball) 3 Koma combo: Neutral-B > Up-B > Up-Y > Call Trunks + Air-B simultaneously. Done correctly, it's 12 hits for an impressive 69/103 damage.
- Gyro Zeppelli (JoJo's Bizarre Adventure) 3 Koma combo: Neutral-B > Up-B > Call Gyro > Forward Y.
- Ichigo (Bleach) 2 Koma combo: Neutral-B > Call Ichigo > Up-B > Forward-Y.
- Fuji (Prince of Tennis) 2 Koma combo: Call Fuji + Neutral-B simultaneously > Up-B > Up-Y > Air-Y.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide states damage in an X/Y format where X is damage to Power and Knowledge characters and Y is damage to Laughter characters; this is preserved verbatim in each damage field rather than split, per the schema's 'copy as written' rule.
- The guide explicitly says Lenalee has 'No alternate-alignment battle koma' and only one nature (Knowledge) across both battle koma, unlike many other characters who have a Power/Knowledge/Laughter split across koma.
- Guide credits itself as having made up all normal move names ('I made up all of the names for the normals, by the way'), so 'name' fields for normals are guide-invented, not official.
- 3-Koma Support and 2-Koma Support koma shapes/graphics text include flavor quotes (e.g. 'Welcome home, Allen-kun') that were omitted from JSON as non-mechanical flavor text.
- Deck-building recommendations (Allen Walker, Train Heartnet, Don Patch, Yuu Kanda, Sena, Trunks, Gyro Zeppelli, Gintoki, Ichigo, Sanji, Fuji, Light Yagami) describe OTHER characters and team synergy, not Lenalee's own moves/koma, so only combo lines that name a Lenalee input sequence were captured under 'combos'.
