### Kenshiro — FAQ 46478

**Source:** `46478-kenshiro.txt`; author: Demon-Inuyasha; version 1.1.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Jab:** A plain jab, can be used for starting combos.
  - Damage: 9 (Power) / 9 (Laughter) / 13 (Knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **side B — Forward Jab:** Kenshiro slides forward and throws a punch; can be used for combos.
  - Damage: 10 (Power) / 10 (Laughter) / 15 (Knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **up B — Lift Kick:** Kenshiro quickly kicks upwards, sending the opponent airborne. Great for juggling and combos.
  - Damage: 10 (Power) / 10 (Laughter) / 15 (Knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: launcher.

- **down B — Blue Aura:** Kenshiro surrounds himself with a blue aura, causing the opponent to switch character.
  - Damage: 10 (Power) / 10 (Laughter) / 15 (Knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Karate Chop:** Kenshiro does a quick chop in the air.
  - Damage: 10 (Power) / 10 (Laughter) / 15 (Knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **Y — Blue Aura:** Kenshiro stops and surrounds himself with a blue aura, hitting anything near, and increases the amount of hits for his Y attacks.
  - Damage: 15 (Power) / 15 (Laughter) / 22 (Knowledge); Hits: 1.

- **side Y — Rapid Kick:** Kenshiro slides forward a little and kicks rapidly in front of him; repeatedly tap Y for more hits. Great range and good offense move.
  - Damage: 16 (Power) / 16 (Laughter) / 24 (Knowledge); Hits: 13; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **up Y — Nun-Chuck Beatdown:** Kenshiro gets out a pair of Nun-Chucks and swings them around his body; repeatedly tap Y for more hits. Great for people over you on platforms and great for juggling for combos.
  - Damage: 17 (Power) / 17 (Laughter) / 25 (Knowledge); Hits: 14; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **down Y — Gakuchu:** Kenshiro points his finger and, if it hits, breaks the opponent's guard.
  - Damage: 20 (Power) / 20 (Laughter) / 30 (Knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Rapid Kick Aerial:** Kenshiro rapidly kicks below him at an angle; repeatedly tap Y for more hits. Great pressure move and for edgeguarding since it has a great spike effect.
  - Damage: 14 (Power) / 14 (Laughter) / 21 (Knowledge); Hits: 7; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Kenshiro stops and points ahead saying "Omae wa mo shindeiru", and the opponent will stop for a brief second.

#### Combos

- COMBO #1: up+B to send opponent in the air, then up+Y (repeat for more hits), then before up+Y finishes, use a special with a big hit box to connect. Recommended specials per koma: 4 Koma Special A (can open with Special B first for more damage); 5 Koma Special A or B; 6 Koma Special B only (Special A has slow start-up); 7 Koma Special A (tap up+Y once, skip extra hits, then Special A due to slow start-up); 8 Koma Special A (same approach as 7 Koma due to huge start-up time). Advanced (4,5,6 koma only): summon a support during the special hit portion, e.g. Hitsugaya to freeze and repeat the combo.
- COMBO #2: B into up+B, or just up+B, then quickly neutral Y (counts as a hit), then Forward+Y repeated for kicks. Works with any Kenshiro version.
- COMBO #3: neutral+B then quickly forward+B (2 quick punches), then forward+Y kicks to finish. Advanced (4 koma only): finish with Special Attack A instead of forward+Y. Advanced (5 koma only): finish with Special Attack B instead of forward+Y.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide lists two different 'Down+B' moves: one at line 91 ('Blue Aura', causing opponent to switch character) and another at line 143 ('Gakuchu', breaks guard). Given the guide's own pattern (neutral/forward/up/down/air for B, then neutral/forward/up/[down]/air for Y), the second 'Down+B' almost certainly should be 'Down+Y' -- it appears between Up+Y and Jump+Y in the move list and no down+Y is otherwise listed. Recorded here as 'down Y' but the guide's own text literally says 'Down+B' for Gakuchu; this is a contradiction in the source, not a typo we corrected silently.
- The Table of Contents lists section order as Help/Support Koma before Battle Koma, and section 8 is 'Combos' -- both present and consistent, no other TOC mismatches found.
- Special Attack B entries for 4, 6 (Hiko-Jinchukyoku has damage/hits so not applicable), and 7 Koma ('Hokuto Shin Ken Tenryu Kokyu Ho' and 'Tomo no Saoku') have no stated damage or hit count in the guide -- left null per schema rather than inferring 'status-only' moves have zero damage.
- The guide's general 'Passive Effect' (less damage from specials, gain SP when blocking) is stated once under the '7. Battle Koma' section header before any koma-specific entries; it isn't explicitly repeated per koma. We applied it to all five battle koma (4-8) since the guide gives no indication it's koma-specific and no koma states a different passive.
- No standalone 'B string' move is described; combo write-ups (e.g. Combo #3 chaining neutral+B into forward+B) are guide-described sequences, not a discrete 'B string' input, so no such move entry was created.
- Guide credits another author (Genroh) for 'amazing faq with tons of info which I got from' -- suggests this guide's data may partially derive from a second source guide (46478 may overlap with a separate Genroh Kenshiro FAQ), but this guide gives no further detail to resolve any resulting discrepancies.
