### Sakura — FAQ 46574

**Source:** `46574-sakura.txt`; author: SMW; version 2.1.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **up B:** Sakura sends a kunai in a diagonal upward direction.
  - Damage: 8/12.

- **B:** Sakura punches forward.
  - Damage: 9/13.

- **side B:** Sakura throws a shuriken forward.
  - Damage: 7/10; Properties: projectile.

- **down B:** Sakura charges up then punches in an up to down arc.
  - Damage: 12/18; Properties: charge.

- **air B:** Sakura does a heal kick.
  - Damage: 10/15.

- **up Y:** Sakura does a 360 spinning kick that sends the opponent into the air. Does 3 hits.
  - Damage: 14/21; Hits: 3; Properties: launcher, multi-hit.

- **Y:** Sakura punches downwards. Careful: the attack sends you forward and may cause you to jump off the cliff if too close to the edge.
  - Damage: 18/27.

- **side Y:** Sakura throws 1 shuriken then 3 shuriken while pushing backwards.
  - Damage: 3-12/4-18; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Picks up a rock and throws it forwards.
  - Damage: 18/27; Properties: projectile.

- **air Y:** Sakura comes crashing down with a punch. Damage is still given if the dust hits. If you miss ground, you will die.
  - Damage: 10/15; Properties: knockdown.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Uses a Substitution Jutsu: if hit while active, a log replaces you and you're free to attack. Takes 1 second to activate, and you're open to attack while activating. Stays active for 2 seconds. If caught in a multi-hit, you can still be hit; the first punches activate the log, but if not dodged, the rest can still hit you. Hold a direction on the D-pad when hit to dodge the rest of the attack and end up in that direction. The UA can be used again while the first substitution is still in use, allowing repeated use and near-immunity while pressing select.
  - Properties: counter.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Status time is reduced
  - Passive: can Wall Jump
  - X: Sakura punches the ground. The explosion after the hit also does damage (Damage: 36/54)
  - X up: Sakura makes a pink orb, dashes a few feet forward and ends attack, hitting for a combo of 3. Makes enemy confused. (Damage: 23/34; Hits: 3; Properties: status:confused)

- **5-koma — power:** HP 144.
  - Passive: Status time is reduced
  - Passive: can Wall Jump
  - X: Sakura jumps up and does a giant kick. Explosion does damage. Can commit suicide. (Damage: 44/66)
  - X up: Sakura kneels and makes a blue orb, healing about a half bar.

- **6-koma — power:** HP 160.
  - Passive: Status time is reduced
  - Passive: can Wall Jump
  - X: Sakura summons her Inner Sakura to punch high and low. Damage is the same whether the enemy is above you or at ground level. You can tap X to increase hits and damage. (Damage: 38-57/57-98; Properties: mash-extend)
  - X up: Sakura makes a blue orb, healing about a quarter of an ally's bar.

- **6-koma — laughter:** HP 160.
  - Passive: Status time is reduced
  - Passive: can Wall Jump
  - X: Sakura summons her Inner Sakura to punch high and low. Damage is the same whether the enemy is above you or at ground level. You can tap X to increase hits and damage. (Damage: 38-57/57-98; Properties: mash-extend)
  - X up: Sakura makes a blue orb, healing about a quarter of an ally's bar.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide does not describe a down+B forced-switch move for Sakura; only B, side B, up B, down B, and air B basics are given, with no forced-switch attack mentioned anywhere in the guide.
- Koma 4 has no explicit HP number given directly next to it; HP values (128,144,160) are given as a set for the 4/5/6 koma in the Battle Koma section, and the Ally Boost section separately lists per-koma HP progressions (4 Koma: 128->136->144->152->160, 5 Koma: 144->152->160->168->176, 6 Koma: 160->168->176->184->192) which confirm base HP of 128/144/160 for koma 4/5/6 respectively.
- The guide states koma 6 is Laughter type ('the fourth is a Laughter Type') but the X-up special for koma 6 is separately labeled 'Type:Knowledge' in the Specials section, so koma 6's X-up special has a different nature (Knowledge) than the koma's own listed nature (Laughter). Recorded nature at koma level as 'laughter' per the Battle Koma section, and the X-up special's own Knowledge typing is preserved in its section header context but not encoded as a separate field since schema doesn't support per-special nature override cleanly.
- Ally Boost section is somewhat garbled: 'Sakura's HP increases by eight per bonus, and if leader gains an additional 8' is an incomplete/unclear sentence about leader bonus stacking; exact mechanic unclear.
- The guide gives no separate name for most moves/specials (guide uses only input labels like 'B', 'X up', etc.), so 'name' is null throughout.
- Section numbering in table of contents (xx5a Help/Support Koma, xx5b Specials) does not match the actual section headers used later in the body (xx4: Help/Support Koma appears again reusing number 4; xx5a: Battle Koma; xx5b: Specials) — the guide's own internal numbering is inconsistent/duplicated (two 'xx4' sections).
- Guide credits multiple version contributors (numberOneRookie, MasterTurtle, Dabula) inline within move descriptions and strategy sections; these are guide commentary/tips rather than strict move data, included in quotes/support notes where directly tied to a move or support synergy.
- The 'Extra' section at the end mentions a 'sprite Glitch' with Gintoki's koma using the X up special from koma 5 or 6 — this is a glitch/exploit note, not a standard move property, so left out of the moves list and only noted here.
