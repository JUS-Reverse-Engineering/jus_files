### Goku — FAQ 47182

**Source:** `47182-goku.txt`; author: Terminus_Est90 (Azor); version 1.01.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** First hit used to open essentially every combo in the guide (e.g. 'B, forward B, Up Y, kamehameha').

- **side B — forward B:** Second hit used to extend combos after B in nearly every listed combo.

- **up B:** Used as a combo opener/extender; the strategy section says it can be used to end combos early by teleporting up and away from opponents.

- **Y:** Standard combo hit used after B strings, e.g. 'B, Up B, Y, Forward Y'.

- **side Y — forward Y:** Used to gain space/distance and keep enemies far; also useful for edge-guarding by keeping opponents from hanging on a ledge. Can be repeated (e.g. 3 times) in combos.

- **up Y:** Rising attack used mid-combo to launch/juggle the opponent before following up with a kamehameha or special; holding up while using it can teleport Goku up and away, ending combos safely.
  - Properties: launcher.

- **down Y:** Not described anywhere in the guide.

- **air Y — aerial Y:** Recommended as a first attack when jumping in, especially in sudden death.

- **air B — aerial B:** Used for edge-guarding; sends opponents into a quick knockdown/away from the ledge, though riskier if used near an occupied ledge.
  - Properties: knockdown.

- **down B:** Not described anywhere in the guide as a forced-switch attack.
  - Properties: forced switch.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Hold Select to recover SP gradually (base Goku entry under Ally Boosts/passives).

- **B string:** The guide's combos consistently chain B then forward B as the opening string before Y attacks or specials.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide's 'Goku's Evolution Chart' and 'Goku's Komas' sections use a single character label 'Goku' across the whole evolution line, but per docs/research/chr_b-Complete-Mapping.md these are actually THREE distinct chr_b rows: base Goku = chr_b 0 (db_b_01, 4-koma), Super Saiyan Goku = chr_b 1 (db_b_02, 5/6/7-koma), and Vegetto = chr_b 11 (db_b_03, 8-koma, a fusion of Goku and Vegeta, not Goku himself). This file's top-level chr_b/koma array follows the guide's own presentation (one continuous progression) rather than splitting into three files; treat the 6/7/8-koma entries as describing chr_b 1 and chr_b 11 respectively, not chr_b 0.
- The 4-koma entry lists 'Type: Laughter and Power' and gives dual damage numbers ('to power/laughter' vs 'to knowledge'), suggesting the damage varies by the koma's own nature rather than by two natures simultaneously; the guide's phrasing is ambiguous about whether 'power/laughter' means the move deals that damage when Goku's koma nature is power OR laughter, or something else. Recorded verbatim rather than resolved.
- The 5-8 koma entries state 'Type: Power' only, but damage lines still list a 'to power/laughter' figure alongside 'to knowledge' — same ambiguous nature/damage relationship as the 4-koma entry, unresolved.
- No down B, down Y, or down+B forced-switch move is described anywhere in the guide despite the schema expecting them; recorded as null/missing rather than invented.
- The guide never gives an HP value for the 2-koma or 3-koma support entries (Kamehame ha / Warp kamehame ha), only damage.
- Ally boosts and passives sections are split by form name ('Son Goku', 'Super Saiyan Son Goku', 'Vegetto') rather than by koma size, so passives/support effects/ally boosts per exact koma number (5 vs 6 vs 7) cannot be individually attributed beyond what's captured under 'Ally Boosts and passive abilities' — only the base Goku Ultimate Action ('Hold Select to Recover SP Gradually') was placed on the koma/moves entries; the SSJ passive ('Gain more SP when Health is low', 'max SP gauge increases on KO') and Vegetto passive ('Gain SP energy when blocking') are not attached to specific koma sizes in this file.
- The guide describes a 'paralysis glitch' exploit (hitting with a multi-hit move right as paralysis would land keeps the opponent paralyzed after) that doesn't map to any single move/special field in the schema; noted here only.
- Combos reference many support/help koma characters (Hanamichi Sakuragi, Shun, Trunks, Toki, Kurapica, Allen, Keigo Atobe, Inui Sadaharu, Nube, Ryota Miyagi, Usopp, Rei, Mello, Seto Kaiba, Gyro Zeppelin, Bo-bobo, Sanosuke) that are not Goku koma themselves; their effects are summarized under 'Supports' in the guide but are out of scope for this Goku-only moves/koma extraction beyond appearing in the combos list verbatim.
- FAQ section is mostly joke/non-informational Q&A (e.g. 'Does goku smell good?') with no moveset content; skipped for extraction as it does not fit the schema.
- Guide gives no separate description or numbers for 'B' and 'side B' alone outside of combo strings — description/damage for these two moves is inferred only from their consistent use as combo openers, not from a standalone move writeup; the guide never describes them in isolation.
