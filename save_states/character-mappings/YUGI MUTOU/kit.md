### Yugi — FAQ 46477

**Source:** `46477-yugi.txt`; author: BalisticGeneral/SamSnake123; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Yugi summons Celtic Guardian, which slashes in front of him with his sword.
  - Damage: 10 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 15 Damage Against Laughter Characters.

- **side B:** Yugi rolls a pair of Dungeon Dice in front of him. Hits anywhere from 1-5 times, and stuns the opponent while being attacked.
  - Damage: 2 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 3 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Hits: 1-5; Properties: multi-hit, status:stun.

- **up B:** Yugi throws down a capsule, and a monster that resembles Yugi pops out and punches upwards. If the capsule from this attack hits the opponent, it will stun them for about half a second.
  - Damage: 10 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 15 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: status:stun.

- **down B:** Yugi summons Gaia the Fierce Knight, which lunges forward. Also forces opponent's character to change.
  - Damage: 10 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 15 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B:** Yugi summons Winged Guardian of the Fortress, which shoots a ball of flames from its mouth diagonally downwards. Also knocks the opponent down.
  - Damage: 8 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 12 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: projectile, knockdown.

- **Y:** Yugi summons Buster Blader, which swings his sword and spreads fire straight forward on the ground.
  - Damage: 4-6 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 6-24 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: projectile.

- **side Y:** Yugi summons Dark Magician Girl, which shoots out a ball of energy from her staff.
  - Damage: 20 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 30 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: projectile.

- **up Y:** Yugi summons Dark Paladin, which points a ball of energy at the top of his staff upwards.
  - Damage: 20 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 30 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: projectile.

- **down Y:** Yugi summons Dark Magician, which explodes a ball of energy in front of him. Also guard breaks.
  - Damage: 20 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 30 Damage Against Laughter Characters; Properties: guard break, projectile.

- **air Y:** Yugi plays Swords of Revealing Light, which shoots downwards. Also stuns the opponent for about half a second.
  - Damage: 11-21 Damage Against Power/Knowledge Characters; 16-31 Damage Against Knowledge Characters; Properties: projectile, status:stun.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Yugi switches with another of your battle characters.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- For air Y, the guide states damage 'Against Power/Knowledge Characters' as 11-21, then immediately restates a second, higher figure of 16-31 'Against Knowledge Characters' — this is an internal contradiction (Knowledge is listed in both brackets with different numbers) and is preserved verbatim rather than resolved.
- The 5 Koma Special A description gives separate lightning and punch damage figures plus a 'connects for X' combined figure; recorded together in one damage string with hits:2, since the schema doesn't have a clean way to represent lightning+punch as two fully separate sub-hits with their own hit counts.
- Guide never states koma HP values for any of Yugi's koma.
- Sections YM4 (evolution/gem costs) are deck-building/economy content omitted per scope. YM4 also mentions a 'Question Koma (Q)' on Yugi's evolution chart that is never described anywhere else in the guide (no stats, effects, or unlock info given) — flagging this gap here.
- YM5 describes several non-Yugi character koma (Kaiba Help/Support 1/2, Jonouchi Katsuya Help, Hiroto Honda Help, Anzu Mazaki Help) as part of Yugi's 'friends' section — these are other characters' koma, not Yugi's own, so they are omitted from the koma array per scope (Yugi-only extraction), even though the guide presents them in the same section.
- YM7/YM8 (strategy commentary, e.g., recommending Daijiro Ohara from Kochi Kame as a synergy partner, or edge-guarding tips) is qualitative advice not tied to a specific move stat and is not modeled as a move.
