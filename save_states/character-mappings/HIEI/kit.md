### Hiei — FAQ 46926

**Source:** `46926-hiei.txt`; author: Adam Miller (AKA Actawesome); version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **Y — Strong Attack:** Sends a barrage of punches out, comboing up the enemy, and allowing you to do another attack.
  - Properties: multi-hit.

- **side Y — Strong left/right attack:** A quick lunge forward and flame sword attack. Does fourteen damage and is a great fast attack.
  - Damage: fourteen.

- **up Y — Strong Up Attack:** A quick glide upwards, allowing you to hit the enemy again with regular B or Y.

- **down Y — Guard Break:** A quick sword uppercut. Not the best guard break, but does some damage and looks like Hiei is doing another attack, so it lands often on Wi-Fi.
  - Properties: guard break.

- **B — Weak attack:** Quick sword attack pushing the enemy.

- **side B — Weak Side Attack:** Lunge and sword. Not a very good attack, but can work for combos.

- **up B — Weak Up Attack:** One of Hiei's worst attacks; a quick sword motion in the air. Also useful to force people off the edge to ride the Darkness of the Dragon Flame to death.

- **down B — ALMOST Guard Break:** Quick flame punch.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Hiei turns around, gaining very little SP, but if someone hits him while turned around he teleports behind them as a surprise counter.
  - Properties: counter.

- **other — Jump (A):** Guide calls this the best attack ever, but gives no further detail.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma:**
  - X: A quick purple lightning-like sword attack, lunging forward. Good for finishing combos.
  - up X: A weaker version of the 5 Koma's up+X special. Not very good except against people getting up from the edge.

- **5-koma:**
  - X: A bunch of slashes that quickly launch the enemy into the air. Also a good combo finisher. (Properties: launcher)
  - up X — Dragon of the Darkness Flame: A good, long-lasting version of Dragon of the Darkness Flame, great for hitting people off the edge or chasing them around the level. Can power Hiei up if it hits him, making his attacks purple and 'twice as strong'. (Properties: status:power-up-if-self-hit)

#### Combos

- Use regular B attacks to start and Y to end, racking up damage; finish combos with Y up followed by regular Y for massive damage.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide does not describe an 'air B' or 'air Y' move for Hiei at all; not stated as absent, just never mentioned.
- No per-koma HP values, nature, or passives are given anywhere in the guide.
- 'A - Jump' is listed under the move list alongside B/Y/Select inputs but is a jump button, not a standard special; kept as 'other' per instructions since the guide treats it as part of the moveset list.
- Help koma effects (immune to poison, immune to blindness, see invisible characters, slow SP regen while idle, boosts Kakashi/Kurama/Yusuke) are described as one combined 'help' effect without the guide clearly splitting help vs support koma sizes; support specials are explicitly tied to '2 Koma' and '3 Koma' sizes but the guide's battle koma specials section only covers 4 and 5 Koma, so the support/help koma sizes (2 and 3) are recorded separately from the battle koma sizes (4 and 5) without an explicit HP/nature stated for any of them.
- The guide spells the character's ally boosts as 'Kakashi Hatake (Naruto), Yusuke Uramashi, Kurama' powering up Hiei, seemingly the reverse pairing from the help-koma text which says Hiei's help 'boosts kakashi, Kurama, and Yusuke' -- direction of the boost (who boosts whom) is not entirely clear/consistent.
