### Killua — FAQ 46804

**Source:** `46804-killua.txt`; author: Adam Miller (AKA Actawesome); version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **Y — Strong Attack:** Sends Killua's yo-yos in a circular motion, comboing the enemy and allowing a second attack.
  - Properties: multi-hit.

- **side Y — Strong left/right attack:** Killua lunges at the enemy and claws them in the face; great for finishing combos and/or hitting people off the edge of the stage.

- **up Y — Strong Up Attack:** Sends a yo-yo in the air, hitting the enemy above, then swings back to hit the enemy in front; good for multiple targets.
  - Properties: multi-hit.

- **down Y — Guard Break:** Killua claws the enemy; a great guard break, though not the best.
  - Properties: guard break.

- **B — Weak attack:** Merely hits the enemy; good for when they're right in front of you.

- **side B — Weak Side Attack:** Throws a yo-yo in whichever direction you point; not extremely strong but usually connects.

- **up B — Weak Up Attack:** Sends the yo-yo up in a quick motion; good for quick attacks, but one of Killua's worse attacks.

- **down B — Guard Break:** Quick motion forward and lightning, stunning the enemy briefly and giving you enough time to do another special.
  - Properties: guard break, status:stun.

- **air Y:** Yo-yo attack usable in the air; controllable to swing to a person diagonal to you after the first hit.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Killua forms a power up position like he did in Greed Island, slowly but steadily increasing your special bar.

- **B string:** Best combo: side Y followed immediately by B; described as an instant kill on someone over the edge, working especially well on Naruto/One Piece pit levels.
  - Properties: knockdown.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma:**
  - X: Takes a gem quickly out of the opponent, but goes to the opposite side of the side that hits the opponent.
  - up X: Powers up Killua quickly, then makes him blind for about 5 seconds; while blind you can do massive damage, but don't attack its weak point.

- **5-koma:**
  - X: Creates clones of himself while walking forward; if someone is in the path it blasts them with lightning and they can't recover until they land. (Properties: status:lightning, knockdown)
  - up X: Quickly jumps up and shoots an energy beam down at the person below. (Properties: projectile)

#### Combos

- Left/Right+Y to hit the opponent over the edge, then air Y (yo-yo in the air) to stun them as they try to get up.
- Y rapid (yo-yo combo), then side+Y claw move, followed by B; described as a likely kill in sudden death.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide never states the koma count/size for the help koma (only its effect is described).
- Guide title/date lines list version '1.0' dated 1/04/07, but the version-history section at the end refers to it as 'Version 1.1'; source metadata above uses the '1.0' header value.
- ACT6 describes 'the yo-yo comboing technique, or Jump+Y' and 'in the air press y' as tactics text rather than a formal move entry; treated as equivalent to the air Y move above, not a separate move.
- Guide states Killua's dash is 'one of the fastest in the game, and probably THE fastest of the ones that don't teleport' but gives no numeric or mechanical detail, so it isn't captured as a move or passive.
