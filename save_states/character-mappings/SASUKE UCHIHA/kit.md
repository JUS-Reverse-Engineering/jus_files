### Sasuke — FAQ 46543

**Source:** `46543-sasuke.txt`; author: Danexedas; version 1/16/07 update.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A simple punch.
  - Damage: 7 neutral / 10 dominant; Hits: 1.

- **side B:** A kick followed by a kick from the other leg.
  - Damage: 7 neutral / 10 dominant; Hits: 2.

- **up B:** A heel kick to the air, followed by another heel kick by the other leg.
  - Damage: 7 neutral / 10 dominant; Hits: 2.

- **air B:** A spiking heel kick.
  - Damage: 7 neutral / 10 dominant; Hits: 1.

- **down B:** Waves a no-no finger and Sharingan activates. This is the forced switch attack; using it on a low-health opponent forces a character switch.
  - Damage: 8 neutral / 12 dominant; Hits: 1; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** A slash of his sword.
  - Damage: 13 neutral / 19 dominant; Hits: 1.

- **side Y:** Four flashy, rapid slashes to the opponent.
  - Damage: 15 neutral / 22 dominant; Hits: 4; Properties: multi-hit.

- **up Y:** Two flying kicks into the air.
  - Damage: 14 neutral / 21 dominant; Hits: 2.

- **air Y:** Fly downwards with sword positioned to stab target in the head. Has lag time after the attack whether it hits or misses.
  - Damage: 14 neutral / 21 dominant; Hits: 1.

- **down Y:** Grab ahold of sword, charging it with lightning before hitting the opponent.
  - Damage: 14 neutral / 21 dominant; Hits: 1; Properties: charge.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Does a seal with fingers and uses the replacement jutsu that lasts for 3-4 seconds. Upon getting hit within that time, Sasuke will be replaced with a log, unharmed. He will reappear right after, with a half-a-second invincibility effect.
  - Properties: counter.

- **B string — Basic Combo:** B, B+forward, Y, Y+forward. A good combo for Sasuke that does 8 hits total.
  - Damage: 42 neutral / 63 dominant; Hits: 8; Properties: multi-hit.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide splits the 8-koma battle piece into '8 Koma(battle) 1' (Nature: Knowledge, 4-row shape) and '8 Koma(battle) 2' (Nature: Power, 2-row shape) but lists only one shared pair of specials (Chidori and Chidori Nagashi, both marked Nature: Knowledge) after both. It is unclear whether this represents one physical 8-koma piece with two nature sides or two distinct 8-koma pieces; this JSON models it as two koma entries sharing the same specials, per the guide's ambiguous layout.
- Both '-Special Attack A(Normal X)-' and '-Special Attack B(Normal X)-' are labeled with the same input 'Normal X' for the 8-koma, which cannot both be correct since they are different moves (Chidori vs Chidori Nagashi); the combos section implies Chidori Nagashi is activated via 'Up+X(8 koma)', contradicting the 'Normal X' label given in the specials section.
- The Down+Y basic attack ('Grab ahold of sword, charging it with lightning before hitting the opponent') is distinct from the 7-koma special 'Koukana Kisote' (Up+X) despite similar flavor text about lightning/charging; the guide does not clarify if these are related or how they differ mechanically.
- The guide states 'none of Sasuke's basic ground attacks causes the opponent to fall to the ground' (Weaknesses section), yet the combo 'B, Y+up, B' is described as making 'the opponent will not only fly into the air, but will also find their face on the ground' -- a possible contradiction depending on whether Y+up counts as a basic ground attack.
- Extra note on the power 8-koma: it takes extra damage from laughter characters even though the guide says Sasuke's specials are knowledge-based and he 'has no real nature weekness' -- kept as written since it's the guide's own claim, not resolved into a single properties/nature field.
- Ultimate Action is described as full invincibility/replacement during the 3-4 second window ('unharmed... he will reappear... with a half-a-second invincibility effect'), which reads as two different durations (3-4 seconds vs half-a-second) without clarifying how they relate; kept verbatim in quote.
