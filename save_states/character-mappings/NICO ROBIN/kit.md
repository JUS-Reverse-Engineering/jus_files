### Nico Robin — FAQ 46791

**Source:** `46791-nico-robin.txt`; author: FFandMMfan; version 2.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Creates 2 extra hands from one of them, and stretches her arm out straight forward.
  - Damage: 8 to P/K and 12 to L.

- **side B:** Creates several arms from one of them, and steps forward a little. Deals multiple hits.
  - Damage: Max damage is 6 to P/K and 9 to L; Properties: multi-hit.

- **up B:** Creates a spread of arms from both sides of her, and turns around. Turns the enemy to the other side of you (meaning if they are on the right side of you, she'll move them to the left side).
  - Damage: 8 damage to P/K and 12 to L.

- **air B:** Stretches a string of arms on both sides of her. Deals multiple hits.
  - Damage: Max damage is 8 to P/K and 12 to L; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down B:** Tosses out a small flower petal that hits the ground and pushes the enemy towards you a little. Switches the opponent's battle character.
  - Damage: 8 damage to P/K and 12 to L; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Robin tosses out a small flower petal nearby, which creates a set of hands on the ground that grab a foe standing above them and slam them to the ground. Pulls foes through thin floors.
  - Damage: 16 damage to P/K and 24 to L; Properties: knockdown.

- **side Y:** Robin creates a long stream of hands that stretches forward. If she connects with a foe, she grabs them and pulls them towards her. Deals no damage when she initially grabs them, but rather when they reach her.
  - Damage: 8 damage to P/K and 12 to L.

- **up Y:** Creates a huge spread of arms above, and to the sides of her. Deals damage to foes in every direction, except for below her.
  - Damage: 14 damage to P/K and 21 to L.

- **down Y:** Throws a flower petal towards the ground, which produces a pair of hands that clap together, and guard break the enemy.
  - Damage: 15 damage to P/K and 22 to L; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y:** Produces a pair of hands on the nearest platform below her. The hands toss a pair of knives from that platform, into the air, all the way past Robin until it hits something. Can be used to attack opponents that are on the ground, while you're in the air.
  - Damage: 16 damage to P/K and 24 to L; Properties: projectile.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Robin produces a hand from the ground, sits on it, and reads a book. Slowly restores SP.
  - Properties: status:sp-restore.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Robin always has the ability to deplete a small portion of her SP gauge in order to Auto-guard enemy attacks.
  - Passive: Robin always gains an increased amount of SP energy when attacking or blocking characters of the Laughter nature.
  - X — Special Attack A: Releases a few flower petals from her hand. If they connect with an enemy, they will suffer several hits from her hands that spawn on their body. Robin cannot move while this attack is in effect, then again, your foe cannot either. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Laughter: 52, Against Power/Knowledge: 35; Properties: multi-hit)
  - X+up — Special Attack B: Creates a tower of hands around her that knocks every foe in the area into the air. Deals multiple hits, damage figures are for best possible damage. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Laughter: 42, Against Power/Knowledge: 28; Properties: launcher, multi-hit)

- **5-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Robin always has the ability to deplete a small portion of her SP gauge in order to Auto-guard enemy attacks.
  - Passive: Robin always gains an increased amount of SP energy when attacking or blocking characters of the Laughter nature.
  - X — Special Attack A: Creates a long trail of hands along the ground that sweep forward and drag anyone caught in it for a pretty long distance. Similar to her 3 Koma, but only in one direction. Unlike most other stream-like multi-hit attacks, she does LESS damage when the opponent is against a wall, so you'll want to hit them with the first hit and let them get dragged all the way for the full damage. The attack works across platforms; hands split across platforms and hit both foes, though range is usually less on the bottom platform. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Laughter: 48, Against Power/Knowledge: 32; Properties: multi-hit)
  - X+up — Special Attack B: Robin waves her arms around to create a circulating field of flower petals. Continuously hit X for more hits. Drags nearby enemies closer to the center and then fires them away at the end. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Laughter: 45, Against Power/Knowledge: 30; Properties: multi-hit, mash-extend)

- **6-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Robin always has the ability to deplete a small portion of her SP gauge in order to Auto-guard enemy attacks.
  - Passive: Robin always gains an increased amount of SP energy when attacking or blocking characters of the Laughter nature.
  - X — Special Attack A: Robin creates a wave of hands on BOTH sides of her, much like her 3 Koma Support. However, the hands are fairly spaced apart, and while the full combo deals SERIOUS damage, especially to Laughter characters, it's VERY easy to miss the opponent on a majority of the hits. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Laughter: 90, Against Power/Knowledge: 60; Properties: multi-hit)
  - X+up — Special Attack B: Spins around slowly and spreads flower petals around, that cause foes to be attacked by her hands that spawn from their bodies. Strong, and wide-spread, but can be blocked and is very easy to see coming. (Damage: Damage Output: Against Laughter: 66, Against Power/Knowledge: 43; Properties: multi-hit)

#### Combos

- B -> B+up -> Y+up = Deals 30 damage to P/K and 45 to L.
- Y+forward -> B -> B+up -> Y+up = Deals 38 to P/K and 57 to L.
- B+forward -> Y RIGHT next to a ledge = Forcing them off the edge and pushing them into the pit with Y, there is no escape if you can hit them with it. Damage doesn't matter, as it's a ringout move.
- Y on a thin platform (the kind you can jump through) = Slamming the enemy straight through the floor at a high speed.
- B+down -> Y = Deals 24 damage to P/K and 36 damage to L. Force switches the enemy and slams them to the ground. Note that her Y+down cannot combo into her Y.
- 4 Koma Only: B+down -> X = Deals 43 damage to P/K and 64 to L.
- 4 Koma Only: B -> B+forward -> X = Deals 49 damage to P/K and 73 to L
- 5 Koma Only: Y+up -> X+up (with continuous X tapping for more hits) = 43 damage to P/K and 66 to L (with maximum hits).
- 5 Koma Only: B -> B+up -> Y+up -> X+up = 58 damage to P/K and 87 to L with maximum hits.
- 5 Koma Only: B+down -> Y+up -> X+up = 50 damage to P/K and 75 to L with maximum hits. Must be performed close to the foe or the special won't hit.
- 6 Koma Only: To my knowledge, Robin has no unique support-less combos with her 6 Koma.
- Support combo, any Battle Koma: Y+Forward -> Inui 3 (launch Inui as soon as they are grabbed) = 26 Damage to P/K and 51 to L. Causes Support Seal effect, kills 2 of their SP bars and launches them backwards.
- Support combo, any Battle Koma: Y+down -> Anna 3 = 57 damage to Power, 43 to Knowledge and 50 to Laughter. Double guard breaks and inflicts Slow status.
- Support combo, 4 Koma Only: X -> Hitsugaya 3 -> X -> Hitsugaya 3 -> X -> Hitsugaya 3 -> etc (Semi-Infinite). Very simple, very effective ringout technique. Eats SP like crazy.
- Support combo, 4 Koma Only: X -> Orihime 3. Prevents foes from attacking you while you heal.
- Support combo, 4 Koma Only: X -> Zoro 3 = 71 damage to Power, 89 damage to Knowledge and 88 to Laughter. Strict hitbox and timing window.
- Support combo, 5 Koma Only: B -> B+up -> Y+up -> X+up -> Kurama 2 = Same damage as the standard 5 Koma combo, but with blindess and an SP bar removal added in.
- Support combo, 6 Koma Only: Y+down -> Lucifer 3 -> X+up = 91 damage to P/K and 136 to L.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide states 'the standard combo (B -> B+foward -> Y -> Y+forward) does NOT work with Robin, because her Y will break the combo' — a negative-combo note, not a real combo, kept out of the combos list.
- Support Koma 2 and 3 are listed without an explicit 'size' field in the koma table (guide gives them as '2' and '3' Koma but does not restate size the way Battle Koma sizes are given); size left null here though context implies 2 and 3.
- Help Koma has no explicit size given in the guide (implicitly 1, as is standard, but never stated as a number).
- Naruto 3 Koma support note: guide reports getting 131 damage from Robin 6's X+up combined with Naruto 3 due to a 'strange hitbox' overlap bug, but explicitly says this is not reliable/repeatable and shouldn't be relied on — flagged as an outlier data point, not a normal combo entry.
- Battle Koma J-Soul values (4: 128, 5: 144, 6: 160) are given but J-Soul is not part of the schema's koma object; recorded here since schema has no field for it: Koma 4 J-Soul 128, Koma 5 J-Soul 144, Koma 6 J-Soul 160.
