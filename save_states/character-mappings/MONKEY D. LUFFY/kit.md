### Monkey D. Luffy — FAQ 47359

**Source:** `47359-luffy.txt`; author: Dylan Neumann AKA Zero EXE; version 1.01.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Punch:** Luffy's basic unaided punch. Good for halting attacks and leading into combos, but has minimal range.
  - Damage: 6 (9); Hits: 1.

- **side B — Gomu Gomu no Rifle:** Luffy stretches his arm behind him and releases it in a long-range punch that combos into almost anything.
  - Damage: 6 (9); Hits: 1.

- **up B — Gomu Gomu no Stamp:** Luffy kicks his leg at a 45 degree angle to attack above enemies; a good anti-air with less lag than Pistol/Shotgun.
  - Damage: 8 (12); Hits: 1.

- **down B — Gomu Gomu no Ballon:** Luffy inflates his body to attack from both sides; not great against people, but launches items.
  - Damage: 8 (12); Hits: 1; Properties: knockdown.

- **air B — Gomu Gomu no Spear:** Luffy stretches both legs down to strike like a spear; good for hitting directly below, including through floors.
  - Damage: 8 (12); Hits: 1.

- **Y — Gomu Gomu no Gatling:** A barrage of fast punches; handy for most situations, and mashing Y increases the number of hits.
  - Damage: 15 (22); Hits: 7; Properties: knockdown, mash-extend.

- **side Y — Gomu Gomu no Bazooka:** A super strong double-palm strike after stretching both arms back; Luffy's main combo finisher and primary Ring Out tool.
  - Damage: 16 (24); Hits: 1; Properties: knockdown.

- **up Y — Gomu Gomu no Pistol/Shotgun:** Luffy punches up at a 45 degree angle to hit airborne foes; the anti-air version of Gatling, and mashing Y adds a multi-hit flail follow-up, but missing leaves Luffy wide open.
  - Damage: 15 (22); Hits: 11; Properties: knockdown, mash-extend.

- **down Y — Gomu Gomu no Axe:** Luffy kicks his leg up then brings it down for a guard-breaking impact, but it is slow and telegraphed, and the initial kick whiffs.
  - Damage: 16 (24); Hits: 1; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — Diving Headbutt:** Luffy dives from above to plant his face into the opponent at a diagonal; a fairly quick lead-in, but he can't attack immediately after landing.
  - Damage: 16 (24); Hits: 1.

- **select — Lunch Box:** Luffy's taunt, which increases his HP while held.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - neutral/forward X — Gomu Rush: Luffy nails the enemy with a Gatling, a Bullet, then two Bazookas in rapid succession. (Damage: 20, 20 (30, 30); Hits: 10, 1; Properties: knockdown)
  - up X — Gomu Gomu no Storm: Luffy kicks the opponent into the air, inflates himself, and exhales downward to follow up with a barrage of punches. (Damage: 36 (54); Hits: 23; Properties: knockdown, mash-extend)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - neutral/forward X — Gomu Gomu no Golden Rifle: Luffy performs a Rifle with a giant gold orb stuck to his arm, causing extra knockback and damage. (Damage: 40 (60); Hits: 1; Properties: knockdown)
  - up X — Gomu Gomu no Golden Peony: Luffy jumps into the air and swings the gold orb stuck to his arm around him, making melee attacks worthless against him for the duration. (Damage: 36 (54); Hits: 7; Properties: knockdown)

- **6-koma — power or laughter:**
  - neutral/forward X — Gomu Gomu no Cannon: A powerful version of Gatling, rushing ahead while punching furiously; Luffy becomes 'Afro Luffy' during this move. (Damage: 41 (62); Hits: 26; Properties: knockdown, mash-extend)
  - up X — Gomu Gomu no Flail: Luffy slacks his arm and whirls it above his head before tossing it at the target; powerful but slow and easily avoided. (Damage: 56 (74); Hits: 1; Properties: knockdown, charge)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide describes a second, separate character progression, 'Gear Second' Luffy (chr_b 18 per docs/research/chr_b-Complete-Mapping.md), presented as sections 3.A-3.D immediately after base Luffy, with its own Basic Moves (B set), a taunt ('Air Pump'), and 7/8 Koma specials. Per task instructions this file's top-level chr_b/koma/moves cover base Luffy (chr_b 12) ONLY; Gear Second content is summarized here rather than mixed into the koma/moves arrays, to avoid conflating two different chr_b entries. Gear 2 Basic Moves as written: Neutral B Punch 9 (14) x1; Forward B Gomu Gomu no JET Pistol 10 (15) x1; Upward B Gomu Gomu no JET Stamp 10 (15) x1; Downward B Gomu Gomu no Ballon 8 (12) x1 (knockdown yes, less lag than base); Aerial B Gomu Gomu no JET Spear 10 (15) x1; Neutral Y Gomu Gomu no JET Gatling 20 (30) x7 (mash-extend); Forward Y Gomu Gomu no JET Bazooka 16 (24) x1; Upward Y Gomu Gomu no JET Shotgun 16 (24) x11 (mash-extend, 'increase the number of hits and damage'); Downward Y Gomu Gomu no Axe 16 (24) x1 (same as base); Aerial Y Missile Headbutt 18 (27) x1; Taunt 'Air Pump' increases Forward+Y and Down+Y damage from 16 to 20 and makes Forward+Y a guard-breaking attack.
- Gear Second 7 Koma (J-Soul 192, Power): the guide explicitly notes 'Luffy's specials in 7 Koma are actually moves he uses while not using Gear 2.' Neutral/Forward X Gomu Gomu no Storm (Horizontal) 62 (93) x24 hits, mash-extend, knockdown yes -- risk of falling off ledges near the edge. Upward X Gomu Gomu no Volcano 63 (93) x2 hits, knockdown yes, only hits enemies directly above.
- Gear Second 8 Koma (J-Soul 208, Power): guide states these are Gear 3 moves ('Gigant' in the name); after using Gear 3, Luffy temporarily shrinks for a duration equal to how long Gear 3 was used. Neutral/Forward X Gomu Gomu no Gigant Gatling 64 (96) x28, mash-extend, knockdown yes, Luffy moves forward slowly and is vulnerable from behind. Upward X Gomu Gomu no Gigant Axe 50 (75) x2, knockdown yes, does more damage when the opponent is nearby and level with Luffy (kick-up combos into kick-down).
- The guide's section lettering is garbled/duplicated: after '4 Koma [4Kom]' content, the next header reads 'E) 6 Koma [6Kom]' but its content (Gomu Gomu no Golden Rifle / Golden Peony, J-Soul 160) is structurally the 5 Koma tier per the table of contents ('D) 5 Koma [5Kom]' is listed but its own header never appears in the body). A second 'E) 6 Koma [6Kom]' header immediately follows with the Cannon/Flail content (J-Soul 176), which matches the actual 6 Koma tier. This file treats the first mislabeled block as 5 Koma and the second as 6 Koma, per the J-Soul progression (144, 160, 176) and power-koma layouts, not per the guide's literal (duplicated) headers.
