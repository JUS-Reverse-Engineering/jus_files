### Hitsugaya — FAQ 46708

**Source:** `46708-hitsugaya.txt`; author: FFandMMfan; version 1.3.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Simple slash.
  - Damage: 9 damage to P/K and 13 to L.

- **side B:** Simple slash.
  - Damage: 9 damage to P/K and 13 to L.

- **up B:** Simple slash.
  - Damage: 9 damage to P/K and 13 to L.

- **air B:** Slashes horizontally in the air.
  - Damage: 9 Damage to P/K and 13 Damage to L.

- **down B:** Creates a shockwave below him that force switches the opponent's Battle Character. Fair range and pretty quick.
  - Damage: 18 damage to P/K and 27 to L; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Slashes downwards and creates a wave of Ice in his path. Can be charged. Charging does not increase damage, but increases how long the Ice wave stays in the air.
  - Damage: 18 Damage to P/K and 27 damage to L (with maximum hits); Hits: 2; Properties: charge.

- **side Y:** Dashes forward and slashes as he moves to create a trail of Ice in his wake. Can be charged; charging increases the dash distance. Deals up to 3 hits, but the third hit's hitbox is small so 2 hits is usual.
  - Damage: 3 hits: 14 damage to P/K and 21 to L. 2 hits: 13 damage to P/K and 19 damage to L; Hits: up to 3 (usually 2); Properties: charge, multi-hit.

- **up Y:** Jumps and slashes upwards, creating a trail of Ice as he goes. Can be charged; charging does not increase damage but increases jump height and therefore the chance of landing all 8 hits.
  - Damage: first hit: 12 damage to P/K and 18 to L. Maximum at 8 hits: 19 to P/K and 28 to L; Hits: between 1 and 8; Properties: charge, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Swings a rope with a hook on it that guard breaks the enemy.
  - Damage: 18 to P/K and 27 to L; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y:** Slashes downwards and creates an arc of Ice in the air below him. Can be charged; charging gives aerial hang-time, increases the size of the Ice wave, and increases how long it remains in the air.
  - Damage: 18 to P/K and 27 to L (maximum, both hits); Hits: 2; Properties: charge.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Hitsugaya puts his sword away, sighs, and says something within a text bubble. If either his breath or the text bubble hit an enemy, he regains some SP and they are stunned for a second.
  - Properties: status:stun.

- **B string:** B into B+forward or B+up. Both deal the same damage; very quick.
  - Damage: 18 damage to P/K and 27 to L.

- **B string:** B or B+up into Y with no charge. Only worth it if all 3 hits connect. Described as his strongest basic combo; either of the first two moves work.
  - Damage: 27 to P/K and 40 to L; Hits: 3; Properties: multi-hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Hitsugaya can always Air Dash
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Freeze
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Burn
  - X — Special Attack A: Fires a large ice beam straight ahead of him. As with most of Hitsugaya's attacks (and beams in general) the damage it does is tied to how many times it hits the enemy. Pretty much the same thing as his 3 Koma Support, but stronger. (Damage: 49 (Laughter) / 33 (Power/Knowledge); Hits: 10 (maximum); Properties: status:freeze, projectile, multi-hit)
  - X — Special Attack B: Stabs the ground and creates a shockwave of ice that freezes everything nearby. Pretty much the same as his 2 Koma Support. (Damage: 36 (Laughter) / 24 (Power/Knowledge); Hits: 1; Properties: status:freeze)

- **5-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Hitsugaya can always Air Dash
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Freeze
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Burn
  - X — Special Attack A: Creates a blast of ice that spikes up and covers a fair area both upwards and outwards. Pretty quick. (Damage: 48 (Laughter) / 32 (Power/Knowledge); Properties: status:freeze)
  - X — Special Attack B: Matsumoto, Hitsugaya's Vice-Captain, appears and creates a veil of smoke that causes Blind effect on the enemy and deals heavy damage. The veil appears where the enemy was standing when she was summoned, and has a high chance of being evaded. (Damage: 48 (Laughter) / 32 (Power/Knowledge); Properties: status:blind)

- **6-koma — knowledge:**
  - Passive: Hitsugaya can always Air Dash
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Freeze
  - Passive: Hitsugaya is Immune to Burn
  - X — Special Attack A: Hitsugaya calls out his Bankai and dashes forward to pierce his foe with a lance of ice and freeze them. Despite being his Bankai, guide calls it easily his worst Special among all his Koma: slow to appear, slow to dash forward, deals good damage, but way too easy to see coming and will be blocked. (Damage: 72 (Laughter) / 48 (Power/Knowledge); Properties: status:freeze)
  - X — Special Attack B: Same as his 5 Koma, calls out Matsumoto. Deals a bit more damage though. Still causes Blind, still easy to block. (Damage: 60 (Laughter) / 40 (Power/Knowledge); Properties: status:blind)

#### Combos

- B -> B+forward/B+up = Both deal 18 damage to P/K and 27 to L. Very quick.
- B/B+up -> Y (no charge) = Only worth it if all 3 hits connect. 27 to P/K and 40 to L. His strongest basic combo. Either of the first two moves work.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide's damage/hit note says Hitsugaya's attacks vary hugely in actual damage depending on how much of a hit connects (e.g. as low as 1 damage on a grazing hit), and the listed numbers are only the 'best possible' damage assuming every piece connects — treat listed move damage as maximums, not fixed values.
- Guide explicitly notes Y attacks appear to inflict Freeze visually on hit but do NOT actually apply Freeze status, even fully charged — this contradicts the general 'his attacks cause Freeze' framing in the Introduction section.
- Guide states the three passive effects (always Air Dash, Immune to Freeze, Immune to Burn) are innate and always on for Hitsugaya as a Battle Character; text notes it 'puzzles' the author that they can't be doubled up even though two of his Boosters' Help Koma grant duplicate immunities.
- Battle Koma HP values are never given anywhere in the guide (only J-Soul: 136/152/168 for Koma 4/5/6).
- Koma shapes for Battle Koma 4/5/6 are described only via ASCII art, not converted to a numeric grid; recorded shape here only as koma size.
- Help Koma size is not explicitly stated as '1' in the guide; inferred as 1 since it's a Help Koma (standard convention) — guide never gives an explicit size number for it.
- Support Koma 2 and 3 sizes (2 and 3) are taken from their ASCII shape diagrams (2 squares vertical, 3 squares horizontal) and their section labels ('Support Koma 2', 'Support Koma 3'), not from an explicit stated integer elsewhere.
- The Y-attack 'freeze' note under HT4 appears to directly contradict move-level Freeze status that might be assumed from other guides/sources — flagging per schema rules since Y moves are NOT tagged with status:freeze here, unlike the X specials which do inflict Freeze.
