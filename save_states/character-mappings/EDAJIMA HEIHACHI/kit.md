### Edajima — FAQ 52424

**Source:** `52424-edajima.txt`; author: ninja1357; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A simple punch, standard basic punch and combo starter with fairly high priority.
  - Damage: 7/10/15 (reduced/neutral/super effective).

- **side B — FB:** A double punch mini combo that combos easily from B; can be cut off between the two punches to mix up attack pattern.
  - Damage: 15/21/31; Hits: 2; Properties: mash-extend.

- **up B — UB:** An upward thrust with a pitchfork; will not hit most smaller-sized characters. Combos from B and knocks the opponent into the air.
  - Damage: 7/10/15; Properties: launcher.

- **down B — DB:** A self lightning strike, called Edajima's worst move; only hits if his sprite is actually touching the opponent.
  - Damage: 8/11/16.

- **air B — AB:** An airborne doggie paddle; standard aerial attack.
  - Damage: 9/12/18.

- **Y:** A one-legged kick combo, the standard multi-hitting attack; can be comboed from any B attack. Has crazy fast priority.
  - Damage: 6-15/9-21/13-31; Properties: multi-hit.

- **side Y — FY:** A flying aura-punch, called Edajima's 2nd worst attack; slow moving aura fist with heavy startup lag and bad range. Can be held to charge and released automatically; good for edge guarding.
  - Damage: 11-18/15-25/22-37; Properties: projectile, charge.

- **up Y — UY:** An uppercut that summons a tornado; comboes from any B attack (except AB) and sometimes Y against a wall; great for guard pressure and edge guarding.
  - Damage: 19/26/39; Properties: launcher, guard break.

- **down Y — DY:** Rock throw; Edajima rips out a slab of concrete and throws it. Oddly deals neutral damage even against opponents with the take-less-from-punches koma; won't hit some smaller characters while guarding.
  - Damage: 18/18/27; Properties: projectile.

- **air Y — AY:** An aerial head butt; Edajima slams his head into the ground at a 45 degree angle. Cannot be stopped once started; goes through many attacks.
  - Damage: 15/20/30.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **8-koma — power:** HP 224.
  - Passive: Principal - Gains more SP when KOing, hard to knock back.
  - X — I AM THE PRINCIPAL OF OTOKOJUKU, EDAJIMA HEIHACH-: Edajima says something in a text bubble followed by a red aura burst in the shape of himself. Strange hit box that hits above and a little to the side; small lag before the aura burst appears. (Damage: 52 neutral, 78 super effective; Properties: status:paralyze)
  - up X — Shintoumetsukyakusu Rebahimomatasuzushi: Edajima jumps up, grabs a satellite, and smashes it into the ground. Hit box lands in the center of the next screen over, which can cause accidental self-ring-out if used near the edge. (Damage: 60 neutral, 90 super effective)
