  - Damage: 9 (power/laughter), 13 (knowledge).

- **Y — spirit punch:** Description not recorded.
  - Damage: 19 (power/laughter), 28 (knowledge).

- **side Y — small spirit gun:** Description not recorded.
  - Damage: 16 (power/laughter), 24 (knowledge); Properties: projectile.

- **up Y — spirit shotgun:** Description not recorded.
  - Damage: 20 (power/laughter), 30 (knowledge); Properties: projectile.

- **down Y — headbutt:** Guard breaker.
  - Damage: 18 (power/laughter), 27 (knowledge); Properties: guard break.

- **air Y — downward spirit gun:** Description not recorded.
  - Damage: 16 (power/laughter), 24 (knowledge); Properties: projectile.

- **select — ultimate action:** Yusuke charges up (hold for a couple seconds). Y attacks will do more damage and his forward+Y attack will have more range.
  - Properties: charge.

- **B string:** Basic combo string examples given in the combos section: B, forward+B, Y; B, forward+B, Y, forward+Y; B, forward+B, up+Y; B, up+B, (aerial)B or (aerial)Y; B, forward+B, up+B, (aerial)Y.

- **other — The Almighty Yusuke Combo:** B, Y, up+Y, then a blank finisher that is usually demon Yusuke, a spirit gun (special or non-special), or a support. Most of these combos can have a spirit gun (X button) at the end.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - X — Spirit gun: Yusuke fires a quick spirit gun. (Damage: 28 (power/laughter), 42 (knowledge); Properties: projectile)
  - up+X — Super spirit punch: Yusuke uses his spirit energy to punch anyone that's foolish enough to get in the way. (Damage: 28 (power/laughter), 42 (knowledge))

- **5-koma — power:**
  - X — Spirit gun (hold X to charge): Yusuke fires a spirit gun, more powerful and chargeable. (Damage: 32-44 (power/laughter), 48-66 (knowledge); Properties: projectile, charge)
  - up+X — Super spirit punch: Yusuke uses his spirit energy to punch anyone that's in the way; this one is more powerful and chargeable. (Damage: 34 (power/laughter), 51 (knowledge); Properties: charge)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - X — Spirit gun (hold X to charge): Another Spirit gun attack, very powerful, but takes a while to fully charge. (Damage: 46-56 (power/laughter), 69-84 (knowledge); Properties: projectile, charge)
  - up+X — Demon combo: Yusuke transforms into his demon form and does a quick combo, sending your opponent flying. (Damage: 51 (power/laughter), 76 (knowledge); Properties: multi-hit, launcher)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The Basic Controls movelist table gives no damage for the down+B move in a separate line beyond the shared '9/13' row shown for B/side B/up B/down B/aerial B; the guide lists them all together with identical numbers, which may not reflect distinct per-move values (player-written table, not verified).
- 1-koma is listed as 'help' but the guide's koma section header says '--1 Koma(help)--' with no J-Soul, nature, or shape given, unlike battle komas.
- 2-koma and 3-koma support entries give no J-Soul, nature, or shape info, consistent with support/help komas generally lacking that data in this guide.
- Deck Building section (VII) mentions other characters' koma effects (Kuwabara, Genkai, Neuro) which are not Yusuke moves; excluded from moves/koma but noted here since guide discusses them in context of Yusuke's deck.
- 'Other help and supports' section (Big 3, Dodge this! with Hien, Suppppppppper! with Franky, Yusuke Rendan with Naruto) describes combo interactions with other characters' support/help koma, not Yusuke's own koma; captured under combos since they involve Yusuke's attacks but they reference non-Yusuke characters' effects (e.g., Hien's paralysis on 4th hit, Franky's damage) which are not part of Yusuke's own kit.
- Ultimate Action (SELECT) description says it boosts Y attacks broadly but only specifically calls out forward+Y range increase; unclear if all Y attacks get more range or just forward+Y.
- Guide's B-string combo section is prose narrative rather than a strict formal 'B string' move definition; treated as closest schema fit under input 'B string' plus 'other' for the Almighty Yusuke Combo since it doesn't cleanly match a single input type.
- No explicit passives are stated for any koma size (4/5/6); passives left as empty arrays rather than null since schema expects an array field.
