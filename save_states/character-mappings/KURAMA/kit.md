### Kurama — FAQ 46226

**Source:** `46226-kurama.txt`; author: Justin "discoinferno84" Boot; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **air B:** Short-range aerial slash performed with A + B.
  - Damage: 7.

- **air Y:** Throws a rose down diagonally, performed with A + Y.
  - Damage: 14; Properties: projectile.

- **B:** Forward slash with the whip.
  - Damage: 7.

- **up B:** Upward slash.
  - Damage: 7.

- **down B:** Summons a swirl of petals; at 4-Block this changes the enemy into another character, and at 5-Block it switches the enemy's active character. Also usable while defending, per the controls section, to swirl rose petals that switch a hit enemy to another character in their Koma Deck.
  - Damage: 7; Properties: forced switch.

- **side B:** Forward thrust.
  - Damage: 7.

- **B string:** Forward dash into a forward thrust, performed by double-tapping forward then B.
  - Damage: 7.

- **Y:** Forward swing with the whip.
  - Damage: 4.

- **up Y:** Upward swing with the whip; pressing Y repeatedly scores multiple hits.
  - Damage: 4-10; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Throws a seed to temporarily stun the enemy.
  - Damage: 12; Properties: projectile, status:stun.

- **side Y:** Forward swing with the whip; pressing Y repeatedly scores multiple hits.
  - Damage: 4-14; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **B string:** Forward dash into a forward whip swing.
  - Damage: 4.

- **other:** While defending, throws a seed projectile at the enemy (from the general controls section, distinct from the down Y seed listed in the koma-size movelist).
  - Properties: projectile.

- **select:** Plants a seed on the ground that will stun an enemy if stepped on. Listed only under the 5-Block moveset table.
  - Damage: 0; Properties: status:stun.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:**
  - X: Quick multi-hit whip combo. (Damage: 15; Properties: multi-hit)
  - D-Pad + X: Summon a barrage of vines. (Damage: 19)

- **5-koma — knowledge:**
  - X: Morph into Youko and summon a barrage of vines. (Damage: 36)
  - up X: Morph into Youko and summon a group of man-eating plants. (Damage: 38)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The 4-Block and 5-Block tables both list nearly identical B moves (A+B, up B, down B, side B, B string) with the same 7 damage; these are treated as a single move set shared across koma sizes rather than duplicated, since the guide gives no size-specific damage differences for B attacks except Y and X.
- Y damage differs between koma sizes: at 4-Block, plain Y does 4 damage; at 5-Block, plain Y does 11 damage. The guide does not explain this discrepancy or state it's a scaling effect from koma size explicitly for this move (though the introduction to koma size states 'a character will gain new moves' and power increases with size).
- The up Y and side Y damage ranges also differ slightly between the 4-Block table (4-10 for up Y) and 5-Block table (4-14 for up Y), and side Y stays 4-14 in both tables.
- The final side Y string entry in the 5-Block table ('D-Pad Forward (2x) + Y') is described as 'Forward swing with the whip' at 4 damage, identical wording to the 4-Block table's 'Forward Dash + Forward Swing' -- likely the same move, guide text is just abbreviated/inconsistent between the two tables.
- Select (Ultimate Action, seed trap) is only listed in the 5-Block table, not the 4-Block table -- unclear if it's unavailable at 4-Block or just omitted by the guide.
- The 5-Block X specials (36 and 38 damage) both describe morphing into Youko; the guide does not clarify if this is a temporary transformation state that persists or a single-hit special.
- Guide states general controls (D-pad Up+X summons man-eating plants at any koma size) in the Controls section, but the actual per-koma tables only show up+X for the 5-Block koma; the 4-Block table has no up+X entry, so it's unclear if the plant-summon X move exists at 4-Block despite the controls section implying it's a base-game mechanic.
- Version 1.0 of the guide explicitly states the Strategy/Tactics section is unfinished ('Still working on this; expect a more developed section in the next version'), so only one Tactic (Jump Canceling) and general Strengths/Weaknesses prose are present -- no additional move-specific combo strings were given beyond prose description of 'potential combos.'
- Ally Boost / Koma Deck Support section lists Hiei, Kuwabara, and Shun as granting boosts to Kurama, and Kurama granting boosts to Hiei and Robin -- this is deck-support meta info, not a move, and doesn't fit the schema's move/koma structure, so it's noted here rather than modeled.
