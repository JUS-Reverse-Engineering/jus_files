### Raoh — FAQ 46626

**Source:** `46626-raoh.txt`; author: LifeVirus7; version 1.31.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Raoh slaps the enemy in front of him.
  - Damage: 10 damage / 15 Damage; Hits: 1 hit.

- **side B:** Raoh lunges forward with his palm, going about twice his length forward.
  - Damage: Up to 11 damage / Up to 16 damage; Hits: Up to 4 hits; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down B:** Raoh lets out an aura burst at his feet, hitting in front and behind him. Forces the opponent to switch their battling character with another in their deck.
  - Damage: 12 damage / 18 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit; Properties: forced switch.

- **up B:** Raoh stabs his finger up at a 45-degree angle.
  - Damage: 10 Damage / 15 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit.

- **air B:** Raoh kicks in front of him, going a body length forward as he kicks.
  - Damage: 10 Damage / 15 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit.

- **Y:** Raoh charges his aura, damaging those around in front and behind him. Using this attack makes his next Forward + Y or Up + Y attack stronger.
  - Damage: 20 Damage / 30 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit; Properties: charge.

- **side Y:** Raoh launches his palm forward, with an aura burst hitting in front of him. Using Y before this attack makes his aura reach to the edge of the screen.
  - Damage: 20 Damage / 30 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit.

- **down Y:** Raoh swings his arm, causing a small shockwave on the ground to strike forward. Breaks the opponent's guard.
  - Damage: 23 Damage / 34 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit; Properties: guard break.

- **up Y:** Raoh unleashes an aura, damaging people to the side and above him. Using Y before this attack makes his aura expand in size and adds some damage.
  - Damage: 28 Damage / 42 Damage w/o aura charged, 30 Damage / 45 Damage with aura charged; Hits: Up to 10 Hits; Properties: multi-hit.

- **air Y:** Raoh slams down towards the earth, thrusting his palm down. Will knock an enemy through a platform if possible.
  - Damage: Up to 20 Damage / Up to 30 Damage; Hits: Up to 2 Hits; Properties: knockdown.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Holding the select button down slowly recharges Raoh's special meter.

- **other — Tapping his koma (touch screen activation attack):** Raoh does the same animation as his Y attack, but it doesn't charge his aura.
  - Damage: 20 Damage / 30 Damage; Hits: 1 Hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from special attacks.
  - Passive: Gains special energy while guarding attacks.
  - X: Raoh starts charging, then unleashes an energy burst a slight distance to the sides and above. Gains Attack Boost status. (Damage: 40 Damage / 60 Damage; Properties: charge, status:Attack Boost)
  - up X: Raoh's horse gets up on its hind legs for a second, then comes crashing down on the enemy in front. (Damage: Up to 54 Damage / Up to 81 Damage)

- **7-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from special attacks.
  - Passive: Gains special energy while guarding attacks.
  - X: Raoh swings his arms, then unleashes a beam of energy forward, going about one screen distance. (Damage: Up to 56 / Up to 84; Properties: projectile)
  - up X: Raoh's horse gets up on its hind legs for a second, then comes crashing down on the enemy in front. High knockback power. (Damage: Up to 68 / Up to 102 Damage)

- **8-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Takes less damage from special attacks.
  - Passive: Gains special energy while guarding attacks.
  - X: Raoh swings his arms, then unleashes a large beam of energy forward, going about one and a half screen's distance. (Damage: Up to 77 damage / Up to 115 damage; Properties: projectile)
  - up X: Raoh lets off a wavy aura. If he is attacked while it is activated, he counterattacks and shortly gains invincibility. Not all attacks activate the counter, some will knock him out of it instead. (Damage: 85 Damage / 127 Damage; Properties: counter)

#### Combos

- A basic but effective combo is B, Y, Up Y. Raoh hits them for 60 / 90 without any special bars used. You can add in Forward B, but there is a chance the opponent will be knocked too far for the rest of the attacks to hit.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Cover header reads 'Raoh Character FAQ V1.3', but the Version Info section lists updates through V1.31; source.version set to 1.31 (the latest stated revision).
- Base J-Soul values given per koma size (6-koma: 184, 7-koma: 200, 8-koma: 216) don't fit the schema's koma fields; recorded here instead.
- Ally boost (+8 max HP) characters listed for Raoh: Toki (Hokuto no Ken), Yuria (Hokuto no Ken), Cascade (Midori no Makibaou) — no schema field for ally-boost partners.
- Guide states Raoh has no support koma versions at all ('He has no support koma versions. Oh well.'); the support koma recommendations in the Strategies section (3-block Trunks, Seiya, Sena, plus HAH Brothers, Daijiro, Hyoga, Ikki, Gyro, Ta-Chan, Toki, Hiei, Nami) are general-purpose support koma suggestions to pair with Raoh, not Raoh's own support koma.
- Help koma (1H, unlocked via Boss Tower mission 10 objective 1) has no single fixed effect stated; the guide instead recommends choosing from several help koma types when playing Raoh: Halve status condition time, Recover health slowly, Strengthen guard (paired with Yuria for her ally boost), Triple jump, Increase SP gauge when attacking with a battling character, Add one to the maximum special gauges. It explicitly says NOT to use 'Reduce damage from special attacks' on Raoh since he already has that passive innately.
- Evolution/unlock currency costs (e.g. 6B: 500 red, 100 green, 30 white) and the unlock path requiring Yuria's second evolution path upgrade for 7/8-koma access don't fit the schema and are omitted from koma entries.
- Innate passives ('Takes less damage from special attacks', 'Gains special energy while guarding attacks') are stated once for the character overall, not per koma size; applied identically to all three battle koma entries here since the guide doesn't distinguish them by size.
- The guide notes Raoh's 8-koma has the second-highest health in the game (only Edijima's 8-koma is higher), but gives no numeric HP value, so hp is null for all koma.
