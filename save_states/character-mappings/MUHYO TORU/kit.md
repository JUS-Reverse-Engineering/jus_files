### Muhyo — FAQ 47061

**Source:** `47061-muhyo-yamcha.txt`; author: Yamcha; version 1.1.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Book Smack:** Muhyo's combo starting move; a fast, short-ranged attack.
  - Damage: 6 [Power][Knowledge], 9 [Laughter].

- **side B — Ghost Attacker:** A small monster slashes at opponents in front of you. Minimal downtime, so it's a safe poke to use often. Hold B to increase the range.
  - Damage: 9 [P][K], 13 [L]; Properties: charge.

- **up B — Spike Summon:** The air launcher; fairly fast, sends the opponent upwards. Used to start air combos.
  - Damage: 9 [P][K], 13 [L]; Properties: launcher.

- **down B — Palm Thrust:** A standard character-change move. Activates quickly but has lag time afterward. Cannot be integrated into combos.
  - Damage: 7 [P][K], 10 [L]; Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Aerial Ghost Attacker:** Somehow hits harder than its ground counterpart. A good air poke to encourage others to keep their distance.
  - Damage: 10 [P][K], 15 [L].

- **Y — Satan's Shadow:** A purple monster pops out of the ground and drags the opponent away if it connects. Significant lag afterward whether it hits or misses; don't use it alone or if someone is behind you. Can't go through walls, and ordinarily can't go off platforms/ledges unless it grabs someone near a ledge. Hold Y to extend range.
  - Damage: 16 [P][K], 24 [L]; Properties: charge.

- **side Y — Giant Fork Summon:** Launches people upwards; quick, but use in moderation because it becomes predictable. If the near side of the fork connects, the enemy launches toward you; if the far side connects, they launch away. Directly after Neutral Y, the latter happens. Hold Y to extend range.
  - Damage: 20 [P][K], 30 [L]; Properties: launcher, charge.

- **up Y — Never Returning Owl:** A quick anti-air attack. Best used as combo filler since it's hard to connect on its own; can also scare aerial fighters back to the ground.
  - Damage: 20 [P][K], 30 [L].

- **down Y — Satan's Iron Hammer:** A nice guard break that comes out quickly and can hit multiple blockers.
  - Damage: First Hit: 8 [P][K], 12 [L]; Both Hits: 18 [P][K], 27 [L]; Hits: 2; Properties: guard break, multi-hit.

- **air Y — Shadow Blobs:** Three shadow things emerge from the ground and explode in mid-air. Useful for hitting unsuspecting crowds underneath you.
  - Damage: 7 [P][K], 10 [L] per hit; Hits: 3; Properties: multi-hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Unknown
  - X — Meiou no Bansan no Kei: A monster's mouth appears from the ground and swallows anyone caught in it. Leaves you open when used. Best used after a Neutral Y attack. (Damage: 40 [P][K], 60 [L])
  - up X — Mazukan no Kei: A large spellbook appears overhead and its pages flip to damage enemies. Difficult to set up for full damage. (Damage: 33 [P][K], 49 [L])

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - Passive: Unknown
  - X — Maretsusha no Kei: A train appears behind Muhyo and rushes forward to hit anyone in front of you. (Damage: 40 [P][K], 60 [L])
  - up X — Yuurishoukan: A grey monster appears and swirls upwards, creating a small vortex that catches people around him. Use the control pad to change his vertical direction. (Damage: 48 [P][K], 72 [L])

- **6-koma — knowledge:** HP 160.
  - Passive: Unknown
  - X — Makensui no Kei: A knight(?) on horseback is summoned and charges forward. Powerful, but can be easily blocked by enemy supports occupying the field. (Damage: ~50 [P][K], ~72 [L])
  - up X — Yuurisoukan Hontou no Sugata: An improved and more powerful version of the regular Yuurisoukan; a larger spinning monster with a bigger vortex area that can catch two fighters. Use the control pad to change direction. (Damage: ~56 [P][K], ~84 [L])

- **7-koma — knowledge:** HP 176.
  - Passive: Unknown
  - X — Meiouruararieshoukan Touato no Yari: An extremely slow but powerful attack; a giant monster is summoned to throw a lance ahead of you. Best used in a free-for-all when everyone else is busy, out of their field of view. (Damage: 52 [P][K], 78 [L])
  - up X — Meiouruararieshoukan Senpen no Shita: Another big monster is summoned that tosses a flurry of punches in front of you. Has some edge-guarding potential if timed correctly. Press X for more hits. (Damage: 64 [P][K], 96 [L]; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit)

### Muhyo — FAQ 50905

**Source:** `50905-muhyo-lordlegacy.txt`; author: Lord_Legacy (Andarel); version 0.7.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Muhyo puts on his urn disguise and goes to sleep, regaining SP.

- **B — Book Hit:** Decently fast B interrupter. Muhyo swings his book forwards, nothing special. Generic combo starter, but not very effective for Muhyo; only combos into his Y when used to interrupt.
  - Damage: 6 (9).

- **side B — Ghost Attacker:** A small ghost appears and dashes forwards, hitting and stunning for a moment. Very little lag after; combos flawlessly into Y (if close) or side Y. Can be held to increase range, and continues even if Muhyo is hit.
  - Damage: 9 (13); Properties: charge.

- **up B — Spikes:** Spikes appear from below Muhyo and knock the opponent upwards. Fast and a superb combo starter, with less range restriction than side B; also hits slightly behind Muhyo. Combos flawlessly into Y or up Y. Slight delay after use.
  - Damage: 9 (13); Properties: launcher.

- **down B — Palm Strike:** Muhyo steps forward slightly after a moment of charging, and palm strikes the opponent. Okay as a character change move, but nothing special; combos only into up Y.
  - Damage: 7 (10); Properties: forced switch.

- **air B — Ghost Attacker (Midair Version):** Muhyo shoots off a small ghost to dash forward and hit in mid-air. Identical to side B in sprite, but its reach cannot be extended. Does slightly more damage, and is a fairly good aerial poke. Does not spike.
  - Damage: 10 (15).

- **Y — Satan's Shadow (For the Crime of "Illegal Inhabitance of an Object"):** The Satan's Shadow summon comes out of the ground and grabs the opponent, dragging them forward. Slow with a lot of lag, but combos well out of side B or up B at certain ranges. Holds for a while, making it good for support combos. Cannot go through walls. Hold Y to increase range, though that range cannot go off ledges/platforms once grabbed it can. Can whiff and go into its "grab mode" without grabbing.
  - Damage: 16 (24); Properties: charge.

- **side Y — The Devil's Pitchfork (For the Crime of "Unauthorized Transformation"):** Muhyo's ideal combo technique and one of the best moves he has. Hold Y to increase its range; it still goes off if Muhyo is hit; and its launch direction depends on which side hits it - the farther side launches away, the closer side launches towards Muhyo.
  - Damage: 20 (30); Properties: launcher, charge.

- **up Y — Never-Returning Owl (For the Crime of "Taking Another's Features"):** Muhyo's best anti-air attack and one of the best in the game. A fast yellow owl rockets off from in front of him, grabbing anything in its path and dropping it for good damage. Leaves opponents unspinning in the air briefly, letting supports connect.
  - Damage: 20 (30).

- **down Y — Satan's Iron Hammer (For the Crime of "Attempted Murder"):** A two-hit guard break that is fairly fast, has nice range, and can hit multiple blockers. Also spikes.
  - Damage: 8(12)+10(15); Hits: 2; Properties: guard break, multi-hit.

- **air Y — Shadow Creatures:** A great sniping move that comes from the ground below (no matter how far below) and does good damage. Keeps Muhyo unmoving in the air while used, allowing potential dodging.
  - Damage: 7(10) per hit, launches 3; Properties: launcher, multi-hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Regain Sp when fighting Knowledge characters. Any others are unknown.
  - X — Hades' Dinner Punishment: Creates a mouth on the ground in front of Muhyo which eats the enemy and spits them out. Has limited range in front, and will not appear if Muhyo would summon it off the edge of a platform. (Damage: 40(60))
  - up X — Demonic Spellbook Punishment: A spellbook appears above Muhyo and flips its pages, dealing damage. Mashable for more hits. (Damage: 33(49)[1xhit + 20]; Hits: up to 13 (mash); Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - X — Magic Train Summon: Muhyo calls the Magic Train to come forward and deal damage to anyone in front of him. The Train hits 6 times and character-changes on the last hit, carrying the opponent just past the edge of the screen in one direction. (Damage: 40(60); Hits: 6; Properties: forced switch, multi-hit)
  - up X — Secluded Plum - Summon: Muhyo brings forward a small grey being surrounded by a whirlwind, which carries anything hit by it upwards before exploding and sending them flying. Combos out of his guard break at close range and out of Y>. (Damage: 48(72); Properties: launcher)

- **6-koma — knowledge:** HP 160.
  - X — The Great Demon General Summon: Muhyo calls forth the Great Demon General to rush forward and slice any enemies he connects with. Hitting an energy blast, support, or blocking character will cancel this move. Sends very far and combos out of Y; hits slightly above Muhyo when summoned. (Damage: 50 (75))
  - up X — Secluded Plum - Summon (True Form): A bigger version of the 5-Koma's up X, drawing enemies in and bringing them off the screen vertically before exploding and sending them flying. Larger and easier to hit with, but can no longer combo out of guard break and is slightly harder to get max damage on. Platforms above Muhyo make it impossible to combo into. (Damage: 56(84); Properties: launcher)

- **7-koma — knowledge:** HP 176.
  - X — Dark King Ruaire Summon - Douato Spear: A terrible, atrociously slow special. Decent damage and good guard-breaking potential, but Muhyo is stuck in place for a long time. Only safe to use when Muhyo is away from the action. (Damage: 52(78); Properties: guard break)
  - up X — Dark King Ruaire Summon - Thousand Hit Tongue: Sends down a globe of light that pops the enemy up into the air and then whips them with the summoned monster's tongue. Mashable for more hits. Very good with support assistance. Platforms above Muhyo make it impossible to combo into. (Damage: 64(96)[2xhit + 4]; Hits: up to 30 (mash); Properties: pop, mash-extend, multi-hit)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide describes Muhyo as summoner-type explicitly ('grouped with Yugi and Taikoubou... as summoner-type characters. This means that he calls out various spells to attack for him'), consistent with kit_type 'summoner' set per owner instruction. However, several of his named moves (Book Hit, Ghost Attacker, Spikes, Palm Strike) read as direct physical strikes rather than summons, even though flavor text ties them to ghosts/spells; only Y-series moves and X specials are clearly summon-based. Noting this mixed presentation in case it matters for kit_type taxonomy.
- Series attribution: character field uses 'Muhyo' per instructions; series name given in guide is 'Muhyo tou Rouji no Mahou Rishitsusou Dan Jimusho' (his manga) - no simpler series name is given in the guide text.
- Damage numbers throughout are given as 'X(Y)' pairs (e.g. '6 (9)') - guide does not explicitly label which number is which koma/context; format preserved as written, unresolved as to precise meaning of parenthetical value (likely blunt/character-specific multiplier per repo convention, but not stated in this guide).
- 1-Koma passive text ('Gives an increase in attack strength when health is low') is placed under both 'passives' and 'help_effect' since the guide presents it as the sole content of the 1-Koma help entry, with no separate stated support/help effect distinct from the passive.
- 2-Koma and 3-Koma sizes are presented in the guide only as support-type entries (invisibility, invincibility) with no koma HP, nature, or passives given - schema fields for these left null.
- The guide's version header states 'Current Version - v0.7' but the changelog section (S09) refers to 'Original v0.9 created' - a contradiction in the guide's own versioning; source.version set to '0.7' per the top header as instructed to use the guide's own header value.
- Deck section and playstyle section (S05-S07) contain extensive strategic prose (support combo pairings with Kanda 3, Rouji 3, Jelly Jiggler 3, Arystar Krory 3, Gotenks 3; sample decks) that does not map cleanly to the schema's move/koma/combo fields; support-combo pairings are not captured as discrete 'combos' entries since they involve external characters' supports rather than Muhyo's own inputs alone - summarized here instead.
- Guide states Muhyo's boost relationships (boosted by Neuro, Page, Rouji; boosts Neuro) - no schema field exists for ally-boost relationships, so this is noted here rather than dropped silently.
