### Kazuki Mutou — FAQ 46549

**Source:** `46549-kazuki-mutou.txt`; author: bitsquall; version 2.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Kazuki does a sideways slash.
  - Damage: 7.

- **up B:** Kazuki thrusts his lance upwards.
  - Damage: 8.

- **down B:** Kazuki blows a kiss to the enemy capable of forcing them to switch Battle Characters.
  - Damage: 8; Properties: forced switch.

- **side B:** Forward thrust.
  - Damage: 8.

- **other — Double-tap forward + B:** Forward Dash + Forward Thrust.
  - Damage: 8.

- **air B:** Kazuki swings his Lance downward.
  - Damage: 8.

- **Y:** Downward Swing.
  - Damage: 16.

- **up Y:** Kazuki bursts upward after releasing energy from his Lance in a two-part attack.
  - Damage: 8-16; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Kazuki sets the bottom of his Lance on the ground and releases energy from his sash in all directions. Capable of Guard Break.
  - Damage: 16; Properties: guard break.

- **side Y:** Kazuki bursts forward after releasing energy from his Lance in a two-part attack.
  - Damage: 8-16; Properties: multi-hit.

- **other — Double-tap forward + Y:** Forward Dash + Two part attack.
  - Damage: 8-16; Properties: multi-hit.

- **air Y:** Kazuki bursts forward after releasing energy from his Lance in a two-part attack (aerial version).
  - Damage: 8-16; Properties: multi-hit.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Kazuki's Lance dissipates and he pulls out a Milk carton, which he drinks, restoring some HP.
  - Damage: 0.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - X: Kazuki pulls out a second Lance and rushes forward with a burst of energy pointing both Lances in front of him and goes about half a screen's distance capable of damaging anything in its way. (Damage: 28)
  - up X: Kazuki pulls his sash forward and releases a shining energy from it that blinds whatever it damages. (Damage: 20; Properties: status:blind)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - X: Kazuki wraps his sash around his Lance and charges energy in 3 different Levels: Glowing, Glowing with an Arrow, Automatic Release. (Damage: 32/40/52; Properties: charge)
  - up X: Papillon appears and points and sends butterflies that either gather around the closest enemy character or halfway in/out the screen. (Damage: 36)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - X: Kazuki transforms along with his Lance and charges forward. Capable of doing more damage with the more Koma in your deck: 2-5 Koma - 40 Damage, 6-9 Koma - 48 Damage, 10+ Koma - 56 Damage. (Damage: 40/48/56)
  - up X: Tokiko appears and slashes with her Valkyrie Skirt 6 times. (Damage: 2-46; Hits: 6; Properties: multi-hit)

#### Combos

- B, Up B, Y - Kazuki knocks the opponent into the air and back down. Damage : 31 Damage to Power, 46 to Knowledge, 31 to Laughter
- B, Forward B, Y - Kazuki Slashes the opponent, pierces, then pulls his Lance downward. Damage : 31 Damage to Power, 46 to Knowledge, 31 to Laughter
- B, Up B, Forward Y - Kazuki knocks the opponent into the air, and instead of doing Y, he does his two-part attack, the opponent has a chance to block the last attack, but they barely have a split second to block. Damage : 31 Damage to Power, 46 to Knowledge, 31 to Laughter
- B, Up B/Forward B, Y, Up Y - Kazuki attacks the enemy and them bursts upwards, good for escaping because the enemy is also stunned and only can move after you're already in the air. Damage : 31 Damage to Power, 46 to Knowledge, 31 to Laughter
- B, Up/Forward B, Y, Forward Y - Very low chance to hit with the final hit, but if you do, you can manage some nice damage. Damage : 47 Damage to Power, 70 to Knowledge, 47 to Laughter
- Forward B, Forward Y - A new combo I noticed that pierces them with Forward B and should stun them long enough to hit them with Forward Y if you did it fast enough. Damage : 24 Damage to Power, 36 to Knowledge, 24 to Laughter
- Extra Combo : Use a quick support the moment they're open from a Guard Break. Example - Down+Y, L/R.
- 4-Koma - B, Forward B, Y, Up X - Causes Blind. Damage : 51 to Power, 76 to Knowledge, 51 to Laughter
- 4-Koma - Down Y, Up X - Guard Breaks, Causes Blind. Damage : 36 to Power, 54 to Knowledge, 36 to Laughter
- 4-Koma - B, Up B, X - Damage : 43 to Power, 66 to Knowledge, 43 to Laughter
- 5-Koma - B, Up B, X - Damage : 47 to Power, 70 to Knowledge, 47 to Laughter
- 6-Koma - B, Up B, Up X - Damage : 61 to Power, 91 to Knowledge, 61 to Laughter

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide's own button legend does not define an explicit 'air B'/'air Y' term; the A+B and A+Y table rows are inferred to be aerial versions of B and Y because A is the jump button (per the Controls section), but the guide never labels them that way directly.
- The B and Y attack tables (B, up B, down B, side B, double-tap-forward B, air B, Y, up Y, down Y, side Y, double-tap-forward Y, air Y) are printed identically, with identical damage numbers, in the 4-Koma, 5-Koma, and 6-Koma sections of part V. They were treated here as shared top-level moves rather than duplicated per koma size, since the guide gives no koma-specific variation for them.
- Guide states 'all of Kazuki's Koma is Power-based' (part II, Terminology), so nature 'power' was applied to every koma size including the 1-Koma help koma and 2-3 Koma support komas, though the guide never states a nature for those specifically.
- No HP values are given anywhere in the guide for any koma size; all hp fields are null.
- The Strategy/Strengths section (part VI-B) says Kazuki has an 'Auto-HP Regen' passive strength, and the 1-Koma entry separately says 'Health Recovers slowly' -- these may be the same underlying passive described twice, or two different things (a base regen plus a help-koma effect). The guide does not clarify, so no passives were added to the 4-6 Koma battle entries beyond what's explicitly tied to them (none stated).
- Table of contents numbering is inconsistent/garbled: 'Koma Deck Support' and 'Koma Opinions' are both labeled 'VII.' in the body text, and the numbering skips/misaligns with the listed contents (VIII, IX) versus what's actually in the document body.
- The intro line 'he's the only Battle...AND Support Komas' suggests kit_type could arguably be considered unusual (both battle and support koma forms), but the guide never uses item-creator/summoner/other wording, so kit_type was left as 'standard' per the schema's default rule.
- 2-Koma and 3-Koma support damage is given per opposing Koma affinity (Power/Knowledge/Laughter) rather than as a single base number; these three values were folded into the support_effect text as written since the schema has no dedicated per-affinity damage field for support koma.
- 5-Koma Koma Opinions section says the X attack 'doing only 32 Damage at minimum' and 'fully charged it does 52 Damage', consistent with the moveset table's 32/40/52, but never separately mentions the 40 middle value in that prose -- treated as consistent, not a contradiction, since the table is explicit.
