### Momotaro — FAQ 50074

**Source:** `50074-momotaro.txt`; author: Christian Fontanet / TheFenikkusu (Ryobit); version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Momotaro swings his sword in a downward arc.
  - Damage: 10 neutral, 15 knowledge.

- **Y:** Momotaro unleashes a flurry of punches. Tap Y rapidly for extra damage.
  - Damage: max-33 knowledge, max-22 neutral; Properties: mash-extend.

- **side B:** Momotaro quickly kicks.
  - Damage: 10 neutral, 15 knowledge.

- **side Y:** After about a 0.5 second lag, Momotaro shoots a blue tiger from the tip of his sword. Good edge-guarding move; the panel can be touched repeatedly to keep shooting the blue tiger.
  - Damage: about 18 neutral, 27 knowledge; Properties: projectile.

- **up B:** Momotaro removes his headband and whips it upwards diagonally, depending on which direction he is facing.
  - Damage: 9 neutral, 13 knowledge.

- **up Y:** After about a 0.5 second lag, Momotaro throws his sword into the air, hitting the enemy several times. Useful for hitting enemies descending into the stage.
  - Damage: at least 4, at most 20 neutral; at least 6, at most 30 knowledge; Properties: multi-hit.

- **air B:** Like his normal B only the arc is a bit bigger.
  - Damage: same as B.

- **air Y:** Momotaro does a tornado-spin all the way to the ground.
  - Damage: same as up Y.

- **down B:** Momotaro's force-switch attack. It's reasonably fast.
  - Damage: 10 neutral, 15 knowledge; Properties: forced switch.

- **down Y:** Momotaro's guard-break. It's very fast but has poor range.
  - Damage: 20 neutral, 30 knowledge; Properties: guard break.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Recovers a decent amount of SP, but the guide advises against using it unless absolutely necessary because Momotaro's built-in effect drains SP away while not fighting.
  - Properties: status:SP recovery.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - X: Lags a long time, leaving the user open for about 2 seconds afterward. Not usable without support setup.
  - up X: Takes several seconds to use; its only effect is to recover a little bit of SP energy. (Properties: status:SP recovery, charge)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - X: Much better than the 4-koma X but has just enough lag that it can't be combo'd into with just Momotaro's normal attacks. Good for ring-outs.
  - up X: Takes so long it's pretty much worthless; makes the user invulnerable for about 2 seconds. (Properties: counter, charge)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - X: Very good for edge-guarding and catching opponents off-guard.
  - up X: Lags for about 1.5 seconds but works very well with supports. (Properties: charge)

- **6-koma — knowledge:**
  - X: Pretty much the same as the Power type 6-koma X, but this koma is Knowledge nature.
  - up X: Not separately described; guide treats this koma as functionally the same as the Power 6-koma.

#### Combos

- B -> Side-B -> Y -> Side-Y: Deals about 44-48 damage against neutral types and 66-72 damage against knowledge types depending on how many times you tap Y.
- B -> Up-B -> Side-Y: Deals 37 damage to neutral types and 55 damage to knowledge types.
- B->Side-B->Y->Shishio-2->[run towards opponent]B->Y->Shishio 2: Rinse and repeat until you reach the limit. (Also works with Gotenks-3 and Bobobo-2 with slightly different timing.)
- 4-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Gotenks-3 -> X: Deals 108 damage to power types, 96 damage to laughter types, and 122 damage to knowledge types.
- 4-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Kagura-3 -> [wait until you hear the 5th smash...] -> X: Deals 113 damage to power types, 123 damage to knowledge types, and about 93 damage to laughter types.
- 5-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Kenshiro-3 -> X: Deals about 76 damage to neutral types and about 113 to Knowledge types.
- 5-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Kenshin-2 -> X: Only deals about 44 damage but the paralysis from Kenshin lasts long enough to repeat the combo; great for ring-outs.
- 6-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Naruto-3 -> Up-X: Deals 102 damage to neutral types and 162 damage to Knowledge types.
- 6-Koma: B -> Side-B -> Y -> Shishio-2 -> Up-X: Deals about 108 damage to neutral types and up to 165 damage against knowledge.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Guide's Table of Contents header is malformed/duplicate ('X. - Credits [CRDT]' appears alongside a section IX. Useful Info; content order followed as in the body text).
- The guide gives damage numbers by nature type inconsistently: base moves are described only for 'neutral' and 'knowledge' types, while combo sections mix in 'power' and 'laughter' type damage numbers without explaining the relationship between 'neutral' and 'power' nature labels; left as written.
- Help koma is described only by its SP-regeneration effect; the guide does not give it a numeric koma size.
- Support koma sizes (2 and 3) are given but their nature is implied as 'power' only by omission/context; guide does not explicitly restate nature for the 3-koma support beyond listing it under 'Power'.
- The 6-koma Knowledge battle koma's Up-X is not separately described in the text; guide only says the koma is 'pretty much the same as the Power type' overall, so Up-X details for this koma are assumed identical to the Power 6-koma but this is not explicitly stated.
- Ally boosts are mentioned as 'terrible, with the exception of Edajima' but no move-level detail is given, so this is omitted from the moves/koma structure.
- Section IX note I calls Momotaro's SELECT move the 'Ultimate Action' and says it recovers 'a decent amount' of SP, which is a different characterization than found for other characters' Ultimate Actions; kept as written, no numeric SP value given.
- Guide credits list an 'Offensive_Altar' FAQ as a guideline and 'Genroh' as a primary source; no additional moveset data beyond what's in the main body was found in these credits.
