### Eve — FAQ 46504

**Source:** `46504-eve.txt`; author: Taoto; version 1.0 per title header; body text (section 11) says updated to v.2.0 with damage numbers added.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **other — A (Jump):** Eve can jump a max of three times using wings she gives herself; this is her second passive ability.

- **select — Super Jump:** Eve's Ultimate Action: she bends down, gives herself wings, and jumps to the height of a triple jump. Combined with her normal triple jump, she can effectively reach a height as if she'd jumped five times.

- **other — Guard:** Eve turns one of her hands into a shield, using her other hand to support it. This is the standard guard move.

- **B:** Eve punches forward with her hair, hitting about two character widths in front of herself.
  - Damage: 7 to Power/Knowledge, 10 to Laughter.

- **side B:** Eve punches forward with her hair, hitting about four character widths in front of herself.
  - Damage: 8 to Power/Knowledge, 12 to Laughter.

- **up B:** Eve bends down and turns her hair into spikes, hitting anybody above or next to her within close range.
  - Damage: 8 to Power/Knowledge, 12 to Laughter.

- **down B:** Eve turns one of her hands into a mace, takes a step forward, and attacks with it. Like everybody's down+B, this forces the opponent to change characters when struck, if able.
  - Damage: 7 to Power/Knowledge, 10 to Laughter; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Eve turns her hand into a giant knife, steps forward, and slashes with it.
  - Damage: 16 to Power/Knowledge, 24 to Laughter.

- **side Y:** Eve thrusts forward, throwing her hair forward about four character widths. If the enemy is about three to four character widths away, Eve grabs the enemy with her hair and throws him/her over her head about the same distance. If the enemy is too close or too far, the attack does nothing; a successful grab sets the enemy up for other attacks, especially specials.
  - Damage: 14 to Power/Knowledge, 21 to Laughter.

- **up Y:** Eve bends down, her wings come out with one looking like a fist and one hand also in a fist; she jumps up and performs an uppercut with both her fist and wing, counting as one hit. Lifts the enemy into the air, leaving them prone to her aerial Y attack.
  - Damage: 16 to Power/Knowledge, 24 to Laughter; Hits: 1; Properties: launcher.

- **down Y:** Eve turns one of her hands into a giant mallet, grabs it with her other hand, takes a few steps forward, and slams it down on the enemy. Like everybody's down+Y, this attack breaks guards.
  - Damage: 16 to Power/Knowledge, 24 to Laughter; Properties: guard break.

- **air B:** In the air, Eve turns her hair into a fist, punching at a 45 degree angle downwards. Has about the same range as her forward B attack, only at a downward angle.
  - Damage: 8 to Power/Knowledge, 12 to Laughter.

- **air Y:** In the air, Eve turns her hair into a fist, punching straight ahead. This attack reaches almost to the edge of the top screen, in a straight line.
  - Damage: 16 to Power/Knowledge, 24 to Laughter.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 128.
  - Passive: Eve's health automatically regenerates, over time, thanks to her nanomachines healing her.
  - Passive: Eve can jump a max of three times (triple jump).
  - X: Eve turns her hair into several fists and attacks with a barrage of punches; repeatedly tapping X makes the barrage last longer and moves Eve forward. About seven to eight fists appear on screen at a time, for a max of 10 hits. (Damage: 18 for 4 hits, 24 for 7, 30 for 10 to Power/Knowledge; 27 for 4 hits, 36 for 7, 45 for 10 to Laughter; Hits: up to 10 (most common counts: 4, 7, 10); Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit)
  - up X: Eve raises a wing and shoots off six feather blades in front of herself, hitting the enemy up to six times. (Damage: each hit does 5 for a max of 30 to Power/Knowledge; 5 hits do 6 damage each (guide notes an unclear number for a 7th hit) for a total of 37 to Laughter; Hits: up to 6; Properties: projectile, multi-hit)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 144.
  - Passive: Eve's health automatically regenerates, over time, thanks to her nanomachines healing her.
  - Passive: Eve can jump a max of three times (triple jump).
  - X — Lance: Eve bends down, her wings come out, turns one hand into a large lance, then dashes forward skewering any enemy in front of her; when she stops moving, the enemy is flung off the lance. Damages once on the skewer and once when flung; good for ring-outs since the flung enemy spins as they fall. (Damage: 10 on the first hit, 26 on the second, 36 total to Power/Knowledge; 12 on the first hit, 33 on the second, 45 total to Laughter; Hits: 2; Properties: launcher)
  - up X — Nanoslicer: Eve turns her hair into two large spear heads and starts to spin, jumping in the process. Tapping X gets more hits, to a maximum of 8; the last hit flings the enemy a short distance away. Good for clearing space around herself. (Damage: 14 for 2 hits, 22 for 4, 26 for 5, 34 for 7, 38 for 8 to Power/Knowledge; 17 for 2 hits, 27 for 4, 32 for 5, 42 for 7, 47 for 8 to Laughter; Hits: up to 8 (most common counts: 2, 4, 5, 7, 8); Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Version conflict: the guide's title header says 'v.1.0' but section 11 (Version Info) describes a v.2.0 update that added the damage numbers used throughout this extraction. Recorded both in source.version.
- The guide names the 4 koma X special's max-hit damage breakpoints as 4/7/10 hits but never gives a name for the move; likewise the 4 koma Up+X (feather shoot) is unnamed in the Move List section though referenced as 'Feather Shoot' in the Combos section.
- Section 5's Up+X Laughter damage line is garbled: 'Damage to Laughter: 5 hits do 6 damage, number for doing 7 instead, for a total of 37 damage.' The sentence appears to be missing words (possibly meaning some hits do 7 damage instead of 6); copied verbatim since the intended number cannot be confirmed.
- 4 koma and 5 koma base HP (128 and 144) are stated only as the pre-boost values in the Ally Boost section; the Move List/Special Attacks sections do not restate per-koma HP directly.
- The 3 space Support Koma special's exact input/trigger isn't a button the player presses on Eve herself (it's activated as a support koma by another character), so 'input: support' is a placeholder, not one of the schema's listed inputs.
- The A (Jump) and guard (Down) entries are not attacks; they are included per the task's instruction to capture every move, using input:'other' since the schema's input enum has no jump/guard option.
