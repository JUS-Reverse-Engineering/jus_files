**Source:** `47526-naruto.txt`; author: son1cwildfire.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Basic B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (P/L), 12 (K).

- **side B:** Basic side B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (P/L), 12 (K).

- **up B:** Basic up B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (P/L), 12 (K).

- **down B:** Basic down B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (P/L), 12 (K).

- **air B:** Basic aerial B attack.
  - Damage: 8 (P/L), 12 (K).

- **Y:** Basic Y attack.
  - Damage: 15 (P/L), 22 (K).

- **side Y:** Basic side Y attack.
  - Damage: 12 (P/L), 18 (K).

- **up Y:** Basic up Y attack.
  - Damage: 16 (P/L), 24 (K).

- **down Y:** Basic down Y attack.
  - Damage: 16 (P/L), 24 (K).

- **air Y:** Basic aerial Y attack.
  - Damage: 16 (P/L), 24 (K).

- **other — Guard break:** Naruto's guard break is a ranged attack, which is good because most guard break attacks only work up close.
  - Properties: guard break.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Lets Naruto absorb one hit for free while the wood icon in the bottom left corner is still showing. Best used against opponents with rapid-punching specials: get hit once, then teleport behind them and attack.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power/laughter:**
  - X — Rasengan: Basic Rasengan special A; needs a little start-up time. (Damage: 32 damage to power/laughter, 48 damage to knowledge; Properties: charge)
  - up X — Uzumaki Rendan: Special B; surprisingly stronger than the Rasengan. (Damage: 36 damage to power/laughter, 54 damage to knowledge)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - X — Rasengan: Easier to combo into than the 4 koma version; begins with 2 kicks while the Rasengan is charging. (Damage: 40 damage to power/laughter, 60 damage to knowledge; Properties: charge)
  - up X — Shadow Clone Attack: A shadow clone attack that can hit people within your view; used less often than the Rasengan. (Damage: 32 damage to power/laughter, 48 damage to knowledge)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - X — Rasengan: A powerful Rasengan that is held by 2 Narutos. (Damage: 48 damage to power/laughter, 72 damage to knowledge)
  - up X — Rasengan Combo: Naruto first kicks the opponent up and summons shadow clones to hold the opponent down, then uses the Rasengan on the held opponent. (Damage: 44 damage to power/laughter, 66 damage to knowledge; Properties: multi-hit)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide never mentions a down+B forced-switch attack for Naruto; omitted rather than guessed.
- The guide describes a ranged 'guard break' but never states which input (B/Y/X direction) triggers it; recorded as input 'other' with no damage value.
- The guide labels the koma-4/5/6 specials only as 'Special A' and 'Special B', never naming a button input. Inputs 'X' (Special A) and 'up X' (Special B) in this file are inferred from the combo notation ('B, B^, X^' and 'B, B^, X') in section III/V, not stated explicitly by the guide as button codes.
- 4 koma has two nature variants (power and laughter) with identical Special A/B base damage, but the laughter-version combo 'B, B^, X^' is listed as 60(P)/70(K)/52(L) -- a three-way split rather than the usual power/laughter-vs-knowledge split used everywhere else in the guide. Kept verbatim in combos; not reconciled.
- No koma HP values are given anywhere in this guide.
- No version number is stated for this FAQ; only a 2007 copyright year appears.
- Ally boost section (Sakura, Gaara, Jiraiya) describes other characters' help/support effects as they apply to a Naruto deck, not Naruto's own moves; included here only as background, not as Naruto koma/move entries.
- chr_b-Complete-Mapping.md table also lists chr_b[21] Sasuke, chr_b[22] Sakura, chr_b[23] Kakashi, and chr_b[24] Kyuubi Naruto under series 'Naruto', but these are separate characters (not alternate Naruto komas), so chr_b=20 for base Naruto was unambiguous; noting for completeness since the mapping doc's header calls out a related correction about chr_b[24].
