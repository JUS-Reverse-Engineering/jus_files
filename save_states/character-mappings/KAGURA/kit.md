### Kagura — FAQ 46302

**Source:** `46302-kagura.txt`; author: FFandMMfan; version 2.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Basic hit.
  - Damage: 15 to P and 10 to K/L.

- **side B:** Basic hit (same family as B and up B).
  - Damage: 15 to P and 10 to K/L.

- **up B:** Basic hit (same family as B and side B).
  - Damage: 15 to P and 10 to K/L.

- **air B:** Swings downwards, thrusting the enemy to the ground.
  - Damage: 13 damage to P and 9 to K/L.

- **down B:** Creates a text bubble that forces a character switch on the enemy.
  - Damage: 15 Damage to P and 10 to K/L; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Grabs the enemy and swings them around in circles, damaging all foes within the swinging range.
  - Damage: 27 damage to P and 18 damage to K/L (to the grabbed enemy).

- **side Y:** Dashes forward and swings her umbrella, sending the enemy in the opposite direction.
  - Damage: 30 to P and 20 to K/L.

- **up Y:** Uses her umbrella as a spring and dives at an upwards angle to kick an enemy.
  - Damage: 30 to P and 20 to K/L.

- **down Y:** Creates a text bubble that breaks an enemy's guard.
  - Damage: 30 to P and 20 to K/L; Properties: guard break.

- **air Y:** Does a diving kick.
  - Damage: 30 damage to P and 20 to K/L.

- **select — Ultimate Action:** Kagura pulls out a piece of celery or something green, eats it, and it slowly recovers her life.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when attacking.
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when she breaks treasure chests.
  - Passive: Slowly restores her Special bar at all times.
  - X — Special Attack A: Fires energy blasts from her Umbrella. Continuously tap X for more shots. Can fire up to 16 shots. (Damage: Max Damage: Against Power: 48; Against Knowledge/Laughter: 32; Properties: mash-extend, projectile, multi-hit)
  - X — Special Attack B: Kagura eats a bowl of food that restores some health. Can be interrupted by enemy attack.

- **5-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when attacking.
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when she breaks treasure chests.
  - Passive: Slowly restores her Special bar at all times.
  - X — Special Attack A: Hops on her giant dog Sadaharu and rides around, damaging anyone she runs into. You can control Sadaharu's direction with the D Pad. (Damage: Against Power: 45 Damage; Against Knowledge/Laughter: 30 Damage)
  - X — Special Attack B: Eats from what appears to be a rice cooker, restoring HP. Can be interrupted by enemy attack.

- **6-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when attacking.
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when she breaks treasure chests.
  - Passive: Slowly restores her Special bar at all times.
  - X — Special Attack A: Sadaharu appears and charges forward. Once he connects with an enemy he bites them and throws them forward. Good damage, and an excellent way to ring out someone. (Damage: Against Power: 67 Damage; Against Knowledge/Laughter: 45 Damage; Properties: forced switch)
  - X — Special Attack B: Same as her 3 Koma: a table appears, she hides under it; if anyone touches her head at one end of the table, they'll be pulled under, suffer some damage, and have their character switched upon release. (Damage: Against Power: 60 Damage; Against Knowledge/Laughter: 40 Damage; Properties: forced switch)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when attacking.
  - Passive: Restores her Special bar when she breaks treasure chests.
  - Passive: Slowly restores her Special bar at all times.
  - X: Since Kagura's Specials are ALL of the Laughter Nature even when using her Power Koma, she deals the same damage as her other 6 Koma with her specials to the same-natured characters. (Guide does not restate exact numbers for this Koma; presumably identical to the Laughter 6 Koma specials.)

#### Combos

- B -> B+Up -> B in the Air = Deals 43 damage to Power characters and 29 to Knowledge and Laughter characters.
- Y in the air -> Y in the air = When she hits an enemy with her flying kick, she'll be sent back up into the air. You can perform this again to deal two hits, or hit someone else who may have gotten in the way. Deals 30 damage to P and 20 to K/L per hit.
- B + Up -> Y in the Air near a ledge = Pretty much ensures they'll be knocked straight off the stage with no hope of recovery. Be careful though, you can't stop once you do the aerial Y, so if you miss, you'll kill yourself.
- Y -> 3 Koma Gotenks = 75 Damage to Power, 50 to Knowledge and Laughter. (Excellent ringout technique, Laughter Kagura)
- Y -> 3 Koma Gotenks = 66 Damage to Power, 59 to Knowledge and 50 to Laughter. (Excellent ringout technique, Power Kagura)
- Y+up -> 3 Koma Gotenks -> Aerial Y = Unpredictable damage since it's very hard to land both starting and ending hits plus every shot from Gotenks, but can do over 100 damage to weaker natured characters, and 60+ to neutral natured characters.
- Y -> 2 Koma Piccolo -> X Special (6 Koma Laughter) = 110 damage to Power, 87 to Knowledge, and 79 to Laughter.
- Y -> 3 Koma Arale -> X special (6 Koma Laughter) = 139 damage to Power, 93 to Knowledge and Laughter. (Excellent ringout technique)
- Y -> 2 Koma Shishio -> X Special (6 Koma Laughter) = 122 to Power, 105 to Knowledge and 91 to Laughter.
- Y -> 2 Koma Piccolo -> X Special (6 Koma Power) = 101 Damage to Power, 96 damage to Knowledge and 75 damage to Laughter.
- Y -> 3 Koma Arale -> X special (6 Koma Power) = 130 damage to Power, 102 to Knowledge and 93 damage to Laughter. (Excellent ringout technique)
- Y -> 2 Koma Shishio -> X Special (6 Koma Power) = 113 to Power, 114 to Knowledge and 91 to Laughter.
- Combined with Gotenks 3 Koma (Against Power characters) her 5 Koma Sadaharu special can deal 93 damage if every hit connects.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide states basic damage numbers are 'based on her Laughter-based koma' and that for Power Koma you 'switch the higher Damage from Power characters, to higher damage to Knowledge characters' — this implies normal-attack damage differs by Koma nature, but exact Power-koma numbers for normal B/Y attacks are never spelled out, only the substitution rule.
- For Battle Koma 6 (Power), the guide explicitly says her specials do the same damage as the Laughter 6 Koma against same-natured characters, but never restates the actual numbers for that Koma's specials — recorded as null damage/no restated figures, quote preserved.
- Section KA2 (evolution chart, gem costs) and KA9 (deck lists) are entirely deck-building/economy content and were omitted from the schema per scope (battle-mechanics-only).
- KA6/KA7 (playing tips, matchup notes) contain qualitative strategy text (e.g. 'all of her Y attacks have a small lag time before and after the attacks... none of her Y attacks can combo into each other except Y+up -> Aerial Y') that doesn't map cleanly to a single move entry; the Y+up->aerial Y note is captured only implicitly via the aerial-Y combo entries and the general Y-attacks-can't-combo statement is not separately modeled.
- KA8 boost/support-character notes (Gintoki, Shinpachi, Vegeta as boosters; Lenalee, Sanji, Satsuki, Gotenks, Piccolo, Arale, Shishio as non-boosting synergy Koma) are roster/support-character info, not Kagura's own moves, and are omitted except where they appear in explicit combo lines under 'combos'.
