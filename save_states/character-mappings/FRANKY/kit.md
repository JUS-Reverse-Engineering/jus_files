## Passive effects

- Take less damage from Sword attacks.
- Gain SP energy when being attacked by opponents.
- Take more damage from "Lightning" based attacks.

## Ultimate action

Franky inserts Cola into his system and white steam shoots from him. Consume 2 Colas to power up his Y attacks. Occasionally Franky will consume other beverages and not get the boost, this does not affect previous boosts. You'll know them by the different colored steams. (Brown = Tea and Green = Vegetable Juice)

### Franky — FAQ 51204

**Source:** `51204-franky.txt`; author: Kira93.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B — Regular B:** A head butt that moves him forward a bit. A close range move.
  - Damage: 8 to knowledge/laughter, 12 to power.

- **side B:** A simple straight punch that moves him forward a bit. A close range move.
  - Damage: 9 to knowledge/laughter, 13 to power.

- **down B:** He bends down, picks up a circular table, and throws it at the enemy; decent range, almost travels all the way across the screen.
  - Damage: 10 to knowledge/laughter, 15 to power.

- **up B:** Shoots fire from his mouth aimed diagonally into the air; direction depends on facing. A close range move.
  - Damage: 8 to knowledge/laughter, 12 to power.

- **air B — Jump B:** He jumps up and hits an opponent down with both hands (a double axe handle) and moves forward slightly. Rather close range, but the slight forward movement adds a bit more range.
  - Damage: 9 to knowledge/laughter, 13 to power.

- **Y — Regular Y:** He goes into a boxer stance and punches the enemy a few times; the number of hits rises after each Ultimate Action use. A close ranged move.
  - Damage: 16 to knowledge/laughter, 24 to power; after 1 UA 27/40; after 2 UA 36/54; Properties: mash-extend.

- **side Y:** The tip of his finger flips up and he shoots a bullet that travels to the end of the screen. After 1 UA he shoots 3 cannon balls, after 2 UA a big cannon ball.
  - Damage: 14 to knowledge/laughter, 21 to power; after 1 UA 24/36; after 2 UA 33/49; Properties: projectile.

- **down Y:** He stays still for 1 second then attacks with his right hand, a straight punch, staying in place. After 1 UA the punch detaches and flies further; after 2 UA it flies to the end of the screen.
  - Damage: 14 to knowledge/laughter, 21 to power; after 1 UA 21/31; after 2 UA 36/54; Properties: projectile.

- **up Y:** He loads for about half a second, fills his butt with gas, and propels himself into the air diagonally depending on facing; decent range, about twice the range of up B, and UA use increases the range a lot.
  - Damage: 16 to knowledge/laughter, 24 to power; after 1 UA 24/36; after 2 UA 30/45; Properties: charge.

- **air Y — Jump Y:** He jumps and breathes fire at a downward diagonal angle, much like Up B but from the air and facing down, with slightly more range; damage depends on number of hits connected (max 6). UA use raises max damage, not max hit count.
  - Damage: 1 hit 2/3 up to 6 hits 16/24; after 1 UA 1 hit 4/6 up to 5 hits 20/30; after 2 UA 1 hit 6/9 up to 5 hits 32/48; Hits: up to 6 (up to 5 after UA); Properties: multi-hit.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Take less damage from blades.
  - Passive: Take more damage from lightning attacks.
  - Passive: Gain SP when being attacked by opponents.
  - X — Coup de Vent: He loads up air in his arms and shoots it forward with much velocity; decent sized, decent damage, moves him back a bit after launching, and has great knock back. (Damage: 30 to knowledge/laughter, 45 to power)
  - up X — Ultimate Hammer: He cracks his knuckles for .75 seconds and jumps forward diagonally (compared to Ichigo's Up X, but slower), grabs the opponent, and punches them down from the air. (Damage: 52 to knowledge, 35 to power/laughter)

- **5-koma — laughter:**
  - Passive: Take less damage from blades.
  - Passive: Take more damage from lightning attacks.
  - Passive: Gain SP when being attacked by opponents.
  - X — Coup de Vent: Same move as the 4-koma version, just bigger and deals more damage. Great for ring outs, but has a slight startup. (Damage: 40 to knowledge/laughter, 60 to power)
  - up X — Chaser Bullet: Franky dislocates his shoulders to reveal holes and shoots multiple bullets while chasing the enemy; takes a second to start up, but is powerful and good for edge guarding since he can't run off stage while using it. (Damage: each hit 1 to knowledge/laughter (40 hits max = 40 total); each hit 1 or 2 to power (40 hits = 60 total); Hits: 40 (max); Properties: multi-hit)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Take less damage from blades.
  - Passive: Take more damage from lightning attacks.
  - Passive: Gain SP when being attacked by opponents.
  - X — Coup de Vent: Guide says same as the laughter variant.
  - up X — Chaser Bullet: Guide says same as the laughter variant.

#### Combos

- B, -> B, Y, -> Y: 47 to knowledge/laughter, 70 to power. Can't use UA with this because the first Y sends the opponent flying.
- B, Up B, Up Y, B: 41 to knowledge/power (as written), 61 to power. UA won't work because of extra distance.
- After UA, B, -> B, Y: after 1 UA 44/66; after 2 UA 53/79.
- After UA, B, Up B, -> Y: after 1 UA 40/60; after 2 UA 49/73 (best for ring outs after 2 UA).
- After 2 UA, B, ? B, Y + Shishio 2 (used about halfway through), X: 4-koma 125 to knowledge/laughter, 152 to power; 5-koma 135/167.
- After 2 UA, B, Up B, ? Y + Shishio 2, X: 4-koma 121/144; 5-koma 131/161.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The combo 'B, Up B, Up Y, B' is written with damage '41 damage to knowledge/power' in the source, which looks like a typo for 'knowledge/laughter' given the pattern elsewhere; kept as written.
- Guide states the 5-koma power variant is 'the exact same' as the laughter 5-koma except nature, so special descriptions/damage for the power variant were not independently given.
