### Bankai Ichigo Kurosaki — FAQ 47060

**Source:** `47060-bankai-ichigo.txt`; author: Prottain.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A horizontal slash that shifts Ichigo forward a bit.
  - Damage: 9.

- **side B:** A downward slash that covers an arc in front of him, stepping forward, with pretty decent range.
  - Damage: 9.

- **up B:** A diagonal stab that lifts an enemy slightly.
  - Damage: 9; Properties: launcher.

- **down B:** Has a second delay to use. A black shockwave forms and forces the character to change, if they have a second battle character in their deck. This is the down+B forced switch attack.
  - Damage: 9; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** A blur of slashes appears with a black aura around Ichigo; any enemy close by is slashed and sent flying into the air. Damage is based on how many slashes are pulled off, and can be increased by repeatedly pressing Y. The closer the enemy generally the more slashes.
  - Damage: 20; Properties: launcher, mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **side Y:** Ichigo travels as far as his dash and immediately stops and turns around to slash the enemy if they are in his path (not if they are on the floor). Range appears to be a down-to-up slash like the Side B slash. There is a half-second delay after use regardless of whether he finds a target.
  - Damage: 18.

- **up Y:** Ichigo flashsteps up, and like Side Y, slashes an enemy down if one is in the distance traveled. The flashstep up goes higher than his regular jump (A button once). Unlike Side Y, there is no delay regardless of whether he finds an enemy. If a directional button is pressed immediately after and he hit an enemy, he will immediately perform his Down Y air attack. Subtracts one from total jumps.
  - Damage: 18.

- **air Y — Down Y air:** Ichigo flashsteps straight down to the ground then slashes horizontally, hitting both his left and right side for as much horizontal range as his Side B attack. Has a 1 second delay after he slashes.
  - Damage: 18.

- **down Y:** Ichigo stabs his sword into the ground, creating a shockwave that breaks the enemy's guard. Radius as wide as the Down B shockwave. Has about a second delay like most guard breaks.
  - Damage: 18; Properties: guard break.

- **select — Mask removal / SP regen:** Ichigo struggles to pull off his mask, regaining a bit of SP gauge. Has about a second delay to fully finish.

#### Combos

- B, Side B, and Up X. Side slash, downward slash, then connects to the aerial special. 67 max damage to Power/Laughter, 100 max damage to Knowledge
- B, Side B, Y, and Up X. Side slash, downward slash, then fast multiple slashes tossing the enemy into the air allowing a catch with Up X special; does not work if the enemy is thrown behind or above Ichigo after the Y attack. 87 max damage to Power/Laughter, 130 max damage to Knowledge
- Down Y + Combo 1 or 2, guard breaking then leading into Combo 1 or 2. With Combo 1: 85 max damage to Power/Laughter, 127 max damage to Knowledge. With Combo 2: 105 max damage to Power/Laughter, 157 max damage to Knowledge
- Y, X (Y used to maximum hits to launch high enough for the X special; X can be replaced with Up X). 70 max damage to Power/Laughter, 105 max damage to Knowledge (95 max damage to Power/Laughter, 142 max damage to Knowledge if random X mask-pull happens)
- B, Side B, Side Y. Regular sideway then downward slashes, then dash to the other side of the enemy for another slash; not a perfect connection, slight delay between the last Side B attack and the Side Y. 36 max damage to Power/Laughter, 54 max damage to Knowledge
- B, Side B, Y, Up Y, directional button immediately after. Like Combo 2 but finishes with a downward slash toward the ground and a dash to the ground for another slash instead of a special; requires the enemy directly above after the Y attack; can be modified with an air dash away after Up Y but requires a wait. Non-modded damage: 72 max damage to Power/Laughter, 108 max damage to Knowledge

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide gives no HP values for any koma size (4 through 8) and does not name a nature per koma size, only stating damage split by 'Power/Laughter' vs 'Knowledge' for every move, implying nature affects damage rather than being fixed per koma; natures_available is inferred from this pattern, not stated as a list anywhere.
- Koma sizes 4, 5, and 6 (battle) have no distinct moveset or passive text in the guide; only sizes 7 and 8 get an explicit X-special writeup labeled '7 Koma Version' and '8 Koma Version'. It's unclear whether 4-6 have different specials, weaker specials, or none at all -- the guide only says Ichigo must evolve to 7/8 to reach 'Bankai form', implying 4-6 are a different (non-Bankai) moveset not covered by this guide, but this isn't stated outright.
- The 2-koma and 3-koma support forms are only referenced in the evolution-cost table (IK4); the guide gives no support_effect text for them at all.
- The Help Koma entry (size/hp unknown) is described functionally (extra jump) but the guide never gives it a koma size number.
- IK7 describes generic Support Koma advice (Big Three: 3 Koma Trunks, 3 Koma Seiya, 3 Koma Sena; also Yoruichi) but these are other characters' support koma, not Bankai Ichigo's own support koma -- included here only as deck-building context, not as Ichigo moveset data.
- Directional/system moves (dash/flashstep, block, fast-fall, jump/double jump, air-dash-jump) are documented in the guide under 'Directional Buttons + A + Select' but are movement mechanics, not attacks, so they were not added to the moves array; flagging here in case they're wanted elsewhere.
- No 'version' string is given for this guide (unlike some GameFAQs guides); source.version left null.
- The X special's 'Random' mask variant is described as a variant that occurs only in the 7 Koma version and is explicitly said not to exist in the 8 Koma version -- captured as a separate special entry under koma size 7 rather than as a property of the base X move.

### Ichigo Kurosaki — FAQ 46858

**Source:** `46858-ichigo-kurosaki.txt`; author: Lunar Twilight.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** A simple, horizontal sword slash. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 10 damage, 15 damage.

- **side B:** Ichigo steps and does a vertical slash. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 10 damage, 15 damage.

- **up B:** Ichigo slashes in a wide sweep, hitting around him. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 9 damage, 13 damage.

- **down B:** Ichigo yells, and makes an energy wave around him. Forces the enemy to change their Battle Koma if they get hit. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 10 damage, 15 damage; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Ichigo spins his sword over his head, which can hit more if Y rapidly pressed. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 6-14, 9-21; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **side Y:** Ichigo slashes and shoots off a small Getsuga Tenshou, which can travel for a small distance. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 15 damage, 22 damage; Properties: projectile.

- **up Y:** Ichigo slashes and shoots off a small Getsuga Tenshou, hitting to his upper diagonal. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 15 damage, 22 damage; Properties: projectile.

- **down Y:** Ichigo makes a small shockwave by thrusting his sword into the ground. Breaks guards. (4-6 Koma, non-Bankai)
  - Damage: 18 damage, 27 damage; Properties: guard break.

- **B:** Still a standard horizontal slash. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 9 damage, 13 damage.

- **side B:** Ichigo steps foward and slashes. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 9 damage, 13 damage.

- **up B:** Ichigo stabs above him, hitting them slightly into the air. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 9 damage, 13 damage; Properties: launcher, pop.

- **down B:** Ichigo yells, making a shockwave that causes a forced change. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 9 damage, 13 damage; Properties: forced switch.

- **Y:** Ichigo slashes all around him, which can hit many times if you keep pressing Y. Sends anyone affected flying. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 11-20, 16-30; Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit, launcher.

- **side Y:** Ichigo flashsteps foward, and if he finds an enemy he turns and slashes it once. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 18 damage, 27 damage.

- **up Y:** Ichigo flashsteps up, and if he finds an enemy he'll slash them down to the stage. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Properties: knockdown.

- **down Y:** Ichigo stabs into the stage, releasing energy that breaks guards. (Bankai, 7-8 Koma)
  - Damage: 18, 27 damage; Properties: guard break.

- **select — Ultimate Action (shout):** A shout that increases the potency of Ichigo's Y-Attacks. Used by his 4-6 Koma.
  - Properties: status:attack boost.

- **select — Ultimate Action (Hollow mask):** Ichigo rips off his Hollow mask, gaining energy but losing some of his health. Used by his 7 and 8 Battle Koma.
  - Properties: status:health loss.

#### Combos

- B, ^B, ^X(7-Koma) - Ichigo slashes twice, followed by his aerial attack. These link easily, and it can be done with >B rather than ^B.
- vB, ^X(7-Koma) - Ichigo breaks their guard, which knockes them up into the air in front of you. He then chains them into his aerial attack. This does just as much damage as the chain above, only it takes longer due to the guard break.
- Y, ^X(7-Koma) - Ichigo slashes them in a frenzy, hitting for just a bit more damage than the above combos. Don't fully use the Y, only a little will do.
- B, >B, Y, ^Y, Aerial Y - Ichigo slashes them up into the air, which is followed by a flashstepped slash, linking into a flashstep to the ground, where he'll slash them onto their back. Hits for a good amount of damage.
- B, >B, Y(only one tap), >Y - Ichigo slashes them fowards and back again.
