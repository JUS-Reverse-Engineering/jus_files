### Seiya — FAQ 46807

**Source:** `46807-seiya.txt`; author: d3m0nsl4y3r; version 1.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Seiya does a simple forward punch. Fairly fast and good for starting combos.

- **side B:** Seiya steps forward while punching with energy surrounding his fist. Pretty decent, rather like Seiya's regular B attack.

- **up B:** Seiya performs an uppercut. Good for launching the enemy a short distance into the air.
  - Properties: launcher.

- **down B:** Seiya makes a burst of energy around him. Changes the opponent's Battle Character if it hits.
  - Properties: forced switch.

- **air B:** Seiya chops downwards. Decent spike attack.

- **Y:** Seiya steps forward while releasing a burst of energy bullets from his fist. Tapping Y quickly yields more hits, up to 10 maximum. Good for edge-guarding and good for finishing combos.
  - Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **side Y:** Seiya steps forward while firing a large energy bullet out of his fist which travels a short distance.
  - Properties: projectile.

- **up Y:** Seiya jumps while releasing a burst of energy bullets from his fist. Tapping Y quickly yields more hits, up to 13 maximum. Good for hitting opponents above you.
  - Properties: mash-extend, multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Seiya punches the ground around him. Breaks enemy's guards.
  - Properties: guard break.

- **air Y:** Seiya kicks downwards at a 45 degree angle. Deals decent damage and is fairly fast.

- **select:** Seiya does a pose while glowing blue. This recharges SP a little at a time, and should only be used if the SP bar is almost full.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Help Koma Boost: Shiryu, Shun, Hyoga
  - Passive: J-Soul: 136
  - X — Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken: Fires a barrage of energy bullets. Fires a second barrage if tapping X repeatedly. (Properties: projectile, mash-extend)
  - X — Pegasus Sui Sei Ken: Fires a medium-sized energy bullet in the air. (Properties: projectile)

- **5-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Help Koma Boost: Shiryu, Shun, Hyoga
  - Passive: J-Soul: 152
  - X — Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken: Fires a barrage of energy bullets. Fires a large bullet after initial barrage if tapping X repeatedly. (Properties: projectile, mash-extend)
  - X — Pegasus Sui Sei Ken: Fires three medium-sized energy bullets into the air. (Properties: projectile, multi-hit)

- **6-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Help Koma Boost: Shiryu, Shun, Hyoga
  - Passive: J-Soul: 168
  - X — Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken 'BIG BANG': Fires a short barrage of energy bullets. If the initial barrage hits the opponent, tapping X repeatedly triggers an explosion after the barrage. (Properties: projectile, mash-extend)
  - X — Pegasus Rolling Crush: Seiya stands in one place and grabs the opponent, taking them up then smashing them into the ground.

- **7-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Help Koma Boost: Shiryu, Shun, Hyoga
  - Passive: J-Soul: 168
  - X — Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken 'BIG BANG': Almost the same as the 6 koma Special 1 but the opponent does not need to be hit to activate the explosion. Tapping X repeatedly yields more hits. (Properties: projectile, mash-extend)
  - X — Pegasus Rolling Crush: Almost identical to the 6 koma Special 2, but Seiya dashes forward a short distance when activated. This dash also blocks any frontal attacks. (Properties: counter)

- **8-koma — power:**
  - Passive: Help Koma Boost: Athena, Mu, Ryotsu(Kochi Kame)
  - Passive: J-Soul: 200
  - Passive: Also called Gold Saint Seiya
  - X — Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken 'BIG BANG': Different from the 6 and 7 koma versions in that there is no explosion at the end. Instead, Seiya releases a more straight-forward barrage of energy bullets after the initial barrage. (Properties: projectile)
  - X — Saggitarius Arrow: Seiya pulls out a bow and fires a straight beam of golden energy into the air. (Properties: projectile)

#### Combos

- 7 koma Seiya's special + Shishio 2 koma sends your opponent flying because of the explosion at the end of Seiya's special and leaves them with a burn effect because of Shishio. This combo can be done with all of Seiya's koma, but the damage output decreases as the koma size decreases.

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- The guide's evolution chart shows a separate 2/3-koma support branch ([S2]-[S3]) alongside the 4-8 battle-koma branch, both stemming from the free help koma [H]. The 8-koma form is explicitly labeled 'Gold Saint Seiya' in this guide's section heading, distinct from base bronze-cloth Seiya (4-7 koma) -- this may correspond to the separate 'Gold Seiya' character at chr_b 64 noted in docs/research/chr_b-Complete-Mapping.md, or it may just be this guide's label for Seiya's own 8-koma gold-cloth form. Top-level chr_b is set to 63 (base Seiya) per instructions; this 8-koma entry is included under that character's koma list as written by the guide, but the chr_b 63 vs 64 boundary for this specific koma is unresolved.
- 'J-Soul' values (136/152/168/168/200) are given per koma size but are not part of the schema (not hp as commonly understood, no explanit); recorded verbatim in each koma's passives array as raw text rather than interpreted as hp.
- 'Help Koma Boost' lists (Shiryu, Shun, Hyoga for koma 4-7; Athena, Mu, Ryotsu(Kochi Kame) for koma 8) describe which OTHER characters' help koma synergize with Seiya, not a self-passive in the usual sense; recorded verbatim in passives for lack of a better field.
- Gem/evolution costs (Support 2/3, Battle 4-8, Athena unlock) are omitted per scope (economy/evolution costs excluded, battle-mechanics-only).
- The guide never gives numeric damage or hit counts for any move or special -- all damage/hits fields are null throughout, per the guide simply not stating them (no numbers invented).
