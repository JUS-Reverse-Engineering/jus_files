### Kakashi — FAQ 46618

**Source:** `46618-kakashi.txt`; author: MasterTurtle (aka Hyperslasher); version 2.0.

#### Basic attacks and actions

- **B:** Kakashi quickly slaps in front of him. Nice hitbox that hits both behind and in front, and it's fast.
  - Damage: 8.

- **side B:** Kakashi dashes forward and slashes upwards with a kunai, then does an upwards kick. Unlike most side B attacks, this knocks enemies into the air, letting you combo into aerial attacks but not ground-based ones.
  - Damage: 2 + 6; Hits: 2; Properties: launcher.

- **up B:** Kakashi creates a clone of himself that kicks upwards. Not as useful as side B since it doesn't cover as much ground, but it doesn't knock the target up as high, leaving them open to some lower-hitting supports.
  - Damage: 8; Properties: launcher.

- **down B:** Kakashi charges for a brief time, then fires Pakkun, a small dog, at his enemies. Forces enemies to switch battle characters and has decent range.
  - Damage: 8; Properties: forced switch, charge.

- **air B:** Kakashi does a downward slash while in the air. Its main selling point is the ability to knock enemies downwards and through thin floors.
  - Damage: 8; Properties: knockdown.

- **Y — The Kakashi Triple Kick:** Kakashi creates 2 clones of himself that kick forward in some strange sort of gymnastics pyramid. Good range and great damage for a regular attack, but limited use in combos.
  - Damage: 24 damage at close range, less if the target is further away; Hits: 3; Properties: multi-hit.

- **side Y:** Kakashi throws up to 6 shurikens in a line. Not a great attack, mainly because of the lag when Kakashi starts and finishes the attack, and the range isn't as good as you'd hope.
  - Damage: each shuriken does 3, for a total of 18 if they all hit; Properties: projectile, multi-hit.

- **up Y:** Kakashi jumps into the air and does a punch, a slash, and a kick. This attack will not hit the opponent unless they're above you. One of Kakashi's most-used attacks; can be comboed out of with a little finesse.
  - Damage: the first two hits do 5 damage each, the third hit does 8; Hits: 3; Properties: multi-hit.

- **down Y:** Kakashi charges for about half a second, then a huge dog jumps at his opponents. Breaks guard, has a huge hitbox, charges quickly, and doesn't leave you open for long, though Kakashi has a lot of difficulty comboing out of it without supports.
  - Damage: 16; Properties: guard break, charge.

- **air Y:** Kakashi throws a Kunai at a downward angle. Average Y aerial, okay for harassing opponents; also usable after up Y if not comboing out of it with a support.
  - Damage: 12; Properties: projectile.

- **select:** Kakashi creates a tree stump that can be used to dodge an attack. If Kakashi is attacked during the next 4 seconds, the stump replaces him and the attack deals no damage. You can also move a short distance after Kakashi disappears.

#### Battle koma specials (4+ panels)

- **4-koma — knowledge:** HP 136.
  - X: Kakashi uses a beam attack, similar to that of any DBZ character. (Damage: up to 30)
  - X: Kakashi summons some of his ninja dogs, and they converge on a nearby enemy. (Damage: 24)

- **5-koma — knowledge:** HP 152.
  - X — Raikiri: Kakashi charges up chakra in his hand, then dashes forward and hits an enemy with it. The damage is always 36; how long you charge just changes the distance Kakashi dashes. (Damage: 36; Properties: charge)
  - X: Kakashi summons his ninja dogs, just like his previous secondary special. (Damage: 30)

- **6-koma — knowledge:** HP 168.
  - X — Chidori: Chidori, again. This one deals 44 damage total. (Damage: 44)
  - X — Mangekyou Sharingan: It creates a red eye that hovers in the air, damaging all enemies that come in contact with it. Useful for edgeguarding since you can control the path of the eye and it goes through walls. (Damage: 48 total; Properties: projectile)

- **6-koma — laughter:** HP 168.
  - X — Chidori: Chidori, a third time. Though Kakashi is Laughter type in this Koma, Chidori still does knowledge type damage.
  - X — Mangekyou Sharingan: Still Mangekyou Sharingan, and still Knowledge type. (Properties: projectile)

#### Guide notes and unresolved details

- Neutral B and down B are mentioned in HK6 as attacking options after a fast-fall, alongside side B and down Y, but no separate damage/description beyond the HK2 entries is given there.
- Y (non-directional) is stated in a combo note to deal about 4 damage and keep the enemy in the combo when inserted between up B and up Y — this conflicts with the HK2 damage value of 24 (close range) for standalone Y; guide does not reconcile this, likely a different hit/positioning scenario.
- 5-koma and 6-koma (both variants) sections do not restate B/Y basic attacks or passives beyond the specials and J-Soul value; guide implies basics are shared across all koma sizes per HK2, but this is not explicitly restated per-koma.
- Support/help koma 'size' (block count) for the help koma is not stated in the guide, only its shape '[]' which is ambiguous for exact block count; left null.
- chr_b_note in schema example says 'null if character is not in the table' — Kakashi IS in the table at 23, so chr_b_note here documents the source rather than being null.
- Guide states in HK1 Kakashi has 'probably the best guard break in the game' (down Y) but HK2/HK6 both note it's hard to combo out of without supports — not a contradiction, just a caveat repeated in both sections.
- Passives 'Can Wall-Jump', 'Can see invisible opponents', 'Take less damage from Special Attacks' are listed in HK5 as innate Kakashi abilities (not tied to a specific koma size) — recorded here as general passives but not assigned to a specific koma entry since the guide doesn't specify which koma size(s) they apply to.
- J-Soul is the guide's term for HP; used directly as 'hp' for battle koma (136/152/168/168).
